Privacy

We help you get into medical school without selling your data. We never sell or share your data, and 520 Quest has no ads and no trackers.

You can play 520 Quest without an account; then your progress, scores and settings are stored only in your browser on your device, and clearing your browser data erases them (use the Backup button in the Me tab if you want a copy).

If you sign in, we store your email address, your saved progress, your plan, a list of the devices you use (so a plan works on up to 3), your duel name, and your duel results. This data is kept on Supabase, our database provider, and is used only to run the app. We don't sell or share it, and there are no ads or trackers. To delete your account and its data, email info@unprocrastinatehub.com.

Purchases are processed by Gumroad, which handles your payment details and email under its own privacy policy. Fonts load from Google Fonts.
