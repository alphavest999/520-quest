Questions

Before you subscribe

When do the plans open?

Soon. The buttons on this page turn on when they do. Until then, play the free version.

Do plans renew?

Yes. Each plan renews automatically at the end of its period, and you can cancel anytime. You keep access until the period you paid for ends.

Is anything held back on Monthly?

No. Monthly, 6 Months and Yearly unlock exactly the same questions, sheets, mnemonics and features.

Does it work on more than one device?

Yes, up to 3. Sign in with your email on each one. You can remove an old device in Me, then Manage devices.

Is the score estimate an official prediction?

No. It's a rough estimate from your answers in the app. Official AAMC practice exams are the best predictor of your real score.

Are the questions from the AAMC?

No. All questions, passages, sheets and mnemonics are original and written for 520 Quest.
