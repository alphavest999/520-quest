Common questions

I subscribed. How do I unlock everything?

Open the app, go to the Me tab and tap Sign in with email, using the same email you paid with. Your plan turns on, and it works on up to 3 devices.

What do the plans include?

Monthly ($14.99), 6 Months ($44.99) and Yearly ($79.99) all unlock all 5,180 questions, all 33 Mastery Sheets, all 150 mnemonics, unlimited boss battles and duels. Free includes 100 questions, 5 Mastery Sheets and 1 mnemonic.

The app says I'm on Free

Make sure you are signed in (Me tab) with the email you paid with. If you cleared your browser data or switched browsers, sign in again.

How do I move my progress to a new device?

Sign in with your email in the Me tab on both devices; your progress follows your account. Free accounts sync to 1 device and paid plans to 3. To swap one out, open Me, then Manage devices, and remove an old one. You can also use Backup or move device in the Me tab to copy a backup code without signing in.

How do I install it on my phone?

On iPhone, open 520quest.com/play in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app. After that it works offline.

How do levels work?

Every 200 questions, your accuracy is checked with harder questions counting a little more. If you've reached the next band, a Gatekeeper boss unlocks. Beat it to level up. Levels never go down.

Can I change my daily goal?

Yes. Pick 15, 25, 40 or 60 questions in the Me tab or from the welcome screen each day.

How do I turn off the music?

Tap the ♪ button at the top of the app. The level-up fanfare has its own switch in the Me tab.

Do you sell my data?

No. We help you get into medical school without selling your data. Your progress stays on your device unless you sign in, and then it is used only to sync your account. See Privacy and terms.
