Privacy Policy

Version 2026-11-07 · Effective November 7, 2026

Short version: we collect only what we need to run 520 Quest. We never sell or share your personal information, and there are no ads and no advertising trackers.

This policy explains how Unprocrastinate LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company ("we", "us"), which operates 520 Quest at 520quest.com (the "Service"), handles your information. It is part of our Terms of Use.

1. What we collect

If you play without an account, your progress, scores, notes and settings stay in your browser's storage on your device. We don't receive them, and clearing your browser data erases them.

If you sign in, we store:

  • your email address (used to send your 6-digit sign-in code) and, if you add one, a display name;
  • your saved progress (answers, scores, levels, review queue, settings) so it syncs between your devices;
  • your plan and when it started;
  • a list of your devices (a random device id, a short label such as "iPhone" or "Mac", and when it was last used) so a plan works on up to 3 devices;
  • the version and time you accepted our Terms of Use and this policy;
  • mnemonic votes you cast.

Anonymous answer statistics. To show the real percent of students who get each question right, the app sends which answer you picked for a question, whether it was right, and a random device id made in your browser. No name or email is attached.

Things you choose to send: question reports and notes, and, if you report it, your official MCAT score, the month you tested, and the app's estimate at that time (stored with a random device id, not your email), and any email you send to support.

Payments. If you buy a plan, Lemon Squeezy (our merchant of record) collects your payment details. We receive your email, the plan, order and subscription ids and status, but never your full card number.

Technical data. Like any website, the services that host and deliver 520 Quest receive your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, which they may keep in short-term logs.

2. How we use it

  • to run the Service: sign you in, save and sync progress, apply your plan, and unlock paid content;
  • to improve questions and score estimates, using anonymous or combined statistics;
  • to answer support requests and send messages about your account, plan, billing or changes to our terms;
  • to keep the Service secure, stop misuse and account sharing, and meet legal obligations.

We do not use your information for advertising, we do not sell it or "share" it for cross-context behavioral advertising, and we do not use it to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

3. Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run 520 Quest, under their own privacy and security terms:

  • Supabase (database, sign-in and email codes, hosted in the United States);
  • Lemon Squeezy (checkout, payments, taxes and receipts);
  • GitHub Pages and Cloudflare (hosting and domain services);
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr (deliver fonts and a code library to your browser, which means they receive your IP address).

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a sale or transfer of our business (in which case this policy keeps applying to your information).

4. Cookies and local storage

We don't use advertising or analytics cookies. The app uses your browser's local storage and a service worker to save your progress, keep you signed in and work offline. You can clear them in your browser settings.

5. How long we keep it

We keep account information while your account exists. When you ask us to delete your account, we delete your profile, progress and device list within 30 days, except records we must keep for legal, tax or fraud-prevention reasons (Lemon Squeezy keeps its own payment records). Anonymous statistics that can't identify you may be kept.

6. Your choices and rights

You can ask us to access, correct, download or delete your personal information by emailing support@520-quest.com from the email on your account. Depending on where you live (for example California, other US states, the UK or the EU), you may have further rights, such as to object to or restrict certain uses, or to complain to a data protection authority. We will not treat you differently for using these rights. We will reply within the time the law requires, usually 30 to 45 days, and may need to confirm it's you first.

7. Children

520 Quest is not for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us information, email us and we will delete it. Users from 13 to 17 need a parent or guardian's permission.

8. Security

We use encrypted connections (HTTPS), email-code sign-in without passwords, and database rules so each account can read only its own data. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you as the law requires.

9. Where your data is processed

We are based in the United States and our providers process data in the United States and other countries. By using the Service from outside the US, you understand your information will be processed in the US, where laws may differ from those where you live.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will show it in the app and ask you to accept it again, and may email account holders. The version and date are at the top of this page.

11. Contact

Privacy questions or requests: support@520-quest.com.
