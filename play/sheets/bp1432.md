# BP1432

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antioxidant compound, Antiox-7, on oxidative stress markers in adult mice. In a randomized controlled trial, 120 mice were divided into three groups: a control group receiving a standard diet (n=40), a low-dose group receiving 10 mg/kg Antiox-7 (n=40), and a high-dose group receiving 30 mg/kg Antiox-7 (n=40). After 12 weeks, plasma malondialdehyde (MDA) levels were measured as an index of lipid peroxidation. The control group had a mean MDA of 12.5 µmol/L (SD 1.2). The low-dose group had a mean of 9.8 µmol/L (SD 1.0), and the high-dose group had a mean of 7.6 µmol/L (SD 0.9). The investigators reported a dose-dependent reduction in MDA, with a statistically significant difference between the control and high-dose groups (p<0.001). The study suggests Antiox-7 may mitigate oxidative damage in vivo.

## bp1432-001 Which of the following best describes the pattern of MDA levels observed in the study?
Correct: C
The data show a progressive decline in mean MDA from control (12.5) to low-dose (9.8) to high-dose (7.6), indicating a dose-dependent reduction.

## bp1432-002 If you were to design a follow-up experiment to test whether Antiox-7’s effect is mediated by upregulation of endogenous antioxidant enzymes, which of the following would be the most appropriate experimental design?
Correct: B
Measuring both MDA and an endogenous antioxidant enzyme (e.g., SOD) in the same design allows assessment of whether enzyme upregulation correlates with reduced oxidative stress.

## bp1432-003 Calculate the absolute risk reduction (ARR) in MDA levels between the control and high-dose groups.
Correct: C
ARR = Mean MDA_control – Mean MDA_high = 12.5 – 7.6 = 4.9 µmol/L.

## bp1432-004 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the study’s results?
Correct: B
A significant p-value indicates statistical difference, not necessarily causation; other factors could contribute to the observed reduction.

## bp1432-005 Which of the following concepts is most closely linked to the study’s finding that Antiox-7 reduces oxidative stress?
Correct: B
Oxidative stress is a component of redox homeostasis; reducing MDA levels indicates improved redox balance.
