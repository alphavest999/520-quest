# BP1134

Researchers studied the effect of a novel cognitive enhancer on reaction time in healthy adults. A double‑blind, placebo‑controlled design was used with 120 participants randomly assigned to one of four groups: placebo (Control), 5 mg (Low Dose), 10 mg (Medium Dose), and 20 mg (High Dose). After a 30‑minute absorption period, participants completed a computerized simple reaction time task. Mean reaction times (RT) and standard deviations (SD) were recorded for each group. The study aimed to determine whether dose‑dependent improvements in RT could be observed and to assess the safety profile of the drug at different dosages. The results were summarized in Table 1 below, showing a clear trend of decreasing RT with increasing dose, while SD values remained relatively stable across groups.

## bp1134-005 Which of the following best links the study’s findings to a potential clinical application?
