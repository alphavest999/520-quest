# BP1512

Researchers at the University of Midvale conducted a controlled laboratory study to investigate the impact of acute sleep deprivation on memory consolidation in adult male mice. The experiment involved three groups: a control group that was allowed 12 hours of uninterrupted sleep, a group subjected to 4 hours of sleep deprivation, and a group subjected to 8 hours of sleep deprivation. After the sleep manipulation, all mice were tested on a novel object recognition task, a widely used behavioral assay for assessing recognition memory. The mean recognition index (percentage of time spent exploring the novel object) for each group was recorded along with the standard deviation. The researchers hypothesized that increasing duration of sleep deprivation would lead to a progressive decline in memory performance, thereby providing evidence for the role of sleep in memory consolidation processes.

| Sleep Condition | Mean Recognition Index (%) | Standard Deviation |
|-----------------|----------------------------|--------------------|
| Control (12h)   | 85.2                       | 4.3                |
| 4h Deprivation  | 72.5                       | 5.1                |
| 8h Deprivation  | 60.1                       | 6.0                |

## bp1512-01 Based on the data presented in the table, which of the following best describes the effect of sleep deprivation on memory performance in the mice?
Correct: C
The recognition index decreases from 85.2% in the control group to 72.5% after 4 hours of deprivation and further to 60.1% after 8 hours, indicating a progressive decline in memory performance with increased sleep deprivation.

## bp1512-02 If the researchers wanted to test whether caffeine administration could mitigate the negative effects of sleep deprivation on memory, which experimental design would be most appropriate?
Correct: A
A randomized controlled trial allows for direct comparison of caffeine versus placebo within each deprivation condition, controlling for confounding variables and establishing causality.

Correct: C
The difference is 85.2% – 60.1% = 25.1%, which matches option C.
