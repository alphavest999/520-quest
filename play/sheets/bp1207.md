# BP1207

Researchers conducted a randomized controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypotensol, in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A total of 200 participants were randomly assigned to receive either Hypotensol (n = 100) or a placebo (n = 100) for 12 weeks. Systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured at baseline and at weeks 4, 8, and 12. The study aimed to determine whether Hypotensol could produce a clinically meaningful reduction in SBP compared to placebo. The investigators also recorded adverse events and adherence rates. The primary endpoint was the mean change in SBP from baseline to week 12. Secondary endpoints included the proportion of patients achieving target SBP < 140 mmHg and the incidence of serious adverse events. The trial was double‑blinded, and all participants provided informed consent. The data presented in Table 1 summarize the mean SBP and standard deviation for each group at each time point. These results will inform clinicians about the potential benefits and safety profile of Hypotensol in a real‑world setting.

| Week | Hypotensol Mean SBP (mmHg) | SD | Placebo Mean SBP (mmHg) | SD |
|------|---------------------------|----|--------------------------|----|
| 0    | 148.2 | 8.5 | 147.9 | 8.7 |
| 4    | 140.5 | 7.9 | 145.3 | 8.2 |
| 8    | 135.7 | 7.4 | 143.8 | 8.0 |
| 12   | 130.1 | 6.9 | 141.2 | 7.8 |

## bp1207_q1 What does the table primarily illustrate regarding the treatment groups over time?
Correct: B
The table shows a progressive decline in mean SBP for the Hypotensol group, reaching 130.1 mmHg at week 12, whereas the placebo group remains above 140 mmHg. This indicates a greater reduction in SBP with Hypotensol.

## bp1207_q2 Which of the following study designs would best allow the researchers to attribute changes in SBP to Hypotensol?
Correct: B
Randomization and blinding control for confounding variables and bias, providing the strongest evidence that observed differences are due to the intervention.

## bp1207_q3 Calculate the percent reduction in mean SBP from baseline to week 12 for the Hypotensol group.
Correct: C
Percent reduction = [(148.2 – 130.1) / 148.2] × 100 = 12.5%.

## bp1207_q4 Which statement is a potential trap when interpreting the study results?
Correct: B
Statistical significance does not automatically equate to clinical relevance; the magnitude of change and patient outcomes must also be considered.

## bp1207_q5 Which concept is most directly linked to the primary endpoint of this study?
Correct: C
The primary endpoint is the mean change in SBP; the proportion of patients achieving target SBP allows calculation of NNT, which is directly related to the clinical impact of the treatment.
