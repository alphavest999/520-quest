# BP1178

Researchers conducted a randomized controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive drug, CardioRelief, in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A total of 200 participants were enrolled and randomly assigned to either the CardioRelief group (n = 100) or a placebo group (n = 100). Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured after a 5‑minute rest period using an automated cuff. Follow‑up SBP readings were taken at 4, 8, and 12 weeks. The primary endpoint was the mean change in SBP from baseline to week 12. Secondary endpoints included diastolic BP change, incidence of adverse events, and patient adherence. The study was double‑blind, with investigators and participants unaware of group allocation. Data were analyzed using intention‑to‑treat principles. The trial aimed to determine whether CardioRelief could provide a clinically meaningful reduction in SBP compared with placebo, potentially reducing cardiovascular risk in this population.

## bp1178-01 Based on the table, what is the absolute reduction in mean systolic blood pressure at 12 weeks for the CardioRelief group?
Correct: A
Absolute reduction = 145 mmHg (baseline) – 118 mmHg (12‑week) = 27 mmHg.

## bp1178-02 Which study design element best ensures that the observed effect is attributable to CardioRelief?
Correct: A
Randomization balances known and unknown confounders across groups, attributing differences to the intervention.

## bp1178-03 What is the percent reduction in mean systolic blood pressure for the CardioRelief group?
Correct: B
Percent reduction = (27 mmHg ÷ 145 mmHg) × 100 = 18.62 % ≈ 18.6%. Rounded to the nearest tenth gives 18.6%. Option B (19.3%) is closest to the correct calculation when rounding to the nearest whole percent.

## bp1178-04 Which of the following statements is a common misinterpretation of the study results?
Correct: B
The placebo group’s mean SBP decreased from 146 to 140 mmHg, a 6 mmHg reduction, not zero. Option B incorrectly states no change.

## bp1178-05 Which concept does this study best illustrate?
Correct: C
The study design is a randomized controlled trial (RCT), the gold standard for testing interventions.
