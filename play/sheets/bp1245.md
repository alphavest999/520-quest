# BP1245

Researchers investigated the impact of a novel antihypertensive agent, CardioX, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants (age 40–65, BMI 25–35). Participants were randomized 1:1 to receive either CardioX (20 mg daily) or placebo for 12 weeks. Baseline SBP averaged 140 mmHg (SD 8). After 12 weeks, mean SBP decreased to 128 mmHg (SD 7) in the CardioX group and 136 mmHg (SD 9) in the placebo group. The between‑group difference was 8 mmHg (95% CI 5–11, p < 0.001). Secondary outcomes included diastolic BP, heart rate, and adverse events. CardioX was well tolerated, with mild dizziness reported in 4% of participants. The authors concluded that CardioX significantly lowers SBP compared to placebo and may be a viable option for patients with mild hypertension.

## bp1245-01 Based on the data presented, which statement best describes the efficacy of CardioX?
Correct: A
The mean SBP reduction in the CardioX group was 12 mmHg, whereas the placebo group had a 4 mmHg reduction. The between‑group difference of 8 mmHg (95% CI 5–11, p < 0.001) indicates a statistically significant and clinically meaningful benefit.

## bp1245-02 Which of the following best describes the study design used in this investigation?
Correct: C
The study randomized participants to CardioX or placebo, blinded both participants and investigators, and used a placebo control, which defines a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial.

## bp1245-03 What is the estimated 95% confidence interval for the mean SBP reduction in the CardioX group?
Correct: A
The reported mean change is −12 mmHg with a standard deviation of 7. Using the standard error (SE = SD/√N = 7/√60 ≈ 0.90) and a 95% CI of ±1.96×SE gives −12 ± 1.76 ≈ (−13.8, −10.2). Rounded to whole numbers, the CI is approximately −14 to −10 mmHg.

## bp1245-04 Which of the following statements is a common misinterpretation of the study’s findings?
Correct: C
The study did not compare CardioX to lifestyle changes; thus, claiming it can replace them is a misinterpretation. The data only demonstrate efficacy relative to placebo.

## bp1245-05 Which of the following resources would best help a clinician apply these findings to practice?
Correct: A
The AHA guideline incorporates evidence from randomized trials like this one and provides dosing, monitoring, and safety recommendations relevant to clinical practice.
