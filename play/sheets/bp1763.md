# BP1763

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel antihypertensive compound, Hypertenso, on systolic blood pressure in adults with stage 1 hypertension. In a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial, 200 participants were assigned to one of four groups: placebo (n=50), low dose (25 mg, n=50), medium dose (50 mg, n=50), or high dose (75 mg, n=50). After 12 weeks of daily dosing, systolic blood pressure was measured in each participant. The results are summarized in Table 1. The placebo group had a mean systolic BP of 150 mmHg (SD 10). The low-dose group showed a mean of 145 mmHg (SD 9), the medium-dose group 140 mmHg (SD 8), and the high-dose group 135 mmHg (SD 7). The data suggest a dose-dependent reduction in systolic BP. However, the study did not assess diastolic pressure or long-term outcomes. These findings provide preliminary evidence that Hypertenso may lower systolic blood pressure in a dose-responsive manner, but further research is needed to confirm efficacy and safety.
