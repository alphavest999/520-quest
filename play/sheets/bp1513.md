# BP1513

Researchers investigated the effect of light intensity on the photosynthetic rate of *Arabidopsis thaliana* leaves under controlled laboratory conditions. Four light intensities (200, 400, 800, and 1600 lux) were applied while maintaining a constant temperature of 25 °C and a 12‑hour photoperiod. Photosynthetic rates were measured using a portable infrared gas analyzer, expressed in micromoles of CO₂ per square meter per second (µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹). The study aimed to determine the light saturation point and to assess how increasing illumination influences carbon assimilation. The data suggested a nonlinear relationship, with a marked increase in photosynthetic rate up to 800 lux, followed by a plateau at higher intensities. The authors discussed potential photoinhibition and the role of chlorophyll content, proposing that beyond a certain threshold, additional light does not translate into higher CO₂ uptake. These findings have implications for optimizing greenhouse lighting and understanding plant responses to fluctuating light environments.

Light Intensity (lux) | Temperature (°C) | Photosynthetic Rate (µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹)
200 | 25 | 12.5
400 | 25 | 18.3
800 | 25 | 24.1
1600 | 25 | 27.8

## bp1513_q1 Which light intensity yielded the highest photosynthetic rate according to the data presented?
Correct: D
The table shows the photosynthetic rate increases from 12.5 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹ at 200 lux to 27.8 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹ at 1600 lux, making 1600 lux the highest intensity tested.

## bp1513_q2 Which variable is the independent variable in this experiment?
Correct: B
The independent variable is the one that the researchers deliberately changed to observe its effect; here, they varied light intensity while keeping temperature constant.

## bp1513_q3 What is the percent increase in photosynthetic rate from 200 lux to 1600 lux?
Correct: A
Percent increase = [(27.8 – 12.5) / 12.5] × 100 % = 15.3 / 12.5 × 100 % ≈ 122.4 %.

## bp1513_q4 Which statement is NOT supported by the data?
Correct: B
The data show a nonlinear increase that plateaus after 800 lux; the rate does not rise linearly to 1600 lux.

## bp1513_q5 Which of the following would be a logical next step to further investigate the observed plateau in photosynthetic rate?
Correct: B
Chlorophyll fluorescence can indicate photoinhibition and energy dissipation, helping explain why photosynthetic rate plateaus at high light intensities.
