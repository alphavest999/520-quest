# BP1683

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antioxidant compound, Antiox-1, on oxidative stress markers in cultured human endothelial cells. Twenty-four cell cultures were divided into four groups: a control group receiving vehicle, a low-dose group (10 µM Antiox-1), a medium-dose group (50 µM Antiox-1), and a high-dose group (100 µM Antiox-1). After 48 hours of treatment, levels of reactive oxygen species (ROS) and malondialdehyde (MDA) were quantified using fluorescence assays. The data are summarized in Table 1 below. The study aimed to determine whether Antiox-1 reduces oxidative damage in a dose-dependent manner and to identify the optimal concentration for therapeutic use.

## 1 Based on Table 1, which of the following statements accurately reflects the relationship between Antiox-1 concentration and oxidative stress markers?
Correct: B
The table shows a clear dose-dependent decline in both ROS fluorescence and MDA concentration as Antiox-1 concentration rises from 0 to 100 µM, indicating reduced oxidative stress.

## 2 If the researchers wanted to test whether the observed reduction in ROS was statistically significant between the medium- and high-dose groups, which experimental design element would be most appropriate?
Correct: B
A paired t-test is suitable for comparing two related groups when the same experimental units are measured under both conditions; here, the medium- and high-dose groups are independent but the paired test is appropriate if the same batch of cells is used. Increasing sample size (A) improves power but does not directly address the specific comparison. Time-course (C) and positive control (D) are useful but not directly related to the significance test between the two doses.

## 3 Assuming the standard deviation for ROS in the high-dose group is 35 RFU, calculate the 95% confidence interval for the mean ROS fluorescence in that group.
Correct: C
Mean = 650 RFU, SD = 35, n = 6 (since 24 cultures ÷ 4 groups). Standard error = 35/√6 ≈ 14.3. 95% CI = 650 ± 1.96×14.3 ≈ 650 ± 28 → 622–678. The closest option is 635–665 RFU (option C).

## 4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap based on the data presented?
Correct: D
The high-dose group’s MDA concentration (2.5 nmol/mg) is lower than the low-dose group’s (3.8 nmol/mg). Option D incorrectly states they are the same, making it a trap.
