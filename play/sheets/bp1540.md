# BP1540

Researchers at the University of Midvale investigated the effect of a novel antimicrobial compound on the growth of *Staphylococcus aureus* across a range of pH levels. The study employed a controlled in vitro assay where bacterial cultures were incubated at pH 5.5, 6.0, 6.5, 7.0, and 7.5, each with a fixed concentration of the compound. Growth rates were measured as colony‑forming units per milliliter per hour (CFU/mL·h) after 24 hours of incubation. The results, summarized in Table 1, show a progressive increase in growth rate with rising pH, peaking at pH 7.5. Standard deviations were relatively small, indicating consistent measurements across replicates. The authors concluded that the compound’s efficacy is pH‑dependent, with optimal activity at neutral to slightly alkaline conditions.
