# BP1129

Researchers at the University of Greenfield investigated the growth dynamics of two bacterial strains, Strain A and Strain B, under identical laboratory conditions. Over a 10‑day period, they measured colony forming units (CFUs) at 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, and 10 days. The data suggested that Strain A exhibited a rapid initial growth phase followed by a plateau, whereas Strain B grew steadily throughout the observation period. The researchers hypothesized that the difference might be due to variations in metabolic pathways affecting nutrient uptake. To test this, they planned to conduct a series of experiments manipulating glucose concentrations and measuring the resulting growth curves. ## bp1129_q1 Based on the data presented, which statement best describes the growth pattern of Strain A over the 10‑day period?
Correct: B
The CFU counts for Strain A increase sharply from day 0 to day 4, then remain nearly constant from day 6 onward, indicating a rapid initial phase followed by a plateau.

## bp1129_q2 Which experimental design would best test the hypothesis that glucose concentration affects the growth rate of Strain B?
Correct: C
To isolate the effect of glucose, multiple cultures with different glucose levels should be compared while keeping all other variables constant.

## bp1129_q3 Using the data for Strain B, calculate the average daily growth rate (%) between day 2 and day 10.
Correct: C
Growth from 1.5e4 to 4.4e4 over 8 days: (4.4e4/1.5e4)^(1/8) - 1 ≈ 0.225 or 22.5%.

## bp1129_q4 Which of the following is a potential confounding factor that could invalidate the conclusion that glucose concentration affects Strain B growth?
Correct: C
Different inoculum sizes would affect initial growth rates, confounding the effect of glucose concentration.
