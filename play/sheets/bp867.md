# BP867

Researchers studied the titration of eight common amino acids to determine their side‑chain pKa values and isoelectric points (pI). Each amino acid was dissolved in 0.1 M phosphate buffer (pH 7.0) and titrated with 0.1 M HCl while continuously measuring the pH with a calibrated electrode. The titration curves were plotted, and the inflection points were used to calculate the pKa values of the side chains. The data were then combined with the known α‑COOH (pKa ≈ 2.18) and α‑NH3⁺ (pKa ≈ 9.74) values to compute the pI for each amino acid. The experiment was performed in triplicate to ensure reproducibility, and the final values were compared to literature values from Lehninger (8th ed.) and the AAMC Mastery Sheets. The results confirmed that basic residues such as lysine and arginine have high pI values, whereas acidic residues like aspartate and glutamate have low pI values.
