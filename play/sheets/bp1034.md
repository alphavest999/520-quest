# BP1034

Researchers studied the pharmacokinetics of a novel anti‑inflammatory compound, Compound Y, in a randomized, double‑blind, crossover trial involving 12 healthy volunteers. Participants received a single oral dose of 200 mg of Compound Y on day 1 and, after a 14‑day washout, a placebo on day 15. Blood samples were collected at baseline and at 1, 2, 4, 6, 8, 12, and 24 hours post‑dose. Plasma concentrations were quantified using a validated LC‑MS/MS method. The mean peak concentration (Cmax) was 3.2 µg/mL, occurring at 4.5 hours (Tmax). The area under the concentration‑time curve (AUC0‑∞) was 18.6 µg·h/mL, and the apparent elimination half‑life (t½) was 9.8 hours. No serious adverse events were reported. The data suggest that Compound Y has a moderate absorption rate and a relatively long elimination half‑life, making it a candidate for once‑daily dosing. However, inter‑subject variability was notable, with Cmax ranging from 1.8 to 4.6 µg/mL.

## 2 Which design feature best ensures that inter‑subject variability is minimized?
Correct: B
A crossover design allows each subject to serve as their own control, thereby reducing inter‑subject variability.

## 3 If the AUC0‑∞ is 18.6 µg·h/mL and the elimination half‑life is 9.8 hours, what is the approximate elimination rate constant (k)?
Correct: A
k = ln(2)/t½ = 0.693/9.8 ≈ 0.0707 h⁻¹, which rounds to 0.07 h⁻¹.
