# BP1640

Researchers studied BP1640, a novel small‑molecule inhibitor targeting the kinase domain of protein X, which is overexpressed in several solid tumors. In vitro assays were performed on three cancer cell lines (A549, MCF‑7, and PC3) to determine the compound’s potency and cytotoxicity. The team then evaluated BP1640 in a murine xenograft model using A549 cells, monitoring tumor volume and survival over 28 days. Key findings included a dose‑dependent inhibition of cell proliferation, an IC50 of 0.8 µM in A549 cells, and a maximum tolerated dose (MTD) of 50 mg/kg in mice. The study also assessed off‑target effects by measuring kinase activity in non‑cancerous fibroblasts, which remained below 10 % inhibition at therapeutic concentrations. The authors concluded that BP1640 selectively targets protein X, offers a favorable therapeutic window, and warrants further preclinical development.
