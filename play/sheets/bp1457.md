# BP1457

Researchers studied the effect of varying concentrations of a novel antibiotic on the growth of *Escherichia coli* in vitro. Five concentrations (0, 1, 5, 10, and 20 µg/mL) were added to identical cultures, and optical density at 600 nm (OD600) was measured after 24 hours of incubation at 37 °C. The aim was to determine the minimum inhibitory concentration (MIC) and assess dose‑response relationships. The experiment was conducted in triplicate, and all cultures were prepared from the same overnight starter to ensure consistency. The data were collected in a single 96‑well plate, and the plate was read using a microplate reader. The results suggested a clear decrease in OD600 with increasing antibiotic concentration, indicating effective bacterial growth inhibition.

Concentration (µg/mL) | OD600
0 | 1.20
1 | 1.05
5 | 0.78
10 | 0.45
20 | 0.12

## 1 Based on the data presented, which antibiotic concentration resulted in the lowest optical density after 24 hours?
Correct: D
The table shows that the OD600 at 20 µg/mL is 0.12, the lowest value among all concentrations, indicating the greatest inhibition of bacterial growth.

## 2 Which of the following controls would best validate that the observed effects are due to the antibiotic and not to other variables?
Correct: B
The 0 µg/mL well serves as the negative control, showing bacterial growth without antibiotic, thereby confirming that any inhibition in other wells is due to the antibiotic.

## 3 Calculate the percent inhibition of bacterial growth at 10 µg/mL relative to the 0 µg/mL control.
Correct: C
Percent inhibition = [(1.20 – 0.45) / 1.20] × 100 = 62.5 %, rounded to 62 %.

## 4 Which of the following is a potential confounding factor that could invalidate the conclusion that the antibiotic inhibits bacterial growth?
Correct: B
If the antibiotic solution contains a solvent (e.g., DMSO) that itself inhibits growth, the observed effect might be due to the solvent rather than the antibiotic.

## 5 Which resource would best provide additional context on the mechanism of action of this novel antibiotic?
Correct: B
The original research paper typically includes mechanistic studies and detailed discussion of how the antibiotic interacts with bacterial targets.
