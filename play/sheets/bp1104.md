# BP1104

Researchers studied the relationship between demographic factors and cardiovascular risk markers in a cross-sectional sample of 1,200 adults aged 20 to 70 years. Participants were recruited from primary care clinics across three urban regions and completed a standardized questionnaire and a physical examination. The investigators measured height, weight, blood pressure, and fasting lipid profile. The data were summarized by variable, mean (standard deviation), and p‑value for the association with age. The table below presents key findings. The study aimed to identify which factors were most strongly associated with elevated cholesterol and blood pressure, thereby informing targeted screening strategies. All procedures were approved by the institutional review board, and informed consent was obtained from each participant. The researchers hypothesized that higher body mass index and older age would correlate with higher systolic blood pressure and cholesterol levels. The results were intended to guide clinicians in prioritizing patients for lifestyle interventions and pharmacologic therapy.

Correct: C
