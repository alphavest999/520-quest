## bp1409_q2 Which experimental design element best controls for placebo effect in this study?
Correct: A
Randomization ensures that both known and unknown confounding factors are equally distributed between the treatment and placebo groups, thereby controlling for placebo effect. Baseline measurement, dosage, and adverse event recording do not directly address placebo bias.
