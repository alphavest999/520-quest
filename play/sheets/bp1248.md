# BP1248

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antihypertensive drug on systolic blood pressure in adults aged 45–60. In a double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial, 120 participants were randomized to receive either the drug or a placebo for 12 weeks. Blood pressure was measured at baseline and at weeks 4, 8, and 12. The mean systolic pressure (mmHg) and standard deviation (SD) for each group are shown in Table 1. The investigators reported a statistically significant reduction in the drug group compared with placebo at week 12 (p < 0.01). They concluded that the drug effectively lowers blood pressure in this population.

Table 1. Mean systolic blood pressure (mmHg) by group and time point

| Time (weeks) | Drug (mean ± SD) | Placebo (mean ± SD) |
|--------------|------------------|---------------------|
| 0 (baseline) | 148 ± 12         | 147 ± 11            |
| 4            | 140 ± 10         | 145 ± 12            |
| 8            | 136 ± 9          | 143 ± 11            |
| 12           | 132 ± 8          | 141 ± 10            |

## bp1248-01 Which of the following best describes the trend in systolic blood pressure for the drug group over the 12‑week study period?
Correct: C
The mean systolic pressure in the drug group decreases from 148 mmHg at baseline to 140 mmHg at week 4, 136 mmHg at week 8, and 132 mmHg at week 12, indicating a steady decline every 4 weeks.

## bp1248-02 Which of the following experimental design elements best supports the validity of the study’s conclusion?
Correct: B
Randomization reduces selection bias and ensures comparable groups, strengthening causal inference about the drug’s effect.

## bp1248-03 What is the percent change in mean systolic blood pressure for the drug group from baseline to week 12?
Correct: B
Percent change = [(148 – 132)/148] × 100 = 16/148 × 100 ≈ 10.8%. ## bp1248-04 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the study’s results?
Correct: C
While the study shows an association, the design does not rule out all confounders; claiming causality requires additional evidence.

## bp1248-05 How do the study’s findings support the hypothesis that the drug lowers systolic blood pressure?
Correct: B
The data show a steady decline in the drug group contrasted with a modest rise in the placebo group, aligning with the hypothesis.
