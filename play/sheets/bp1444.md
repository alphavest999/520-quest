# BP1444

Researchers studied the effect of three novel cognitive enhancers on working memory performance in healthy adults. Participants were randomly assigned to one of four groups: a control group receiving a placebo, or one of three treatment groups receiving Enhancer A, Enhancer B, or Enhancer C. Each participant completed a 30‑minute working memory task, and the time (in milliseconds) taken to correctly retrieve each item was recorded. The mean response times and standard deviations for each group are presented in Table 1. The study aimed to determine whether any of the enhancers produced a statistically significant improvement over the placebo. The researchers also collected secondary measures such as subjective alertness and heart rate variability to assess potential side effects. All participants provided informed consent, and the protocol was approved by the institutional review board. The data were analyzed using ANOVA followed by post‑hoc Tukey tests to compare group means. The results suggested that Enhancer A reduced response times relative to placebo, whereas Enhancer B and C did not differ significantly from the control. The authors concluded that Enhancer A may have a modest cognitive benefit, but further research is needed to confirm these findings and explore long‑term effects.

Table 1. Mean response times (ms) and standard deviations for each group

| Group      | Mean Response Time (ms) | Standard Deviation (ms) |
|------------|------------------------|--------------------------|

| Enhancer A | 780                    | 110                      |
| Enhancer B | 840                    | 115                      |
| Enhancer C | 860                    | 118                      |

## bp1444-01 Which group had the lowest mean response time according to Table 1?
Correct: B
The table shows that Enhancer A had a mean response time of 780 ms, which is lower than the other groups.

## bp1444-02 Which experimental design element best controls for participant expectancy effects?
Correct: C
Blinding participants prevents expectancy effects by ensuring they do not know which treatment they receive.

## bp1444-03 What is the absolute difference in mean response time between Enhancer A and the Control group?
Correct: A
Control mean is 850 ms; Enhancer A mean is 780 ms. 850 – 780 = 70 ms.

## bp1444-04 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data presented?
Correct: B
Enhancer B’s mean (840 ms) is actually slightly faster than the control (850 ms), so it did not produce a slower response time.

## bp1444-05 Which theoretical framework best explains the observed improvement in working memory for participants receiving Enhancer A?
Correct: B
The working‑memory capacity model predicts that increases in attentional resources (as may be facilitated by Enhancer A) lead to faster retrieval times.
