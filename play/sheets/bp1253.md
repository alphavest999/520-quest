# BP1253

Researchers at the University of Health Sciences investigated the acute effects of a novel antihypertensive agent, HypoRx, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in a small cohort of healthy volunteers. The study employed a single‑blind, crossover design in which each participant received both the drug and a placebo in random order, separated by a 7‑day washout period. SBP was measured using an automated cuff at baseline (prior to any intervention) and 2 hours after drug administration. The investigators recorded the following data for five subjects:

- Subject A: Baseline 140 mmHg, Post‑treatment 122 mmHg
- Subject B: Baseline 152 mmHg, Post‑treatment 134 mmHg
- Subject C: Baseline 138 mmHg, Post‑treatment 119 mmHg
- Subject D: Baseline 145 mmHg, Post‑treatment 128 mmHg
- Subject E: Baseline 149 mmHg, Post‑treatment 131 mmHg

The authors reported a mean SBP reduction of 18 mmHg (SD = 4.2 mmHg) following HypoRx, suggesting a clinically meaningful antihypertensive effect. They noted that the drug was well tolerated, with no adverse events reported during the study period.

## bp1253-01 Which subject experienced the greatest reduction in systolic blood pressure after receiving HypoRx?
Correct: C
Subject C’s SBP dropped from 138 mmHg to 119 mmHg, a change of 19 mmHg, which is the largest reduction among the five participants.

## bp1253-02 What type of study design was used to evaluate HypoRx’s effect on systolic blood pressure?
Correct: B
The investigators administered HypoRx and placebo to the same participants in random order, with a washout period, which is characteristic of a single‑blind crossover design.

## bp1253-03 What is the mean reduction in systolic blood pressure across all subjects after HypoRx administration?
Correct: B
Sum of changes: 18 + 18 + 19 + 17 + 18 = 90 mmHg. Dividing by 5 subjects yields a mean reduction of 18 mmHg.

## bp1253-04 Which subject’s systolic blood pressure increased after receiving HypoRx?
Correct: A
All subjects experienced a reduction in SBP; however, if a student incorrectly assumes a misprint, they might think Subject A’s SBP increased. The correct answer is that no subject’s SBP increased.

## bp1253-05 Based on the study’s findings, what would be an appropriate next step in the clinical development of HypoRx?
Correct: A
The pilot study demonstrates a promising effect but is limited by small sample size and single‑blind design. A large, double‑blind randomized controlled trial is the logical next step to establish efficacy and safety before regulatory approval.
