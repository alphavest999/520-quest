# bp002 – Enzyme Kinetics (Michaelis–Menten) with Inhibitors  
*(Markdown format)*  

---

## Passage  

Michaelis–Menten kinetics describe how the rate of an enzyme‑catalyzed reaction depends on substrate concentration \([S]\). The key parameters are \(V_{\max }\) (maximum velocity) and \(K_m\) (substrate concentration at half‑maximal velocity). Inhibitors alter these parameters by binding either to the free enzyme or to the enzyme–substrate complex. Competitive inhibitors bind to the active site, competing with substrate; they leave \(V_{\max}\) unchanged while increasing \(K_m\). Non‑competitive inhibitors bind to an allosteric site, reducing \(V_{\max}\) without altering \(K_m\). Uncompetitive inhibitors bind only to the enzyme–substrate complex, decreasing both \(V_{\max}\) and \(K_m\). Mixed inhibitors bind to both the free enzyme and the enzyme–substrate complex, leading to a decrease in \(V_{\max}\) and an increase in \(K_m\). The table below summarizes these effects.

| Inhibitor Type | Effect on \(V_{\max}\) | Effect on \(K_m\) | Typical Lineweaver–Burk Plot Change |
|----------------|------------------------|--------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Competitive    | No change              | ↑ (increase)       | Lines intersect at the y‑axis      |
| Non‑competitive| ↓ (decrease)           | No change          | Lines intersect at the x‑axis      |
| Uncompetitive  | ↓ (decrease)           | ↓ (decrease)       | Parallel lines (shifted left)      |
| Mixed          | ↓ (decrease)           | ↑ (increase)       | Lines intersect left of y‑axis     |

## Questions  

**Q1.** In a Lineweaver–Burk plot, the presence of an inhibitor causes all lines to intersect at a point on the y‑axis. Which inhibitor type is most likely responsible?  

A. Competitive  
B. Non‑competitive  
C. Uncompetitive  
D. Mixed  

**Answer:** A. Competitive  
**Explanation:** Competitive inhibition leaves \(V_{\max}\) unchanged, so the reciprocal of velocity (\(1/v\)) at infinite substrate concentration is the same. In a Lineweaver–Burk plot, this corresponds to the y‑axis intercept being unchanged, and all lines intersect at that point.

---

**Q2.** A researcher observes that both \(V_{\max}\) and \(K_m\) decrease in the presence of a new inhibitor. Which kinetic mechanism best explains this observation?  

A. Competitive inhibition  
B. Non‑competitive inhibition  
C. Uncompetitive inhibition  
D. Mixed inhibition  

**Answer:** C. Uncompetitive inhibition  
**Explanation:** Uncompetitive inhibitors bind only to the enzyme–substrate complex, reducing the number of active complexes (lower \(V_{\max}\)) and simultaneously lowering the apparent affinity for substrate (lower \(K_m\)). The table confirms this pattern.

---

**Q3.** Which of the following statements correctly describes the effect of a non‑competitive inhibitor on the Michaelis–Menten plot?  

A. The slope increases; the y‑intercept remains unchanged.  
B. The y‑intercept decreases; the slope remains unchanged.  
C. Both slope and y‑intercept decrease.  
D. Both slope and y‑intercept increase.  

**Answer:** B. The y‑intercept decreases; the slope remains unchanged.  
**Explanation:** Non‑competitive inhibition decreases \(V_{\max}\) (y‑intercept = \(1/V_{\max}\) increases), but \(K_m\) (slope = \(K_m/V_{\max}\)) remains the same because the apparent affinity is unchanged. Thus the slope stays constant while the y‑intercept shifts upward (i.e., \(1/V_{\max}\) increases).

---

**Q4.** A drug is known to bind only to the free enzyme, not to the enzyme–substrate complex. Which of the following changes in kinetic parameters would you expect?  

A. \(V_{\max}\) unchanged, \(K_m\) increased  
B. \(V_{\max}\) decreased, \(K_m\) unchanged  
C. Both \(V_{\max}\) and \(K_m\) decreased  
D. Both \(V_{\max}\) and \(K_m\) increased  

**Answer:** A. \(V_{\max}\) unchanged, \(K_m\) increased  
**Explanation:** Binding only to the free enzyme corresponds to competitive inhibition. Competitive inhibitors raise the apparent \(K_m\) (lower affinity) but do not affect \(V_{\max}\).

---

**Q5.** In a mixed‑inhibition scenario, which of the following statements is true regarding the Lineweaver–Burk plot?  

A. The lines are parallel.  
B. The lines intersect left of the y‑axis.  
C. The lines intersect at the y‑axis.  
D. The lines intersect at the x‑axis.  

**Answer:** B. The lines intersect left of the y‑axis.  
**Explanation:** Mixed inhibition decreases \(V_{\max}\) and increases \(K_m\). On a Lineweaver–Burk plot this produces lines that intersect left of the y‑axis (i.e., the intersection point has a positive x‑intercept).
