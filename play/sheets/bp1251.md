# BP1251

Researchers studied the effect of a novel compound, X-101, on the proliferation of human breast cancer cell lines. The experiment involved four distinct treatment groups: (1) control (no compound), (2) low dose (10 µM), (3) medium dose (50 µM), and (4) high dose (100 µM). Cells were cultured for 72 hours, after which viability was assessed using an MTT assay. The results indicated a dose-dependent decrease in cell viability, with the high dose group showing the greatest reduction. The researchers also measured the expression levels of the apoptosis marker cleaved caspase‑3 and found a corresponding increase with higher doses of X‑101. The study suggests that X‑101 induces apoptosis in a dose‑responsive manner and could be a promising candidate for further preclinical development.

Correct: B
A vehicle‑only control ensures that any changes are due to X‑101 itself, not the solvent or handling.
