# BP1744

Researchers studied the effect of a new cognitive training program on working memory in adults aged 50-65. The study recruited 120 participants and randomly assigned them to one of three groups: (1) training group, (2) active control group (engaged in general reading), and (3) passive control group (no intervention). Over a 12-week period, participants completed a battery of working memory tasks at baseline, mid-intervention, and post-intervention. The training group received 30‑minute sessions twice weekly that incorporated adaptive n‑back exercises. The active control group received 30‑minute sessions of non‑adaptive reading comprehension tasks. The passive control group received no sessions. Table 1 summarizes the mean scores (out of 100) and standard deviations for the primary working memory composite at post‑intervention. The researchers hypothesize that the training group will show a statistically significant improvement over both control groups. They plan to use a two‑way ANOVA to analyze group and time effects, followed by post‑hoc pairwise comparisons. The study also examines potential moderators such as baseline cognitive status and sleep quality.

## bp1744-1 Which group achieved the highest mean working memory composite score at post‑intervention?
Correct: A
The training group had the highest mean score (78.5) compared to the active (72.3) and passive (70.1) controls.

## bp1744-2 Which experimental design element best supports the causal inference that the training program improves working memory?
Correct: A
Random assignment controls for confounding variables, allowing causal inference about the training program’s effect.

## bp1744-3 Assuming equal sample sizes, calculate the approximate effect size (Cohen’s d) between the training and passive control groups.
Correct: C
Pooled SD ≈ 8.66; difference in means = 8.4; d = 8.4 / 8.66 ≈ 0.97.

## bp1744-4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap in interpreting the study results?
Correct: C
Social interaction could confound the effect attributed to the training content.

## bp1744-5 Which concept is most directly related to the researchers’ plan to examine moderators such as baseline cognitive status?
Correct: B
Examining moderators involves testing for interaction effects between group and baseline status.
