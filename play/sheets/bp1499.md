# BP1499

Researchers at the University of Health Sciences conducted a randomized controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypotensol, in reducing systolic blood pressure (SBP) among adults with stage 1 hypertension. One hundred and twenty participants were randomly assigned to one of three groups: (1) Hypotensol 10 mg daily, (2) Hypotensol 20 mg daily, or (3) placebo. Baseline SBP was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest using an automated cuff. After 4 weeks of treatment, SBP was re‑measured under identical conditions. The study aimed to determine whether the higher dose produced a greater reduction in SBP compared to the lower dose and placebo. ## bp1499_q1 Which of the following best describes the change in systolic blood pressure for the 20 mg Hypotensol group?
Correct: A
The mean change is calculated as baseline minus week‑4 SBP: 147 mmHg − 123 mmHg = 24 mmHg decrease.

## bp1499_q2 Which experimental design best matches the study described?
Correct: B
Participants were randomly assigned to treatment groups and outcomes were measured prospectively, which is the hallmark of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1499_q3 Calculate the average change in systolic blood pressure across all three groups.
Correct: A
Average change = (−13 + (−24) + (−6)) ÷ 3 = −14.33 mmHg, rounded to −14.3 mmHg.

## bp1499_q4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the study results?
Correct: C
A 4‑week duration may not reflect long‑term efficacy or safety; this is a common trap when evaluating short‑term trials.

## bp1499_q5 Which mnemonic could help you remember the key components of a randomized controlled trial?
Correct: C
The mnemonic "Randomized, Controlled, Trial" captures the essential elements: random assignment, a control group, and a trial structure.
