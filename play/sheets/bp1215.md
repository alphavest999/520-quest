# BP1215

Researchers studied the effect of a novel neuroprotective agent on neuronal survival after ischemic injury in a rodent model. Eighty rats were randomly assigned to one of four groups: a control group receiving vehicle, a low‑dose group (10 mg/kg), a medium‑dose group (30 mg/kg), and a high‑dose group (50 mg/kg). After 24 hours of induced ischemia, neuronal survival was quantified by counting surviving neurons in the hippocampal CA1 region. Results were expressed as mean ± standard deviation (SD) and compared using one‑way ANOVA with post‑hoc Tukey tests. The study found a dose‑dependent increase in neuronal survival, with the high‑dose group showing a statistically significant improvement (p < 0.01) over the control.
