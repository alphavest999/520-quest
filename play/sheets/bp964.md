# BP964

Researchers studied the acid–base behavior of twenty standard amino acids by titrating solutions of each at 25 °C. Each amino acid (0.1 M) was titrated with 0.1 M HCl or NaOH while monitoring pH with a calibrated glass electrode. The pH at which the solution’s net charge was zero was recorded as the isoelectric point (pI). The titration curves revealed distinct inflection points corresponding to the ionizable groups: α‑COOH, α‑NH3⁺, and side‑chain groups (e.g., side‑chain carboxyl of Glu, side‑chain amine of Lys). The experiment also measured the pKa values for each ionizable group by determining the midpoints of the buffer regions. The resulting data are summarized in Table 1. The researchers noted that temperature variations shift the pKa values by ~0.01 pH units per °C, and that the presence of 0.5 M NaCl slightly compresses the titration curves due to ionic strength effects.

| Amino Acid | Side‑Chain pKa | pI | Notes |
|------------|----------------|----|-------|
| Lysine | 10.5 | 9.6 | Basic |
| Glutamic acid | 4.3 | 3.2 | Acidic |
| Histidine | 6.0 | 7.6 | Imidazole |
| Tyrosine | 10.1 | 5.7 | Phenolic |
| Alanine | — | 6.0 | Non‑ionizable side chain |
