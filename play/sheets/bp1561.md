# BP1561

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel anti‑inflammatory compound, Compound X, on the proliferation of cultured human fibroblasts. Twenty‑four fibroblast cultures were divided into three groups: a vehicle control, a low‑dose (10 µM) Compound X, and a high‑dose (50 µM) Compound X. After 48 hours, cell viability was assessed using a resazurin reduction assay. The results, summarized in Table 1, indicate a dose‑dependent decrease in viability. The study concludes that Compound X inhibits fibroblast proliferation in a concentration‑dependent manner, suggesting potential therapeutic value for conditions characterized by excessive fibroblast activity.

Table 1. Cell viability (% of control) after 48 h treatment

| Sample | Treatment | Viability (%) | p‑value |
|--------|-----------|---------------|---------|
| 1 | Control | 100.0 | – |
| 2 | 10 µM Compound X | 78.4 | 0.032 |
| 3 | 50 µM Compound X | 45.6 | 0.001 |
| 4 | 10 µM Compound X (duplicate) | 80.1 | 0.028 |
| 5 | 50 µM Compound X (duplicate) | 47.2 | 0.003 |

*Data are mean ± SD (n = 2).

## 1561-01 Which sample exhibited the greatest reduction in cell viability relative to the control?
Correct: B
Sample 3 shows 45.6 % viability, the lowest value among all samples, indicating the greatest reduction relative to the 100 % control.

## 1561-02 Which experimental design change would most effectively control for inter‑plate variability in future studies?
Correct: C
Ensuring that the vehicle control contains the same solvent concentration as the treatment groups eliminates solvent‑related variability, which is a common source of inter‑plate differences.

## 1561-03 Calculate the percent difference in viability between the low‑dose (10 µM) and high‑dose (50 µM) Compound X treatments.
Correct: C
Percent difference = |78.4 % – 45.6 %| / 78.4 % × 100 % ≈ 54.4 %.

## 1561-04 Which of the following statements is a potential confounder that could have influenced the observed decrease in viability?
Correct: B
Serum contains growth factors that could mask or alter the effect of Compound X on fibroblast proliferation, acting as a confounder.

## 1561-05 Which of the following best summarizes the study’s conclusion?
Correct: C
The data show a clear, statistically significant, dose‑dependent reduction in viability, leading to the conclusion that Compound X inhibits fibroblast proliferation.
