# BP1144

Researchers studied the pharmacokinetics of a novel anti-inflammatory drug, Compound X, in healthy volunteers. In a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled crossover design, 12 participants received a single oral dose of 50 mg Compound X or placebo on separate occasions, with a washout period of 7 days. Blood samples were collected at baseline and at 5, 15, 30, 60, 120, and 240 minutes postdose. The plasma concentration of Compound X was quantified using a validated LC-MS/MS method. Table 1 summarizes the mean concentration (± SD) at each time point. The data indicate a rapid absorption phase, reaching a peak concentration (Cmax) at 30 minutes, followed by a gradual decline. The area under the concentration-time curve (AUC0–240) was 1,200 ± 150 µM·min. No adverse events were reported. These findings suggest that Compound X has favorable pharmacokinetic properties, with a short absorption window and a moderate elimination half-life, supporting its potential use as an acute anti-inflammatory agent.

## bp1144-3 Calculate the average concentration of Compound X over the first 30 minutes (5–30 min) using the mean values.

Average = (200 + 350 + 500) / 3 = 350 µM.

## bp1144-4 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data?
