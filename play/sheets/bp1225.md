# BP1225

Researchers studied the effect of a novel dietary supplement on exercise performance in healthy adults. Forty male volunteers aged 20-35 were randomized into two groups: the supplement group received 500 mg of the compound daily for 8 weeks, while the control group received a placebo. Baseline VO2max and lactate threshold were measured, followed by weekly assessments of muscle strength and endurance. At the end of the study, the supplement group exhibited a 12% increase in VO2max and a 9% improvement in lactate threshold compared to baseline, whereas the placebo group showed negligible changes. Muscle biopsies revealed increased mitochondrial density in the supplement group. The study concluded that the supplement may enhance aerobic capacity and muscular endurance in trained individuals. However, the authors noted that the sample size was limited and the study duration may not capture long‑term effects.
