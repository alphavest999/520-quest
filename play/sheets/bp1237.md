# BP1237

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antihypertensive drug, CardioX, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with stage 1 hypertension. In a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial, 120 participants were assigned to one of four groups: placebo (n=30), low‑dose CardioX (5 mg, n=30), medium‑dose CardioX (10 mg, n=30), or high‑dose CardioX (20 mg, n=30). Blood pressure was measured at baseline and after 12 weeks of treatment. Table 3 summarizes the mean SBP reduction (±SD) for each group. The investigators hypothesized a dose‑dependent response, with the highest dose yielding the greatest reduction. Secondary outcomes included changes in diastolic blood pressure and incidence of adverse events. The study found that CardioX produced a statistically significant, dose‑dependent decrease in SBP, with the 20 mg group showing the largest mean reduction. No serious adverse events were reported, and the drug was well tolerated across all doses.

Table 3: Mean SBP Reduction (mmHg ± SD)

| Group | Mean Reduction | SD |
|-------|----------------|----|
| Placebo | 2.1 | 1.5 |
| 5 mg | 5.4 | 2.0 |
| 10 mg | 8.7 | 2.3 |
| 20 mg | 12.2 | 2.8 |
