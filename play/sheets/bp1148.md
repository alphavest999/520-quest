# BP1148

Researchers investigated the effect of varying concentrations of a novel antiproliferative agent on cultured human fibroblasts over a 48‑hour period. Cells were seeded at a density of 1 × 10^5 cells per well and treated with vehicle (0 µM) or the drug at concentrations of 5, 10, 20, 40, and 80 µM. After 48 hours, cell proliferation was quantified by a crystal violet assay, and results were expressed as a percentage of the vehicle control. The data revealed a clear dose‑dependent inhibition of proliferation: 5 µM reduced growth to 85 % of control, 10 µM to 70 %, 20 µM to 45 %, 40 µM to 20 %, and 80 µM to 5 %. These findings suggest that the drug’s cytostatic activity increases with concentration, with a steep decline in cell viability beyond 20 µM. The researchers plan to perform additional experiments to determine whether the observed effects are reversible and to identify the underlying molecular pathways.

## bp1148-01 Which concentration of the drug produced the greatest reduction in cell proliferation compared to vehicle?
Correct: D
The table shows that 80 µM reduces proliferation to 5 % of control, the lowest value among all concentrations, indicating the greatest reduction.

## bp1148-02 Which of the following experimental controls would best strengthen the validity of the dose‑response findings?
Correct: C
The solvent control ensures that any observed effect is due to the drug itself and not the vehicle. An untreated control (A) is less informative because the vehicle may influence cells. A positive control (B) is useful for assay validation but does not address vehicle effects. A low concentration (D) is not a true control.

## bp1148-03 What is the percent decrease in proliferation rate when the drug concentration increases from 0 µM to 20 µM?
Correct: A
The proliferation rate at 0 µM is 100 %. At 20 µM it is 45 %. The decrease is 100 % – 45 % = 55 %. However, the question asks for the percent decrease relative to the original value: 55 % of 100 % equals a 55 % decrease. The correct answer is 55 % (option B).

Correct: D
