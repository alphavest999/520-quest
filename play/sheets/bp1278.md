# BP1278

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel dietary supplement on metabolic parameters in adult male mice. Twenty-four mice were randomly assigned to either a control diet (n=12) or a supplement-enriched diet (n=12) for eight weeks. Body weight was recorded weekly, and at the end of the study, fasting blood samples were collected to measure serum insulin levels. Table 1 summarizes the key outcomes. The supplement group exhibited a modest but statistically significant increase in average weight gain compared to controls. Serum insulin concentrations were also higher in the supplement group, suggesting an impact on insulin sensitivity. No adverse effects were observed, and food intake remained comparable between groups. These findings support further investigation into the supplement’s potential role in modulating metabolic health.
