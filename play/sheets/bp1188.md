# BP1188

Researchers studied the effect of varying doses of a novel cognitive enhancer on reaction time in healthy adults. Participants were randomly assigned to one of four groups: Control (placebo), Low Dose (10 mg), Medium Dose (20 mg), and High Dose (40 mg). Each participant completed a computerized simple reaction time task, and mean response times were recorded. The study aimed to determine whether increasing doses would produce a dose‑response relationship in reaction time improvement. The data collected are summarized in Table 1 below, which includes mean response times and standard deviations for each group. The researchers hypothesized that higher doses would lead to faster reaction times, with the greatest improvement observed in the High Dose group.

Group,Treatment,Mean Response Time (ms),Standard Deviation
Control,Placebo,350,15
Low Dose,10 mg,330,12
Medium Dose,20 mg,310,10
High Dose,40 mg,290,8

## bp1188_q1 According to Table 1, which group exhibited the fastest mean reaction time?
Correct: D
The High Dose group had the lowest mean response time of 290 ms, indicating the fastest reaction time among all groups.
