# BP1199

Researchers at the University of Health Sciences conducted a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypertensol, in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A total of 200 participants were assigned to one of four groups: placebo (n = 50), 10 mg (n = 50), 20 mg (n = 50), or 40 mg (n = 50). Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest. After 4 weeks of daily dosing, SBP was reassessed under identical conditions. The investigators recorded mean SBP values and calculated mean reductions for each group. The study aimed to determine dose‑response relationships and to identify the optimal dose that maximizes blood pressure lowering while minimizing adverse effects. The data are summarized in Table 1 below. The researchers also collected safety data, but the primary outcome was the mean change in SBP.

Group | Baseline SBP (mmHg) | 4‑week SBP (mmHg) | Mean Reduction (mmHg)
Placebo | 145 | 143 | 2
10 mg | 147 | 132 | 15
20 mg | 148 | 136 | 12
40 mg | 149 | 120 | 29

## bp1199-01 Which dosage group achieved the greatest mean reduction in systolic blood pressure after 4 weeks?
Correct: D
The mean reduction for the 40 mg group was 29 mmHg, which is the largest among all groups. The placebo group had only a 2 mmHg reduction, and the 10 mg and 20 mg groups had 15 mmHg and 12 mmHg reductions, respectively.

## bp1199-02 Which study design best controls for the placebo effect in this trial?
Correct: B
A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial is specifically designed to minimize bias and placebo effects by randomly assigning participants to treatment or placebo groups and blinding both participants and investigators.

## bp1199-03 What is the mean reduction in systolic blood pressure for the 20 mg group?
Correct: B
The table lists the mean reduction for the 20 mg group as 12 mmHg (148 mmHg baseline minus 136 mmHg after 4 weeks).

## bp1199-04 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data presented in Table 1?
Correct: B
The table shows a mean reduction of only 2 mmHg for the placebo group, which is unlikely to be statistically significant. The other statements are directly supported by the data.

## bp1199-05 Which pharmacological concept is most closely linked to the dose‑response relationship observed in this study?
Correct: B
The study demonstrates how increasing doses of Hypertensol produce greater reductions in blood pressure, illustrating a dose‑response relationship—a core principle of pharmacodynamics.
