## bp1212-02 Which experimental design element is most critical for controlling inter‑subject variability in this study?
Correct: B
A crossover design allows each participant to serve as their own control, thereby reducing inter‑subject variability. Randomization and blinding are important, but the crossover design is the key element for controlling variability.
