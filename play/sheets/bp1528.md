# BP1528

Researchers studied the effect of a novel dietary supplement on insulin sensitivity in a cohort of 120 adults aged 30–55. Participants were randomly assigned to either the supplement group (n = 60) or a placebo group (n = 60) and followed for 12 weeks. Baseline fasting glucose and insulin levels were measured, and the Homeostatic Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance (HOMA‑IR) was calculated. After 12 weeks, the supplement group showed a mean HOMA‑IR reduction of 1.2 units (SD = 0.4), whereas the placebo group’s mean reduction was 0.3 units (SD = 0.5). The study also recorded changes in body mass index (BMI) and reported no adverse events. The authors concluded that the supplement significantly improves insulin sensitivity compared to placebo, suggesting potential therapeutic benefits for pre‑diabetic individuals.

## bp1528-1 Which of the following best describes the primary outcome of this study?
Correct: A
The study’s primary outcome was the change in HOMA‑IR, a measure of insulin sensitivity. The supplement group had a greater reduction (1.2 vs. 0.3) indicating improved insulin sensitivity.

## bp1528-2 Which of the following study designs best matches the described methodology?
Correct: C
Participants were randomly assigned to supplement or placebo, and outcomes were measured over time, which is the hallmark of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1528-3 If the researchers wanted to calculate the 95% confidence interval for the mean HOMA‑IR change in the supplement group, which of the following formulas would they use?
Correct: A
For a small sample (n=60) and unknown population SD, the t‑distribution is appropriate: CI = mean ± t × (SD/√n).

## bp1528-4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the study’s results?
Correct: C
Even with randomization, placebo effects can influence subjective outcomes; however, HOMA‑IR is an objective measure, so the placebo effect is less likely to explain the difference. The trap is over‑attributing placebo influence.

## bp1528-5 Which external resource would best help a clinician apply these findings to patient care?
Correct: A
A meta‑analysis aggregates multiple studies and provides broader evidence for clinical decision‑making.
