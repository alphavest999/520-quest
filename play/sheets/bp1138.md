# BP1138

Researchers investigated the relationship between light intensity and the rate of photosynthesis in spinach leaves. Spinach leaf discs were exposed to five different light intensities (200, 400, 600, 800, and 1,000 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹) in a controlled environment chamber set at 25 °C and 50 % relative humidity. Net photosynthesis rates were measured using a portable infrared gas analyzer after a 30‑minute equilibration period. The results indicated a positive correlation between light intensity and photosynthetic rate up to 800 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹, beyond which the rate plateaued. The data suggest that spinach leaves approach their light‑saturated photosynthetic capacity near 800 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹. These findings have implications for optimizing light conditions in controlled‑environment agriculture to maximize crop yield without exceeding the light saturation point.

## bp1138-1 Which light intensity produced the highest net photosynthesis rate in the study?
Correct: C
The table shows the net photosynthesis rate peaks at 15.1 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹ at 800 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹. Rates at 200, 400, 600, and 1,000 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹ are lower or plateaued, so 800 is the highest.

## bp1138-2 If you were to design a follow‑up experiment to test whether temperature influences the light‑saturation point, which variable should you keep constant?
Correct: D
To isolate the effect of temperature on the light‑saturation point, the temperature must be varied while keeping all other conditions (light intensity, leaf disc size, humidity) constant. Thus, temperature is the independent variable to change, not keep constant.

## bp1138-4 Which statement best explains the observed plateau in photosynthetic rate at 1,000 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹?
Correct: B
A plateau indicates that the photosynthetic machinery is saturated; additional light does not increase the rate. Damage or instrument error would likely produce erratic or decreasing rates, not a plateau.

## bp1138-5 How might the concept of light‑saturation point inform the design of artificial lighting in greenhouse production?
Correct: C
The study shows that photosynthesis plateaus near 800 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹. Lighting above this level wastes energy without increasing yield, while lower levels limit photosynthesis. Thus, targeting the saturation point optimizes both yield and energy efficiency.
