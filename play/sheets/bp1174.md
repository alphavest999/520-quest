# BP1174

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel antioxidant compound, Antiox-5, on the oxidative stress levels in cultured human fibroblasts. In a controlled experiment, fibroblasts were divided into four groups: a vehicle control, a low-dose Antiox-5 (10 µM), a high-dose Antiox-5 (50 µM), and a positive control treated with a known antioxidant, N-acetylcysteine (NAC, 20 µM). After 24 hours of treatment, intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS) were quantified using a fluorescent probe, and the mean fluorescence intensity (MFI) was recorded for each group. The data were collected from three independent biological replicates, and the mean ± standard deviation (SD) was calculated. The results indicated a dose-dependent reduction in ROS levels with Antiox-5, with the high-dose group showing a 45% decrease compared to the vehicle control. NAC produced a 55% reduction. Statistical analysis using one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post hoc test revealed that both Antiox-5 doses were significantly different from the control (p < 0.01), but only the high dose differed significantly from NAC (p = 0.04). These findings suggest that Antiox-5 is a potent antioxidant, particularly at higher concentrations, and may be comparable to NAC in efficacy.

## bp1174-001 Based on the data presented in the passage, which statement best describes the effect of Antiox-5 on ROS levels?
Correct: C
The passage reports a 45% reduction in ROS for the high-dose Antiox-5 and a 55% reduction for NAC, indicating both doses are effective and the high dose is comparable to NAC.

## bp1174-002 Which experimental design element is most critical to ensure that the observed reduction in ROS is attributable to Antiox-5?
Correct: B
A vehicle control establishes the baseline ROS level, allowing comparison to treatment groups; without it, changes could be due to other variables.

## bp1174-003 If the mean MFI for the vehicle control is 1200 and the mean MFI for the high-dose Antiox-5 is 660, what is the percent reduction in ROS?
Correct: A
Percent reduction = [(1200-660)/1200] × 100 = 540/1200 × 100 = 45%.

## bp1174-004 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the statistical significance reported in the passage?
Correct: C
Significance in one pairwise comparison does not automatically mean all comparisons are significant; each must be evaluated separately.

## bp1174-005 How does the data support the broader concept of dose-dependent pharmacological effects?
Correct: A
The passage demonstrates a clear dose-response: 10 µM reduces ROS by 45%, 50 µM by 55%, fitting the principle that higher doses often yield greater pharmacologic effects up to a point.
