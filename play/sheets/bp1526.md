# BP1526

Researchers conducted a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled study to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypertensol, in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. Eighty participants were randomly assigned to receive either Hypertensol (n = 40) or a matching placebo (n = 40) for four weeks. Systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured at baseline and after the treatment period. The investigators reported the following results:

Hypertensol group: mean baseline SBP = 150 mmHg; mean SBP at 4 weeks = 120 mmHg.
Placebo group: mean baseline SBP = 148 mmHg; mean SBP at 4 weeks = 140 mmHg.

The study concluded that Hypertensol produced a clinically meaningful reduction in SBP compared with placebo. The authors also noted that the difference between groups was statistically significant (p < 0.01).

Group | Baseline SBP (mmHg) | 4‑week SBP (mmHg)
Hypertensol | 150 | 120
Placebo | 148 | 140

## bp1526_q1 Which group experienced the greater absolute reduction in systolic blood pressure after the 4‑week treatment period?
Correct: A
The Hypertensol group decreased from 150 mmHg to 120 mmHg, an absolute reduction of 30 mmHg. The placebo group decreased from 148 mmHg to 140 mmHg, a reduction of 8 mmHg. Thus, the Hypertensol group had the greater absolute reduction.

## bp1526_q2 What type of study design was employed in this investigation?
Correct: A
The study randomized participants to receive either Hypertensol or placebo, used blinding, and compared outcomes after a defined intervention period, which defines a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1526_q3 What is the percent reduction in systolic blood pressure for the Hypertensol group?
Correct: C
Percent reduction = (Baseline – 4‑week)/Baseline × 100 = (150 – 120)/150 × 100 = 30/150 × 100 = 20 %.

## bp1526_q4 Which of the following statements is false regarding the study?
Correct: D
The baseline SBP values were 150 mmHg for Hypertensol and 148 mmHg for placebo, a difference of only 2 mmHg, which is not statistically significant. The statement that the baseline difference was significant is false.

## bp1526_q5 The authors’ conclusion that Hypertensol produced a clinically meaningful reduction in SBP is best supported by which of the following concepts?
Correct: A
An intention‑to‑treat analysis includes all participants as originally assigned, preserving the benefits of randomization and providing a conservative estimate of treatment effect. This approach supports the claim that Hypertensol produced a clinically meaningful reduction.
