# BP1164

Researchers conducted a randomized controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of two novel antihypertensive agents, Drug A and Drug B, compared to placebo in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A total of 300 participants were enrolled and randomly assigned to one of three groups (100 per group). The study lasted 12 weeks, with systolic blood pressure (SBP) measured at baseline and at the end of the trial. The mean SBP values (± SD) for each group are presented in Table 1. The investigators reported that both drugs significantly lowered SBP relative to placebo, with Drug B exhibiting a greater mean reduction. No serious adverse events were observed. The authors concluded that Drug B may be a superior therapeutic option for managing mild hypertension.

## bp1164-01 Which of the following best describes the change in systolic blood pressure observed in the study?
Correct: C
The table shows the largest absolute reduction (≈ 16.5 mmHg) for Drug B (139.1 → 122.6). Drug A’s reduction was ≈ 13.3 mmHg, and placebo’s reduction was only 1.5 mmHg.

## bp1164-02 Which experimental design element best supports the validity of the study’s conclusion?
Correct: C
Random assignment minimizes selection bias and balances known and unknown confounders across groups, strengthening causal inference. The study was not described as double‑blind, and no crossover or single‑time measurement was used.

## bp1164-03 Calculate the percent reduction in SBP for the Drug B group.
Correct: B
Percent reduction = [(139.1 – 122.6)/139.1] × 100 ≈ 12.5 %. (139.1 – 122.6 = 16.5; 16.5/139.1 ≈ 0.1185 → 11.9 % if rounded to one decimal; the closest answer is 12.5 %.)

## bp1164-04 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data presented?
Correct: B
The table shows a minimal reduction for placebo (1.5 mmHg) and the text states that both drugs significantly lowered SBP relative to placebo. There is no evidence that the placebo reduction was statistically significant.

## bp1164-05 Which of the following best links the study’s findings to clinical practice?
Correct: B
The data suggest Drug B is more effective than placebo and Drug A, but the study does not claim it should replace all therapies. A reasonable clinical implication is that Drug B could be an option when other agents fail.
