# BP1626

Researchers studied the effect of light intensity on the photosynthetic rate of the aquatic plant *Elodea canadensis*. Four light levels were tested: 100, 200, 400, and 800 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹. For each level, oxygen evolution was measured over 30 minutes and chlorophyll a content was quantified. The data showed a steep increase in oxygen production up to 400 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, after which the rate plateaued. The mean oxygen production (µmol O₂ g⁻¹ h⁻¹) and chlorophyll a (mg g⁻¹) were as follows: 100 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ – 12.5 ± 1.2 and 0.8; 200 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ – 23.8 ± 1.5 and 1.1; 400 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ – 37.0 ± 1.8 and 1.5; 800 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ – 38.5 ± 2.0 and 1.6. The researchers hypothesized a linear increase up to a saturation point, and performed regression analysis to test this relationship.

Light (µmol m⁻² s⁻¹) | O₂ production (µmol O₂ g⁻¹ h⁻¹) | Chlorophyll a (mg g⁻¹)
100 | 12.5 ± 1.2 | 0.8
200 | 23.8 ± 1.5 | 1.1
400 | 37.0 ± 1.8 | 1.5
800 | 38.5 ± 2.0 | 1.6

## bp1626-1 Which light intensity had the highest oxygen production?
Correct: D
The data show that oxygen production increases from 12.5 at 100 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹ to 38.5 at 800 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹, making 800 the highest value.

## bp1626-2 Which control was essential for isolating the effect of light intensity?
Correct: A
To attribute changes in photosynthesis solely to light intensity, the plant species must be constant; otherwise, species differences could confound the results.

## bp1626-3 Calculate the percent increase in oxygen production from 200 to 400 µmol m⁻² s⁻¹.
Correct: C
Percent increase = [(37.0 – 23.8) / 23.8] × 100 ≈ 55.5%. Option C is the closest.

## bp1626-4 Which statement is a potential confounding factor in this experiment?
Correct: A
Water temperature can influence photosynthetic rates; if not controlled, it confounds the relationship between light intensity and oxygen production.

## bp1626-5 Which finding supports the hypothesis that photosynthesis saturates at high light?
Correct: A
The small increase from 37.0 to 38.5 µmol O₂ g⁻¹ h⁻¹ when light is doubled from 400 to 800 indicates a plateau, supporting saturation.
