# BP1662

Researchers conducted a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypto‑X, in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. Two hundred participants were enrolled and randomly assigned to either Hypto‑X (n=100) or placebo (n=100). Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest and averaged over three readings. After 4 weeks of daily dosing, SBP was reassessed using the same protocol. The study aimed to determine the mean change in SBP and assess safety profiles. The investigators also collected data on adverse events, heart rate, and renal function. The primary outcome was the mean difference in SBP between groups at week 4. Secondary outcomes included the proportion of participants achieving target SBP <140 mmHg and incidence of treatment‑related adverse events. The trial was registered at ClinicalTrials.gov (NCT04567890).
