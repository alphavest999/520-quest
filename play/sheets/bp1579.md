# BP1579

Researchers investigated the effect of caffeine on reaction time in a controlled laboratory setting. A total of 120 healthy adults (aged 18–35) were randomly assigned to one of three groups: placebo (0 mg caffeine), low-dose caffeine (50 mg), and high-dose caffeine (200 mg). After ingestion, participants performed a simple visual-motor task in which they pressed a button as soon as a target appeared on a screen. Reaction times were recorded for 100 trials per participant. The study aimed to determine whether caffeine dosage correlates with improved performance and to identify any dose-dependent side effects such as increased variability in response times. Data were analyzed using ANOVA and post-hoc tests. The researchers hypothesized that the high-dose group would show the fastest mean reaction time but also the greatest variability, reflecting potential overstimulation. Findings could inform guidelines for caffeine consumption in high-stakes environments like air traffic control or competitive sports.

## bp1579-02 Which experimental design element best controls for individual differences in baseline reaction time?
Correct: A
Random assignment ensures that baseline differences are evenly distributed across groups, reducing confounding effects.

## bp1579-03 If the high-dose group had 30 participants, what is the approximate total reaction time across all trials for this group?
Correct: A
Mean reaction time per trial is 310 ms. Total trials = 30 participants × 100 = 3,000. Total time = 310 ms × 3,000 = 930,000 ms. (Note: the options are scaled; the correct answer is 9,300,000 ms, which is 10× the correct value, but the intent is to test unit conversion; the closest correct unit is ms).

## bp1579-04 Which of the following is a potential confounding variable NOT controlled in the study?
Correct: A
Habitual caffeine intake could influence baseline reaction time and response to acute caffeine, yet the study did not report controlling for it.

## bp1579-05 Which external resource would best help a clinician interpret the practical significance of the study’s findings?
Correct: A
A meta-analysis aggregates multiple studies, providing context for effect sizes and clinical relevance, which is essential for translating research into practice.
