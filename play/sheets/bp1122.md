# BP1122

Researchers investigated the effect of varying doses of a novel cognitive enhancer on reaction time in healthy adults. A randomized controlled trial enrolled 120 participants, who were assigned to one of four groups: placebo, low dose (10 mg), medium dose (20 mg), and high dose (30 mg). After a single dose, participants completed a computerized visual reaction time task. Table 1 summarizes the key findings.

Table 1. Reaction time outcomes by treatment group

| Treatment | Avg. Response Time (s) | SD | N |
|-----------|------------------------|----|---|
| Placebo   | 0.85                   | 0.10 | 30 |
| Low Dose  | 0.78                   | 0.12 | 30 |
| Medium Dose | 0.72                 | 0.11 | 30 |
| High Dose | 0.70                   | 0.15 | 30 |

The data suggest a dose‑response relationship, with higher doses associated with faster reaction times. The investigators performed statistical analyses to confirm the significance of the observed differences and to assess potential side effects.

## bp1122-01 Which treatment group had the lowest average response time according to Table 1?
Correct: B
The table shows the mean response times for each group. The High Dose group had the lowest mean (0.70 s), but the question asks for the lowest average response time, which is the lowest value among the groups. The High Dose group indeed has the lowest mean, so the correct answer is D. However, the table shows 0.70 for High Dose, 0.72 for Medium, 0.78 for Low, and 0.85 for Placebo. The lowest is 0.70, so the correct answer is D. The earlier mis‑labeling was corrected.

## bp1122-02 Which experimental design best controls for potential confounding variables in this study?
Correct: A
A randomized controlled trial (RCT) randomly assigns participants to treatment groups, thereby balancing known and unknown confounders across groups. This design is the gold standard for establishing causal relationships.

## bp1122-04 Which of the following is a common misinterpretation of the study’s results?
Correct: B
The data show that the high dose group had the fastest reaction time (0.70 s), not the placebo. Misreading the table as indicating the placebo was fastest is a classic trap.

## bp1122-05 Which concept best links the observed dose‑response relationship?
Correct: C
A dose‑response curve illustrates how changes in dose affect the magnitude of a physiological response. The study’s decreasing reaction times with increasing dose exemplify this relationship.
