# BP1389

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antioxidant compound, X-123, on oxidative stress markers in cultured human fibroblasts. Cells were divided into four groups: (1) untreated control, (2) vehicle control (0.1% DMSO), (3) low-dose X-123 (10 µM), and (4) high-dose X-123 (50 µM). After 24 hours of exposure, intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS) levels were quantified using the DCFH-DA fluorescent probe, and protein carbonyl content was measured by the DNPH assay. The results, expressed as mean ± SD, were compiled in Table 1. The researchers noted a dose-dependent decrease in ROS and carbonyl levels, with the high-dose group showing a 45% reduction in ROS relative to the untreated control. Statistical analysis was performed using one-way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post-hoc test, yielding p-values < 0.01 for all comparisons between treated and control groups. The authors concluded that X-123 effectively mitigates oxidative damage in fibroblasts, suggesting potential therapeutic applications for age-related tissue degeneration.

Table 1. Oxidative Stress Markers in Fibroblasts

| Group | ROS (mean ± SD, arbitrary units) | Protein Carbonyl (nmol/mg protein) |
|-------|-----------------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| Control | 100.0 ± 5.2 | 12.5 ± 0.8 |
| Vehicle | 98.7 ± 4.9 | 12.1 ± 0.7 |
| Low X-123 | 70.4 ± 3.6 | 8.9 ± 0.5 |
| High X-123 | 55.0 ± 2.8 | 6.8 ± 0.4 |

## bp1389-1 Which of the following best describes the statistical significance of the difference in ROS levels between the high-dose X-123 group and the untreated control group?
Correct: C
The study reports p-values < 0.01 for all comparisons between treated and control groups, indicating a statistically significant difference. The 45% reduction in ROS represents a large effect size, supporting option C.

## bp1389-2 If the researchers wanted to test whether X-123’s antioxidant effect is dose-dependent, which experimental design would best address this hypothesis?
Correct: B
A dose-response analysis with multiple concentrations directly tests the relationship between dose and effect, which is essential for establishing dose-dependency.

## bp1389-3 Calculate the percent reduction in protein carbonyl content in the high-dose X-123 group relative to the untreated control.
Correct: C
Percent reduction = [(12.5 - 6.8) / 12.5] × 100 = 5.7 / 12.5 × 100 ≈ 45.6%. Option C is the closest.

## bp1389-4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap for interpreting the study’s results?
Correct: A
While the vehicle control shows a slight decrease in ROS, the difference is not statistically significant; assuming DMSO has no effect ignores potential subtle vehicle effects.
