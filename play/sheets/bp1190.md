# BP1190

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antioxidant compound, X-42, on oxidative stress markers in a cohort of 120 middle‑aged adults. Participants were randomly assigned to either a placebo group (n = 60) or an X‑42 treatment group (n = 60). Over 12 weeks, blood samples were collected at baseline, 6 weeks, and 12 weeks. The primary outcome was the change in plasma malondialdehyde (MDA) concentration, a marker of lipid peroxidation. Secondary outcomes included serum glutathione peroxidase (GPx) activity and the ratio of oxidized to reduced glutathione (GSSG/GSH). Table 1 summarizes the mean changes in these biomarkers at each time point. The investigators hypothesized that X‑42 would significantly reduce MDA levels and improve antioxidant enzyme activity compared to placebo.

## bp1190-01 Based on the data presented, which statement best describes the trend in plasma MDA levels over the 12‑week study period?
Correct: C
The table shows a progressive decline in MDA concentration from 5.8 µmol/L at baseline to 4.2 µmol/L at 12 weeks, indicating a gradual reduction.

## bp1190-02 Which of the following best describes the experimental design used in this study?
Correct: B
Participants were randomly assigned to placebo or X‑42 groups, and both participants and investigators were blinded to the allocation, constituting a double‑blind RCT.

## bp1190-03 If the mean MDA level in the placebo group at 12 weeks is 5.0 µmol/L with a standard deviation of 0.6 µmol/L, what is the 95% confidence interval for the mean MDA level in this group?
Correct: B
Using t‑distribution with n=60, SE = 0.6/√60 ≈ 0.077. 95% CI = 5.0 ± 2.00×0.077 ≈ 5.0 ± 0.154 → 4.846 to 5.154, rounded to 4.8–5.2 µmol/L. Option B is the closest.

## bp1190-04 Which of the following is a potential confounding variable that the researchers should control for when interpreting the results?
Correct: B
Dietary antioxidant intake could influence oxidative stress markers independently of the intervention, acting as a confounder.

## bp1190-05 Which of the following best links the study’s findings to a broader clinical application?
Correct: B
Enhanced antioxidant enzyme activity (GPx) is associated with reduced oxidative damage in neurodegenerative conditions, suggesting a preventive role.
