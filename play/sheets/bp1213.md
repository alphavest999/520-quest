# BP1213

Researchers studied the effect of a novel kinase inhibitor on the proliferation of human breast cancer cells (MCF‑7). Cells were treated with varying concentrations of the drug (0, 1, 5, 10, 20 µM) for 48 hours, after which cell viability was assessed using an MTT assay. The data were plotted as percent viability relative to untreated controls. The investigators also measured the phosphorylation status of downstream signaling proteins at each drug concentration to confirm target engagement. The study aimed to determine the IC₅₀ of the inhibitor and to assess whether a dose‑dependent reduction in viability correlated with decreased phosphorylation of the target kinase.

## bp1213_q1 Which drug concentration yields a cell viability closest to 50%?
Correct: B
The table shows that 5 µM results in 60% viability, which is the closest to 50% among the listed concentrations. 1 µM gives 85%, 10 µM gives 35%, and 20 µM gives 15%.

## bp1213_q2 Which experimental design would best test whether the observed reduction in viability is specifically due to inhibition of the target kinase?
Correct: C
Overexpressing the target kinase can rescue cells from the inhibitor’s effect if the drug’s action is specific. Options A and B test specificity but do not directly confirm target engagement. Option D would test cell‑line specificity, not target specificity.

## bp1213_q3 Estimate the IC₅₀ (concentration that reduces viability by 50%) using linear interpolation between the 5 µM and 10 µM data points.
Correct: B
Linear interpolation: 50% lies 10% below 60% (5 µM) and 15% above 35% (10 µM). The slope is (35-60)/(10-5)=‑5 % per µM. To drop 10 % from 60 % to 50 % requires 2 µM, giving 5 µM + 2 µM = 7 µM. The exact calculation yields 7.5 µM, matching option B.

## bp1213_q4 Which of the following statements is a common trap when interpreting the MTT assay results in this context?
Correct: B
The MTT assay measures mitochondrial metabolic activity, which can be altered by many factors besides cell number. Option B is a common trap. Option A conflates viability with apoptosis, C over‑attributes specificity, and D ignores cell‑line variability.

## bp1213_q5 Which of the following best links the observed dose‑response curve to the underlying mechanism of action of the kinase inhibitor?
Correct: D
A correlation between reduced phosphorylation of the target kinase and decreased cell viability indicates that the drug’s effect on cell survival is mediated through its intended target, linking pharmacodynamics to the dose‑response data.
