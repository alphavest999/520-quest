Baseline measurements of systolic (SBP) and diastolic blood pressure (DBP) were taken using an automated cuff after a 5‑minute rest period. Following the 12‑week intervention, participants returned for a final assessment. Table 1 summarizes the mean SBP and DBP values for each group at baseline and after treatment. The Hypotensol group demonstrated a mean SBP reduction of 12 mmHg and a DBP decrease of 6 mmHg, whereas the placebo group showed negligible changes. The study concluded that Hypotensol significantly lowers both SBP and DBP compared to placebo, suggesting its potential as a first‑line therapy for mild hypertension.
