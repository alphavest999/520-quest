# BP921

Researchers at the University of Biochem performed a titration study on a panel of amino acids to characterize their side‑chain ionization behavior. In a 25 °C aqueous solution, they added standardized NaOH to each amino acid solution while monitoring pH with a calibrated pH electrode. The titration curves revealed distinct inflection points corresponding to the side‑chain pKa values. Glycine, lacking a functional side chain, showed only the alpha‑carboxyl (pKa ≈ 2.34) and alpha‑amino (pKa ≈ 9.60) groups. Aspartic acid displayed a side‑chain pKa of 3.65, lysine 10.53, histidine 6.04, cysteine 8.18, and tryptophan 16.5. The researchers calculated the isoelectric points (pI) by averaging the pKa values of the groups that change charge at the pI. The data were compiled into a concise table for comparison and used to design further experiments on protein folding and enzyme activity at varying pH levels.

| Amino Acid | Side‑chain pKa | pI |
|------------|----------------|----|
| Aspartic Acid | 3.65 | 2.77 |
| Lysine | 10.53 | 9.74 |
| Histidine | 6.04 | 7.59 |
| Cysteine | 8.18 | 8.18 |
| Tryptophan | 16.5 | 9.28 |
