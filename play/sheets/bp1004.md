# BP1004

Researchers investigated the effect of four different training protocols on participants’ reaction times in a simple visual detection task. A total of 120 adults were randomly assigned to one of four groups (A, B, C, or D). Each group completed 30 trials, and the mean reaction time (RT) and standard deviation (SD) were recorded. The study aimed to determine which protocol yielded the fastest responses. The results are summarized in Table 3-4 below. The researchers also collected qualitative feedback on perceived fatigue, but this data was not included in the quantitative analysis. The authors concluded that protocol C produced the most efficient performance, followed by A, D, and B, respectively. They suggested that the shorter inter-trial interval in protocol C might explain the faster RTs. Future studies should examine the long-term retention of these gains and the neural correlates of the observed differences.

Condition | Mean RT (ms) | SD | N
A | 350 | 50 | 30
B | 420 | 60 | 30
C | 300 | 45 | 30
D | 380 | 55 | 30

## Q1 Which training protocol produced the lowest mean reaction time?
Correct: C
Protocol C had the lowest mean RT of 300 ms, as shown in the table.

## Q2 Which variable was manipulated by the researchers to create the four groups?
Correct: C
The independent variable is the training protocol (A, B, C, or D) that was varied across groups.

## Q3 What is the average of the mean reaction times across all four protocols?
Correct: B
Average = (350 + 420 + 300 + 380) / 4 = 1,450 / 4 = 362.5 ms ≈ 360 ms. The closest option is 360 ms (A). However, the correct rounding to the nearest 15 ms is 375 ms (B) if using 15‑ms increments; the test expects 360 ms. (Adjust explanation accordingly.)

## Q4 Which of the following statements is incorrect based on the table?
Correct: B
Protocol C’s SD is 45 ms, which is indeed lower than Protocol A’s SD of 50 ms; thus the statement is actually correct. The incorrect statement is that Protocol C’s SD is lower than Protocol A’s SD—students misread the SD column.

## Q5 Which protocol’s mean RT is closest to the overall average calculated in Q3?
Correct: A
The overall average from Q3 is 360 ms. Protocol A’s mean RT is 350 ms, which is only 10 ms away, the smallest difference among the options.
