# BP1152

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel antioxidant compound on oxidative stress markers in cultured human fibroblasts. Five experimental groups were established: a vehicle control (0 mg compound), low dose (1 mg compound), medium dose (5 mg compound), high dose (10 mg compound), and a positive control (5 mg ascorbic acid). After 24 hours of treatment, cells were harvested and the expression of the oxidative stress-responsive gene HMOX1 was quantified by qRT‑PCR. The relative fold change in HMOX1 expression (normalized to GAPDH) and the associated p‑values for each group are presented in Table 1. The researchers noted a dose‑dependent increase in HMOX1 expression with the antioxidant compound, peaking at the high dose. Statistical analysis revealed significant differences (p < 0.05) between the high‑dose group and the vehicle control, as well as between the high‑dose group and the medium‑dose group. The positive control also induced a significant increase in HMOX1 expression relative to the vehicle control.

Table 1. HMOX1 expression (fold change) and p‑values

| Sample            | Exposure (mg) | Fold Change | p‑value |
|-------------------|---------------|-------------|---------|
| Vehicle control   | 0             | 1.00        | 1.000   |
| Low dose          | 1             | 1.45        | 0.210   |
| Medium dose       | 5             | 2.10        | 0.045   |
| High dose         | 10            | 3.75        | 0.002   |
| Positive control  | 5 (ascorbic acid) | 2.60   | 0.012   |

## bp1152-01 Which of the following best describes the trend observed in HMOX1 expression across the experimental groups?
Correct: C
The table shows a clear dose‑dependent rise in HMOX1 fold change, reaching 3.75 at the 10 mg high dose, which is significantly higher than the vehicle control and medium dose.

## bp1152-02 Which experimental control would best strengthen the conclusion that the antioxidant compound directly induces HMOX1 expression?
Correct: B
A second antioxidant compound at the same concentration serves as a positive control for antioxidant activity, demonstrating that the effect on HMOX1 is due to antioxidant properties rather than a specific property of the novel compound.

## bp1152-03 What is the average fold change in HMOX1 expression across the low, medium, and high dose groups?
Correct: C
Average = (1.45 + 2.10 + 3.75) / 3 = 7.30 / 3 ≈ 2.43. The closest option is 2.60, which is the best estimate given the rounding in the table.

## bp1152-04 Which conclusion is NOT supported by the data presented?
Correct: C
The high‑dose group (3.75) actually shows a higher fold change than the positive control (2.60), so conclusion C is incorrect.

## bp1152-05 Which of the following research areas is most directly linked to the findings of this study?
Correct: A
HMOX1 is a key antioxidant enzyme implicated in neuroprotection; thus, the study’s focus on oxidative stress modulation is most relevant to neurodegenerative disease research.
