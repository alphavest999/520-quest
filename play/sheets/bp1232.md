# BP1232

Researchers investigated the impact of a novel probiotic strain, L. fermentum X, on the gut microbiome composition and fecal pH levels in a controlled mouse model. Twenty-four male C57BL/6 mice were randomly assigned to three groups: Control (no probiotic), Placebo (vehicle only), and Treatment (daily oral gavage of 1×10^9 CFU L. fermentum X). Over a 4‑week period, fecal samples were collected weekly and analyzed for pH and microbial diversity via 16S rRNA sequencing. The probiotic group exhibited a statistically significant reduction in fecal pH from an average of 7.2 to 6.5, alongside an increase in beneficial Bacteroidetes relative abundance. No adverse effects were observed. The study suggests that L. fermentum X may modulate gut acidity and microbial balance, potentially influencing host metabolic pathways.
