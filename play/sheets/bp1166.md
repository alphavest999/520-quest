# BP1166

Researchers studied the impact of sleep deprivation on short‑term memory recall. Twenty‑eight healthy adults were randomly assigned to one of three groups: (1) Control (normal sleep, 8 h/night), (2) 4‑hour sleep (restricted to 4 h/night for 3 days), and (3) 8‑hour sleep (restricted to 8 h/night for 3 days). After the sleep period, participants completed a standardized word‑list recall test. The mean recall scores and standard deviations were recorded. The study aimed to determine whether reduced sleep duration impairs memory performance and whether partial sleep restriction (8 h) mitigates the effect compared to severe restriction (4 h).

## bp1166-02 Which of the following is a potential confounding variable in this study?
Correct: B
Caffeine intake can affect alertness and memory performance, making it a potential confounder. Random assignment, standardized testing, and controlled sleep duration help reduce confounding.

Correct: A
Control mean = 85, 4‑hour sleep mean = 68. Difference = 85 – 68 = 17 points.
