# BP1736

Researchers at the University of Midvale conducted a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypertol, in adults with stage 1 hypertension. Eighty participants were enrolled and randomly assigned to receive either Hypertol (n = 40) or placebo (n = 40) for 8 weeks. Systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured at baseline, week 4, and week 8. The investigators hypothesized that Hypertol would produce a greater reduction in SBP compared to placebo. In addition to the primary outcome, the study recorded adverse events and adherence rates. The authors reported that at week 8, the mean SBP in the Hypertol group had decreased by 18 mm Hg, whereas the placebo group had a 5 mm Hg reduction. The study concluded that Hypertol was superior to placebo in lowering SBP, with a favorable safety profile.

## bp1736-02 Which of the following best identifies a limitation of the study’s experimental design?
Correct: C
An 8‑week duration may be insufficient to assess long‑term efficacy and safety of an antihypertensive; longer follow‑up is often necessary.
