# BP1068

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypertensol, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants who were assigned to one of three groups: (1) Hypertensol 50 mg daily, (2) Hypertensol 100 mg daily, or (3) placebo. After 12 weeks of treatment, mean SBP and standard deviation (SD) were recorded. The study aimed to evaluate dose‑response relationships and compare the drug’s efficacy to placebo. The investigators also collected data on adverse events and medication adherence. The results are summarized in Table 1 below. The authors concluded that Hypertensol produced a dose‑dependent reduction in SBP, with the 100 mg dose achieving a mean reduction of 12 mm Hg compared to placebo.

## bp1068-01 Which of the following statements best describes the data presented in Table 1?
Correct: C
The 100 mg group’s mean SBP (126 mm Hg) is 24 mm Hg lower than placebo (150 mm Hg), indicating the greatest reduction. The 50 mg group is 12 mm Hg lower than placebo, so the 100 mg dose shows a dose‑response effect.

## bp1068-02 If the investigators wanted to test whether the 100 mg dose of Hypertensol is superior to the 50 mg dose, which study design element is most critical?
Correct: C
Blinding prevents bias in outcome assessment and ensures that observed differences are due to the intervention rather than expectations. While a crossover design (B) could be useful, blinding is the most critical element for comparing two active doses.

## bp1068-03 Assuming the standard deviations are equal, calculate the approximate 95 % confidence interval for the mean difference between the 100 mg and placebo groups.
Correct: A
Mean difference = 126 – 150 = –24 mm Hg. Standard error ≈ SD/√n = 10/√40 ≈ 1.58. 95 % CI = –24 ± 1.96×1.58 ≈ (–26.9, –21.1). Rounded to the nearest whole number gives (–27, –21), which is closest to option A when accounting for rounding in the table’s SDs.

## bp1068-04 Which of the following statements could be a potential trap when interpreting the study’s findings?
Correct: C
While the 100 mg dose lowered SBP by 24 mm Hg, the clinical significance depends on patient risk factors and guidelines. Without context, assuming clinical importance is a trap.

## bp1068-05 Which broader pharmacological concept is most directly related to the dose‑response relationship observed in this study?
Correct: B
The study shows that higher doses of Hypertensol lead to greater reductions in SBP, reflecting increased receptor occupancy at higher concentrations.
