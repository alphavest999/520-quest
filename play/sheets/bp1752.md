# BP1752

Researchers studied the effect of a novel compound, X‑7, on the proliferation of human colorectal carcinoma cells (HCT116). In a controlled experiment, cells were seeded at 5×10^4 cells/well in 96‑well plates and treated with varying concentrations of X‑7 (0, 0.5, 1.0, 2.0, 4.0 μM) for 48 hours. Cell viability was assessed using the MTT assay, and absorbance at 570 nm was recorded. The resulting data were plotted as a dose‑response curve, from which the IC50 value was estimated. Additionally, a parallel set of wells was treated with X‑7 plus the antioxidant N‑acetylcysteine (NAC) to evaluate whether reactive oxygen species mediated the cytotoxic effect. Morphological changes were observed under phase‑contrast microscopy, and apoptosis was quantified by annexin V/propidium iodide staining followed by flow cytometry. The study aimed to determine whether X‑7 induces apoptosis via oxidative stress and to identify the concentration that achieves 50% inhibition of cell viability.

## bp1752_q3 Using the absorbance data, estimate the IC50 concentration of X‑7.
Correct: B
Interpolation between 1.0 μM (0.590) and 2.0 μM (0.420) gives ≈1.97 μM for 50% inhibition.

## bp1752_q4 Which of the following is a potential confounding factor in this experiment?
Correct: C

Correct: B
A caspase‑3 activity assay directly measures the enzymatic activity responsible for apoptosis.
