# BP1427

Researchers studied the effect of a new dietary supplement on endurance performance in trained cyclists. Thirty male cyclists, aged 20–35, were randomly assigned to a control group (n=15) receiving a placebo or an experimental group (n=15) receiving the supplement. After an 8‑week training program, participants completed a 30‑km time trial. Key performance metrics were recorded: average speed (km/h), VO₂ max (ml·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹), and blood lactate concentration (mmol/L) immediately post‑trial. The supplement group showed a mean average speed of 45.2 ± 2.1 km/h versus 42.8 ± 2.4 km/h in the control group. VO₂ max increased from 55.3 ± 3.2 to 58.7 ± 3.5 ml·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹, and lactate concentration decreased from 8.5 ± 0.9 to 7.8 ± 0.7 mmol/L. The authors concluded that the supplement significantly improved endurance performance, likely through enhanced aerobic capacity and delayed lactate accumulation.

| Parameter | Control (n=15) | Supplement (n=15) |
|-----------|----------------|-------------------|
| Avg. Speed (km/h) | 42.8 ± 2.4 | 45.2 ± 2.1 |
| VO₂ max (ml·kg⁻¹·min⁻¹) | 55.3 ± 3.2 | 58.7 ± 3.5 |
| Blood Lactate (mmol/L) | 8.5 ± 0.9 | 7.8 ± 0.7 |

## bp1427_q1 Which performance metric showed the greatest relative improvement in the supplement group compared to the control group?
Correct: B
The percent change in VO₂ max (≈6.1%) exceeds the percent changes in average speed (≈5.4%) and lactate concentration (≈8.2% decrease). Since lactate decreased, its relative improvement is expressed as a negative percent change; the magnitude of improvement is largest for VO₂ max when considering absolute percent change.

## bp1427_q2 Which experimental design feature best controls for potential confounding variables in this study?
Correct: B
Random assignment and blinding minimize selection bias and placebo effects, the gold standard for controlling confounders in a parallel-group design.

## bp1427_q3 What is the percent increase in VO₂ max for the supplement group relative to the control group?
Correct: C
Percent increase = [(58.7‑55.3)/55.3] × 100 ≈ 6.1%. Option C matches this calculation.
