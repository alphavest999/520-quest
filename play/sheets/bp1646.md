# BP1646

Researchers studied the effect of caffeine on reaction time in a controlled laboratory setting. A total of 120 healthy adults aged 18–35 were randomly assigned to one of four groups: (1) placebo, (2) low-dose caffeine (50 mg), (3) medium-dose caffeine (100 mg), and (4) high-dose caffeine (200 mg). Each participant performed a simple visual-motor task that required pressing a button as soon as a light appeared on a screen. Reaction times were recorded in milliseconds. The mean reaction times and standard deviations for each group were as follows: Placebo: 350 ms (± 30 ms); Low-dose: 330 ms (± 28 ms); Medium-dose: 310 ms (± 25 ms); High-dose: 305 ms (± 27 ms). The researchers noted that reaction time decreased with increasing caffeine dose up to 200 mg, after which the improvement plateaued. They concluded that moderate caffeine intake improves cognitive performance but that higher doses offer diminishing returns.

## bp1646_q1 Which of the following best describes the trend observed in reaction times across the caffeine dose groups?
Correct: C
The data show a decrease in mean reaction time from placebo to 100 mg, after which the reduction is minimal (from 310 ms to 305 ms), indicating a plateau.

## bp1646_q2 If the researchers wanted to determine whether the plateau at 200 mg was due to a ceiling effect or to the pharmacokinetics of caffeine, which experimental design modification would be most appropriate?
Correct: B
A crossover design controls for inter-individual variability and allows each participant to serve as their own control, clarifying whether the plateau is due to individual differences or pharmacokinetics.

## bp1646_q3 Assuming the standard deviation remains constant, what is the approximate 95% confidence interval for the mean reaction time of the high-dose group?
Correct: B
With n=30, SE = SD/√n = 27/√30 ≈ 4.93 ms. 95% CI ≈ mean ± 2*SE ≈ 305 ± 9.86 ms, rounded to ±9 ms.

## bp1646_q4 Which of the following statements could be a potential confounding factor in this study?
Correct: C
Baseline caffeine tolerance can influence reaction time, making habitual consumption a confounder if not controlled.

## bp1646_q5 Which of the following research topics would most directly build upon the findings of this study?
Correct: A
The study measured cognitive performance (reaction time); extending to other cognitive domains like memory would directly build on the observed caffeine effects.
