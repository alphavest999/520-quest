# BP1243

Researchers studied the effect of a novel dietary supplement on the lipid profile of adults with borderline hyperlipidemia. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants aged 30‑55 years. Participants were assigned to either the supplement (n=60) or placebo (n=60) groups and instructed to maintain their usual diet and exercise habits. Baseline measurements of total cholesterol (TC), low‑density lipoprotein cholesterol (LDL‑C), high‑density lipoprotein cholesterol (HDL‑C), and triglycerides (TG) were recorded. After 12 weeks, the supplement group showed a mean TC reduction of 15 mg/dL (p<0.01) and an LDL‑C reduction of 12 mg/dL (p<0.05). HDL‑C increased by 4 mg/dL (p=0.08) and TG decreased by 18 mg/dL (p<0.01). No serious adverse events were reported. The authors concluded that the supplement modestly improves lipid profiles in this population, though larger studies are needed to confirm long‑term benefits and safety.

## bp1243_q1 Which of the following best describes the primary outcome of the study?
Correct: C
The study reported statistically significant reductions in TC, LDL‑C, and TG, and a non‑significant increase in HDL‑C. Thus, all measured lipid parameters improved, making option C correct.

## bp1243_q2 If you were to design a follow‑up study to assess long‑term safety of the supplement, which of the following designs would be most appropriate?
Correct: B
A prospective cohort study allows observation of long‑term safety outcomes over several years, capturing incidence of adverse events in a real‑world setting. The other designs either lack temporal depth (A, D) or are retrospective (C).

## bp1243_q3 Assuming the supplement group’s TC reduction is normally distributed with a standard deviation of 20 mg/dL, what is the 95% confidence interval for the mean TC reduction?
Correct: A
Standard error = SD/√n = 20/√60 ≈ 2.58 mg/dL. 95% CI = mean ± 1.96*SE = -15 ± 1.96*2.58 ≈ -15 ± 5.06 → (-20.06, -9.94). The closest option is A (-18 to -12 mg/dL).

## bp1243_q4 Which of the following statements is a logical trap based on the study’s findings?
Correct: A
While the supplement improved all measured lipids, the improvement in HDL‑C was not statistically significant, so claiming effectiveness for all parameters is a trap.

## bp1243_q5 Which of the following resources would best help you apply the study’s findings to clinical practice?
Correct: A
A meta‑analysis synthesizes evidence from multiple studies, providing a broader context for clinical decision‑making. The other options are either too specific (B, C) or not directly related to supplement evidence (D).
