# BP1506

Researchers investigated the acute effects of a novel antihypertensive compound, Hypertensol, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in 120 adults with stage‑1 hypertension. Participants were randomized into three groups: Placebo (n = 40), Low‑dose Hypertensol (5 mg, n = 40), and High‑dose Hypertensol (10 mg, n = 40). Blood pressure was measured at baseline and after 30 minutes of drug administration. Table 1 summarizes the mean SBP reduction and standard deviation for each group. The study aimed to determine whether dose‑dependent efficacy could be observed within the first half‑hour of treatment and to assess the safety profile by monitoring adverse events. The investigators hypothesized that the high‑dose group would exhibit the greatest mean reduction and that the safety profile would remain acceptable across doses. The findings were intended to inform the design of a larger, longer‑term trial to evaluate sustained blood‑pressure control and potential side‑effects.
