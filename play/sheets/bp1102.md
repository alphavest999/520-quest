# BP1102

Researchers studied the effect of caffeine on working memory performance in adults. Sixty healthy volunteers were randomly assigned to one of three groups: placebo, low‑dose caffeine (100 mg), or high‑dose caffeine (200 mg). Each participant completed a digit‑span task before and after ingestion of the assigned beverage. The digit‑span scores were recorded and the change in score (post‑pre) was calculated. Table 1 presents the mean change in digit‑span scores and the standard deviation for each group. The results suggest that caffeine improves working memory, but the effect may plateau at higher doses. The study also recorded subjective alertness ratings, but these were not included in the table. The researchers hypothesize that the optimal dose lies between 100 and 200 mg, and that individual differences in caffeine metabolism may explain the variability observed.

Table 1: Mean Change in Digit‑Span Scores (digits)
Group | Mean Change | SD
Placebo | 0.5 | 0.3
Low‑dose caffeine | 1.2 | 0.4
High‑dose caffeine | 1.0 | 0.5

## bp1102_q1 Which group showed the largest mean improvement in digit‑span scores?
Correct: B
Table 1 shows the mean change in digit‑span scores: placebo 0.5, low‑dose 1.2, high‑dose 1.0. The low‑dose group had the greatest mean improvement.

## bp1102_q2 Which of the following best describes the study’s design?
Correct: A
Participants were randomly assigned to caffeine or placebo groups, which is the hallmark of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1102_q3 What is the approximate 95% confidence interval for the mean change in the low‑dose caffeine group, assuming a normal distribution and using SD = 0.4?
Correct: B
With n = 20 per group, SE = 0.4/√20 ≈ 0.089. 95% CI = 1.2 ± 1.96×0.089 ≈ (1.025, 1.375), which rounds to 1.0–1.4.

## bp1102_q4 Which of the following is a potential confounder that was NOT controlled for in this study?
Correct: C
The study did not report measuring or controlling for participants’ sleep quality, which could influence working‑memory performance.

## bp1102_q5 Which of the following best links the study’s findings to broader literature?
Correct: B
The observed improvement with low‑dose caffeine and a plateau at higher doses aligns with meta‑analytic evidence that moderate caffeine doses enhance working memory but higher doses yield diminishing returns.
