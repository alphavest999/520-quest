# BP1291

Researchers conducted a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of the novel antihypertensive agent, Hypotensol, in adults with stage 1 hypertension. Eighty participants (mean age 52 ± 8 years; 45% female) were randomly assigned to receive either Hypotensol (n=40) or matching placebo (n=40) once daily for 8 weeks. Systolic and diastolic blood pressure (SBP/DBP) were measured at baseline and at week 8 using an automated cuff. Baseline SBP was 152.4 mmHg (SD 10.2) for the Hypotensol group and 150.1 mmHg for the placebo group. Table 1 presents the mean ± SD values for each group. The Hypotensol group showed a mean SBP reduction of 12.4 mmHg (SD 4.7) and a DBP reduction of 8.1 mmHg (SD 3.9). The placebo group had a mean SBP reduction of 4.2 mmHg (SD 5.1) and a DBP reduction of 2.3 mmHg (SD 4.2). The differences between groups were statistically significant (p<0.01). The investigators concluded that Hypotensol effectively lowers both systolic and diastolic pressures compared to placebo.

## bp1291_q1 Which group experienced the larger mean reduction in systolic blood pressure?
Correct: A
The Hypotensol group had a mean SBP reduction of 12.4 mmHg, whereas the placebo group’s reduction was only 4.2 mmHg, indicating a larger effect in the treatment group.

## bp1291_q2 Which experimental design best controls for placebo effect in this study?
Correct: A
Randomization, double‑blinding, and a placebo control eliminate bias and placebo effects, making this the gold‑standard design for drug efficacy trials.

## bp1291_q3 What is the percent reduction in systolic blood pressure for the Hypotensol group?
Correct: B
Percent reduction = (12.4 mmHg ÷ 152.4 mmHg) × 100 ≈ 8.1%. The other options represent incorrect calculations.

## bp1291_q4 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data?
Correct: B
The data show the placebo group’s DBP reduction was only 2.3 mmHg, whereas Hypotensol’s was 8.1 mmHg, so the placebo did not reduce DBP more.

## bp1291_q5 Which physiological mechanism is most likely responsible for Hypotensol’s effect?
Correct: B
Hypotensol is designed as a renin inhibitor, reducing angiotensin‑II production and thereby lowering blood pressure. Vasoconstriction and increased sympathetic tone would raise BP, and sodium retention would also increase BP.
