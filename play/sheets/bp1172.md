# BP1172

Researchers investigated the effects of a novel dietary supplement on blood pressure in adults with prehypertension. The study was a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial conducted over 12 weeks. Participants (n = 120) were assigned to one of three groups: placebo (n = 40), low‑dose supplement (n = 40), and high‑dose supplement (n = 40). Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) averaged 130 mm Hg across all groups. At week 12, the mean SBP reductions were 2.5 mm Hg for placebo, 8.3 mm Hg for low‑dose, and 12.7 mm Hg for high‑dose. The study also measured diastolic blood pressure (DBP) and recorded adverse events. Table 3 below summarizes the key outcomes. The authors concluded that the high‑dose supplement significantly lowered SBP and DBP compared to placebo, with a favorable safety profile.

High‑dose | 117.3 | 72.4 | 40 | 3.7 | 3.2
