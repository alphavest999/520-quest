# BP1655

Researchers studied the effect of a novel anti-inflammatory compound on serum interleukin‑6 (IL‑6) levels in a murine model of acute lung injury. Male C57BL/6 mice (n = 48) were randomly assigned to four groups: (1) vehicle control, (2) low‑dose compound (10 mg/kg), (3) high‑dose compound (30 mg/kg), and (4) positive control (dexamethasone, 2 mg/kg). All treatments were administered intraperitoneally 1 hour before intratracheal instillation of lipopolysaccharide (LPS, 5 mg/kg) to induce lung injury. Serum samples were collected 24 hours post‑LPS and IL‑6 concentrations were quantified by ELISA. The study aimed to determine whether the compound could attenuate the inflammatory cytokine surge induced by LPS and to compare its efficacy with dexamethasone. Data were analyzed using one‑way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post‑hoc test. The results suggested a dose‑dependent reduction in IL‑6, with the high‑dose group approaching the levels seen in the dexamethasone group. The investigators concluded that the compound has promising anti‑inflammatory properties that warrant further investigation in chronic models.

## bp1655-001 Which of the following best describes the trend in IL‑6 levels across the experimental groups?
Correct: B
The data show a clear dose‑dependent decrease in IL‑6: 520.4 (control) → 380.7 (low dose) → 210.3 (high dose) → 190.8 (dexamethasone). The high‑dose compound brings IL‑6 close to the dexamethasone level, indicating effective attenuation of the inflammatory response.

## bp1655-002 Which experimental design element is most critical for attributing changes in IL‑6 levels to the compound?
Correct: A
Random assignment minimizes selection bias and ensures that differences in IL‑6 levels are due to the treatment rather than pre‑existing group differences. While administration route, mouse strain, and timing are important, they do not by themselves isolate the effect of the compound.

## bp1655-003 If the researchers wanted to calculate the percent reduction in IL‑6 for the high‑dose compound relative to the vehicle control, what would that value be?
Correct: A
Percent reduction = ((520.4 – 210.3) / 520.4) × 100 ≈ 59.5%. This calculation uses the mean IL‑6 levels from the table.

Correct: A
Measuring IL‑6 at only one time point may miss peak or trough cytokine dynamics, limiting the ability to fully characterize the inflammatory response. The other options are strengths or standard practices that do not inherently threaten validity.

## bp1655-005 Which of the following best links the study’s findings to a broader clinical implication?
Correct: A
The dose‑dependent attenuation of IL‑6 suggests that the compound could modulate systemic inflammation, a key feature of many chronic inflammatory conditions. The other options overstate or misinterpret the study’s scope.
