# BP1147

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel inhibitor on the enzymatic activity of human lactate dehydrogenase (LDH). In a controlled in vitro assay, they incubated the enzyme with varying concentrations of the inhibitor (0, 5, 10, 20, and 40 µM) and measured the conversion of pyruvate to lactate by monitoring absorbance at 340 nm over 10 minutes. The assay was performed in triplicate at 37 °C, and the data were averaged to determine the percentage of residual activity relative to a no‑inhibitor control. The results suggested a dose‑dependent decrease in activity, with 40 µM reducing activity to 15% of control. The investigators proposed that the inhibitor binds competitively to the NADH-binding site, based on the observed linear relationship between inhibitor concentration and velocity reduction. They also noted that the inhibitor’s potency could be influenced by pH and ionic strength, warranting further kinetic analysis.

## bp1147-03 If the residual activity at 20 µM is 32%, what is the percent inhibition at this concentration?
Correct: B
Percent inhibition = 100% – residual activity = 100% – 32% = 68%.
