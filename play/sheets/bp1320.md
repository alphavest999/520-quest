# BP1320

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antioxidant compound, Antiox-1, on oxidative stress markers in cultured human hepatocytes. Cells were treated with 0, 5, 10, and 20 μM Antiox-1 for 24 hours. After treatment, intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS) levels were measured using the DCFH‑DA fluorescence assay, and the expression of the antioxidant enzyme superoxide dismutase 2 (SOD2) was quantified by qRT‑PCR. The results are summarized in Table 1. The data indicate a dose‑dependent reduction in ROS and a corresponding increase in SOD2 mRNA levels. The authors suggest that Antiox-1 may upregulate endogenous antioxidant defenses, thereby protecting hepatocytes from oxidative damage.

Table 1. Effect of Antiox-1 on ROS and SOD2 expression in hepatocytes

| Antiox-1 (μM) | Mean ROS (RFU) | SD (RFU) | Mean SOD2 (ΔCt) | SD (ΔCt) |
|--------------|----------------|----------|-----------------|----------|
| 0            | 1000           | 50       | 1.2             | 0.1      |
| 5            | 800            | 45       | 1.8             | 0.12     |
| 10           | 600            | 40       | 2.4             | 0.15     |
| 20           | 400            | 35       | 3.0             | 0.18     |

## bp1320_q1 Which of the following best describes the trend observed in ROS levels across increasing concentrations of Antiox-1?
Correct: B
The mean ROS values decline from 1000 to 400 as Antiox-1 concentration rises, indicating a dose‑dependent reduction.

## bp1320_q2 Which of the following experimental controls would best strengthen the conclusion that Antiox-1 directly reduces ROS levels?
Correct: A
A known ROS scavenger provides a benchmark for ROS reduction, confirming that Antiox‑1’s effect is comparable to a direct ROS‑scavenging agent.

## bp1320_q3 Assuming a linear relationship between Antiox-1 concentration and ROS reduction, estimate the percent reduction in ROS at 15 μM.
Correct: B
Linear interpolation between 10 μM (600 RFU) and 20 μM (400 RFU) gives 500 RFU at 15 μM. Reduction = (1000‑500)/1000 = 50%.

## bp1320_q4 Which of the following statements would incorrectly interpret the data?
Correct: C
The data show upregulation of SOD2, suggesting an indirect effect on ROS. Direct scavenging is not demonstrated.

## bp1320_q5 Which of the following statements best links the observed data to the proposed mechanism of action?
Correct: A
The dose‑dependent rise in SOD2 mRNA correlates with the fall in ROS, supporting the mechanism that Antiox‑1 enhances endogenous antioxidant defenses.
