# BP1094

Researchers studied the effect of a novel cognitive training program on working memory performance in adults aged 25–35. Participants were randomly assigned to either the training group (n = 60) or a control group receiving no training (n = 60). Over a 6‑week period, both groups completed a standardized working memory task before and after the intervention. The training group’s mean reaction time (RT) decreased from 650 ms (SD = 45) to 520 ms (SD = 38), while the control group’s mean RT changed from 652 ms (SD = 47) to 640 ms (SD = 42). Accuracy improved in the training group from 78% (SD = 5) to 88% (SD = 4), but remained unchanged in the control group (79% to 80%). The researchers concluded that the training program significantly enhanced working memory efficiency. However, they noted that the sample consisted solely of university students, limiting generalizability. Future studies should include a more diverse age range and assess long‑term retention of the training benefits.

Training | 650 | 45 | 520 | 38 | 60
Control | 652 | 47 | 640 | 42 | 60

## bp1094-001 Based on the data presented, which of the following statements best reflects the observed changes in reaction time for the training group?
Correct: B
The training group’s mean RT dropped from 650 ms to 520 ms, a decrease of 130 ms.

## bp1094-002 If the researchers wanted to determine whether the training effect was due to the specific content of the program rather than general engagement, which experimental design would best address this question?
Correct: B
A second control group receiving a different activity controls for general engagement and isolates the specific effect of the training content.

## bp1094-003 Assuming equal variance, calculate the approximate 95% confidence interval for the difference in mean reaction time between the training and control groups post‑test.
Correct: C
Mean difference = 520 - 640 = -120 ms. Using SD ≈ 40 and n = 60, SE ≈ 5.1 ms. 95% CI ≈ -120 ± 1.96*5.1 ≈ [-130, -110] ms, closest to option C.

## bp1094-004 Which of the following is a potential flaw in the study’s conclusion that the training program significantly enhanced working memory efficiency?
Correct: A
The homogeneous sample limits generalizability; other demographics may respond differently.
