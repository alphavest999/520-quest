# BP1179

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel dietary supplement on insulin sensitivity in adults with prediabetes. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants (age 35–65) who were assigned to receive either the supplement (n = 60) or placebo (n = 60) for 12 weeks. Primary outcome was the change in the Homeostatic Model Assessment of Insulin Resistance (HOMA‑IR) from baseline to week 12. Secondary outcomes included fasting glucose, HbA1c, and lipid profile. Baseline characteristics were comparable between groups. At week 12, the supplement group exhibited a mean reduction in HOMA‑IR of 1.2 (SD 0.4) versus 0.3 (SD 0.5) in the placebo group (p < 0.001). Fasting glucose decreased by 0.8 mmol/L (SD 0.2) in the supplement group compared to 0.1 mmol/L (SD 0.3) in placebo (p = 0.02). HbA1c lowered by 0.4% (SD 0.1) versus 0.1% (SD 0.2) (p = 0.03). Lipid changes were not statistically significant.

## bp1179-01 Which of the following best describes the primary outcome of the study?
Correct: C
The primary outcome was the change in HOMA‑IR, a surrogate marker of insulin resistance. The study reported a mean reduction of 1.2 in the supplement group versus 0.3 in placebo, indicating improved insulin sensitivity.

## bp1179-02 Which of the following best describes the experimental design used in this study?
Correct: C
The passage states that participants were randomly assigned to supplement or placebo, the study was double‑blind, and placebo‑controlled, which matches a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial.

## bp1179-03 Based on the reported means and standard deviations, what is the approximate 95% confidence interval for the difference in HOMA‑IR change between the supplement and placebo groups?
Correct: A
The mean difference is 1.2 – 0.3 = 0.9. Standard error ≈ sqrt((0.4²/60)+(0.5²/60)) ≈ 0.10. 95% CI ≈ 0.9 ± 1.96*0.10 = 0.9 ± 0.196 → 0.704 to 1.096. Since the supplement group had a greater reduction, the difference is negative: -1.096 to -0.704, which rounds to option A.

## bp1179-04 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the study’s results?
Correct: D
A p‑value indicates statistical significance, not causality. While the study design is robust, it does not prove causation beyond the controlled conditions.

## bp1179-05 Which of the following best links the study’s findings to a broader clinical implication?
Correct: B
The reduction in insulin resistance and fasting glucose suggests that the supplement might help prevent or delay the onset of type 2 diabetes in prediabetic individuals.
