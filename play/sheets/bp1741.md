# BP1741

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel antihypertensive drug on systolic blood pressure in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 200 participants who were assigned to one of four groups: placebo, 20 mg, 40 mg, or 80 mg of the drug, administered daily for 12 weeks. The primary outcome was mean systolic blood pressure (SBP) reduction from baseline (mean baseline SBP = 140 mmHg). Table 1 summarizes the results. The study found a dose‑dependent reduction in SBP, with the 80‑mg group showing the greatest mean decrease. Adverse events were mild and comparable across groups. The investigators concluded that the drug is effective and safe at doses up to 80 mg.

Table 1. Mean SBP Reduction by Dosage

Dosage (mg) | Mean SBP Reduction (mmHg) | SD | n
-------------------------------------------------
Placebo     | 2.1                        | 3.5 | 50
20          | 5.8                        | 4.2 | 50
40          | 9.3                        | 4.8 | 50
80          | 13.7                       | 5.1 | 50

## bp1741_q1 According to Table 1, which dosage group experienced the greatest mean systolic blood pressure reduction?
Correct: D
The table shows the mean SBP reduction for each dosage. The 80‑mg group had the highest mean reduction of 13.7 mmHg, which is greater than the reductions observed in the 20‑mg (5.8 mmHg), 40‑mg (9.3 mmHg), and placebo (2.1 mmHg) groups.

## bp1741_q2 Which of the following study designs would best establish a causal relationship between the drug dosage and systolic blood pressure reduction?
Correct: C
Randomization and blinding control for confounding variables and bias, allowing a clear assessment of the effect of drug dosage on SBP. This design is the gold standard for establishing causality.

## bp1741_q3 What is the percent reduction in systolic blood pressure for the 80‑mg group relative to the baseline mean of 140 mmHg?
Correct: A
Percent reduction = (Mean reduction ÷ Baseline) × 100 = (13.7 ÷ 140) × 100 ≈ 9.8%. Option A reflects this calculation. Option D mistakenly uses the raw reduction as a percentage.

## bp1741_q5 Which concept is most directly illustrated by the dose‑response relationship observed in this study?
Correct: C
The study shows that increasing drug dosage leads to greater systolic blood pressure reduction, which is the classic definition of a dose‑response curve. The other options describe different aspects of pharmacology or study design.
