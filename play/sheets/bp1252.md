# BP1252

Researchers studied the effect of different light wavelengths on the photosynthetic rate of the green alga *Chlorella vulgaris*. Algal cultures were grown under monochromatic light at 400 nm (violet), 500 nm (green), 600 nm (orange), and 700 nm (red). Each culture was maintained at identical temperature, CO₂ concentration, and nutrient levels for 48 hours. Photosynthetic rates were measured using an infrared gas analyzer, chlorophyll a concentrations were quantified by spectrophotometry, and growth rates were calculated from cell volume changes. The results showed that the 500 nm light produced the highest photosynthetic rate and growth, while 400 nm and 600 nm produced intermediate rates, and 700 nm yielded the lowest rates. Chlorophyll a concentrations were highest at 400 nm, suggesting a compensatory increase in pigment under suboptimal wavelengths. These findings illustrate the importance of wavelength-specific light quality in algal cultivation and provide insight into optimizing light regimes for biofuel production.

Correct: B
The table shows the photosynthetic rate at 500 nm (120 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹) is higher than at 400 nm (90), 600 nm (95), and 700 nm (70).

## bp1252_q2 Which of the following would be an appropriate control for this experiment?
Correct: B
A dark control allows measurement of baseline respiration and confirms that observed CO₂ uptake is due to photosynthesis, not other metabolic processes.

## bp1252_q3 If the photosynthetic rate at 500 nm is 120 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹, what would be the expected rate at 600 nm assuming a linear decrease of 15 µmol per 100 nm increase?
Correct: A
A 100‑nm increase from 500 to 600 nm reduces the rate by 15 µmol, so 120 – 15 = 105 µmol CO₂ m⁻² s⁻¹.

## bp1252_q5 Which concept best explains the observed pattern of photosynthetic rates across wavelengths?
Correct: B
The pattern matches the absorption spectrum of chlorophyll a, which peaks around 430–460 nm and 660–680 nm; the 500‑nm green light falls near the peak of chlorophyll’s effective absorption, yielding the highest photosynthetic rate.
