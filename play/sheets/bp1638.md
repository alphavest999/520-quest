# BP1638

Researchers investigated how different wavelengths of visible light affect the growth of the freshwater alga *Chlorella vulgaris*. Cultures were grown under monochromatic LEDs at 450 nm (blue), 520 nm (green), 590 nm (yellow), and 660 nm (red) while maintaining constant temperature (25 °C) and pH (7.2). Growth was measured as dry weight per liter after 7 days. The table below summarizes the mean growth rates (±SD) for each wavelength, along with the corresponding pH and temperature conditions used in the experiment. The data suggest a clear wavelength-dependent response, with the highest growth observed under red light.

Wavelength (nm)	Growth rate (g L⁻¹ day⁻¹)	pH	Temperature (°C)
450	8.2 ± 0.4	7.2	25
520	10.5 ± 0.5	7.2	25
590	9.7 ± 0.3	7.2	25
660	12.8 ± 0.6	7.2	25

## bp1638-01 Which wavelength produced the highest growth rate of *Chlorella vulgaris*?
Correct: D
The table shows the growth rate under each wavelength. The highest mean growth rate is 12.8 g L⁻¹ day⁻¹ at 660 nm, so red light (660 nm) produced the greatest growth.

## bp1638-02 Which of the following experimental designs would best test whether temperature influences the optimal wavelength for algal growth?
Correct: D
Option D tests both variables—temperature and wavelength—simultaneously, allowing determination of the temperature that maximizes growth for each wavelength. Options A and B only test wavelength, C only tests temperature at a single wavelength.

## bp1638-03 If a culture grown under 520 nm light shows a growth rate of 10.5 g L⁻¹ day⁻¹, what is the expected dry weight after 4 days of growth, assuming the rate remains constant?
Correct: A
Growth rate × days = 10.5 g L⁻¹ day⁻¹ × 4 days = 42.0 g L⁻¹. Option A is the correct calculation.

## bp1638-04 Which statement is most likely incorrect based on the data presented?
Correct: C
While growth rates generally increase with wavelength, the increase is not perfectly linear (e.g., 520 nm to 590 nm shows a decrease). Thus, statement C is incorrect. The other statements accurately reflect the data.

## bp1638-05 Which fundamental biological process is most directly related to the wavelength-dependent growth observed in this study?
Correct: B
Photosynthesis uses light energy, and the absorption spectrum of chlorophyll peaks in the red and blue regions. The study’s finding that red light yields the highest growth rate directly reflects the efficiency of photosynthetic light harvesting.
