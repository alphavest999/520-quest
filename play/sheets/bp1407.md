# BP1407

Researchers investigated the effect of caffeine on cognitive performance in a controlled laboratory setting. One hundred and twenty undergraduate students were randomly assigned to one of three conditions: (1) caffeine (200 mg), (2) placebo, or (3) no beverage. Each participant completed a 30‑minute computerized working‑memory task after a 15‑minute absorption period. Table 1 summarizes the mean reaction times (RTs) and standard deviations (SDs) for each group. The researchers hypothesized that caffeine would reduce RTs relative to placebo and no‑beverage controls. The study’s design allowed the investigators to isolate the pharmacological effect of caffeine while controlling for expectancy effects via the placebo condition. Data were analyzed using one‑way ANOVA followed by post‑hoc Tukey tests. The authors reported significant differences between caffeine and placebo (p < 0.01) and between caffeine and no‑beverage (p < 0.001). They concluded that a single dose of caffeine improves working‑memory performance in young adults.

Table 1. Mean Reaction Times (ms) by Condition

| Condition      | Mean RT | SD  | N |
|----------------|---------|-----|---|
| Caffeine (200 mg) |  412   |  45 | 40 |
| Placebo          |  438   |  50 | 40 |
| No Beverage      |  470   |  55 | 40 |


## bp1407_q1 Based on the data presented in Table 1, which of the following statements is most accurate?
Correct: A
The mean reaction time for the caffeine group (412 ms) is lower than that for the placebo group (438 ms), indicating faster performance. Lower RTs reflect better performance on the working‑memory task.

## bp1407_q2 Which experimental design feature best supports the conclusion that caffeine itself caused the observed performance improvement?
Correct: A
Random assignment ensures that any differences among groups are likely due to the experimental manipulation (caffeine) rather than pre‑existing differences, thereby supporting causal inference.

## bp1407_q3 What is the percent reduction in mean reaction time for the caffeine group relative to the placebo group?
Correct: A
Percent reduction = [(438 – 412)/438] × 100 = 26/438 × 100 ≈ 5.94 % ≈ 5.8 %. This calculation uses the difference in means divided by the placebo mean.

## bp1407_q4 Which of the following is a common misinterpretation of the study’s findings?
Correct: A
The sample consisted only of healthy young adults, so generalizing the benefit to all age groups would be a trap. Option A reflects that misinterpretation.

## bp1407_q5 The study’s findings are most directly linked to which broader concept in cognitive neuroscience?
Correct: B
Caffeine is an adenosine receptor antagonist that modulates neurotransmitter activity, leading to improved alertness and working‑memory performance. Thus the study exemplifies neurotransmitter modulation.
