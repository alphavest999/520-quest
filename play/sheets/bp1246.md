# BP1246

Researchers studied the effect of a novel dietary supplement on markers of oxidative stress in adults with metabolic syndrome. In a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial, 120 participants were assigned to either the supplement (n = 60) or placebo (n = 60) for 12 weeks. Blood samples were collected at baseline and at the end of the study to measure plasma malondialdehyde (MDA) levels, superoxide dismutase (SOD) activity, and total antioxidant capacity (TAC). The study aimed to determine whether the supplement could reduce oxidative damage and improve antioxidant defenses. Participants were matched for age, sex, and BMI. Compliance was monitored through pill counts and serum levels of the supplement’s active compound. The primary outcome was the change in MDA concentration; secondary outcomes included changes in SOD activity and TAC. Data were analyzed using ANCOVA, adjusting for baseline values.

## bp1246-01 Which of the following statements best reflects the primary outcome of the study?
Correct: B
The primary outcome was the change in MDA concentration, a marker of oxidative damage. The table shows a significant decrease in MDA in the supplement group (3.2 vs. 4.1 µmol/L, p<0.001), indicating reduced oxidative stress. Option B correctly states this finding.

## bp1246-02 Which experimental design feature best supports the internal validity of the study’s findings?
Correct: A
Random assignment minimizes selection bias and balances known and unknown confounders, thus providing the strongest support for internal validity. While the other features are important, randomization is the key design element that ensures the observed effects are attributable to the intervention.

## bp1246-03 Calculate the absolute mean difference in plasma MDA levels between the supplement and placebo groups.
Correct: A
Absolute mean difference = Mean_Supplement – Mean_Placebo = 3.2 – 4.1 = –0.9 µmol/L. The magnitude is 0.9 µmol/L, and the negative sign indicates a reduction in the supplement group. Option A correctly states the absolute difference.

## bp1246-04 Which of the following is a potential trap when interpreting the study’s results?
Correct: D
All listed statements represent common pitfalls: over‑interpreting statistical significance as clinical significance (A), overlooking non‑significant compliance differences (B), and attributing all secondary outcomes to the intervention without considering other factors (C). Therefore, option D is correct.

## bp1246-05 How does this study’s finding relate to the broader concept of oxidative stress management in chronic disease?
Correct: B
The study provides evidence that a supplement can reduce oxidative damage (lower MDA) and enhance antioxidant defenses (higher SOD and TAC). These findings align with the broader strategy of using antioxidant interventions as adjunctive measures to mitigate oxidative stress in chronic conditions like metabolic syndrome.
