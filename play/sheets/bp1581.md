# BP1581

Researchers studied the impact of light intensity on the growth of *Arabidopsis thaliana* seedlings over a 14‑day period. Five light regimes were tested: 50, 150, 300, 600, and 1200 lux, each maintained at 16‑hour photoperiods. Seedlings were grown in identical soil mixtures, at 22 °C, and watered daily. At the end of the experiment, the average stem height (in centimeters) was recorded for each group. The results indicated a clear trend: as light intensity increased, so did stem height, up to a plateau at the highest intensity. The study aimed to determine the optimal light level for maximal growth while minimizing energy consumption.

## bp1581_q4 Which statement is a potential trap when interpreting the data?

## bp1581_q5 Which concept is most directly linked to the observed plateau in growth at high light intensities?
Correct: A
Photoinhibition occurs when excessive light damages photosynthetic apparatus, leading to a plateau or decline in growth despite higher light intensity.
