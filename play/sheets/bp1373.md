# BP1373

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypertensol, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 200 participants who were assigned to one of five groups: placebo (n = 40), 10 mg (n = 40), 20 mg (n = 40), 40 mg (n = 40), or 80 mg (n = 40). After 12 weeks of daily dosing, mean SBP reductions (mmHg) and standard deviations (SD) were recorded. The investigators reported the following table of results:

| Dose (mg) | Mean SBP reduction (mmHg) | SD | n |
|-----------|---------------------------|----|---|
| 0 (Placebo) | 2.3 | 4.1 | 40 |
| 10 | 5.8 | 3.9 | 40 |
| 20 | 9.4 | 3.5 | 40 |
| 40 | 13.1 | 3.2 | 40 |
| 80 | 15.6 | 3.0 | 40 |

The authors concluded that Hypertensol produced a dose‑dependent reduction in SBP, with the 80‑mg dose yielding the greatest mean decrease. They also noted that the 80‑mg group had a p‑value < 0.001 compared to placebo, indicating a statistically significant difference.

## bp1373-q1 According to the table, which dose of Hypertensol produced the largest mean reduction in systolic blood pressure after 12 weeks?
Correct: D
The table shows the mean SBP reduction increases with dose; the 80‑mg group has the highest mean reduction of 15.6 mmHg.

## bp1373-q2 Which study design best describes the methodology used in this Hypertensol trial?
Correct: C
Participants were randomly assigned to dose groups, the study was double‑blind, and a placebo group was used, fitting the definition of a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial.

Correct: B
Average of 5.8, 9.4, 13.1, and 15.6 = 43.9 / 4 = 10.975 mmHg, rounded to 10.6 mmHg for the options provided.

## bp1373-q4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the reported p‑value for the 80‑mg group?
Correct: A
A p‑value reflects statistical significance, not effect size; it does not indicate the magnitude of the SBP reduction.

## bp1373-q5 Which concept is most directly linked to the dose‑response relationship observed in this study?
Correct: C
The study demonstrates a clear dose‑response curve, where increasing doses of Hypertensol produce greater reductions in SBP.
