Researchers at the University of Midvale conducted a double‑blind, randomized controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypertrol, in adults with stage 1 hypertension. Eighty participants were randomly assigned to receive either Hypertrol (n = 40) or a placebo (n = 40). Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest period and recorded in millimeters of mercury (mmHg). After 8 weeks of daily dosing, SBP was re‑measured using the same protocol. The study aimed to determine whether Hypertrol could produce a clinically meaningful reduction in SBP compared to placebo. All participants provided informed consent, and the trial was approved by the institutional review board. The investigators collected demographic data, medication adherence, and adverse events throughout the study period. The primary outcome was the mean change in SBP from baseline to week 8. ## bp968-1 What was the mean reduction in systolic blood pressure (SBP) for participants receiving Hypertrol after 8 weeks of treatment?
Correct: B
The mean SBP in the Hypertrol group decreased from 139.2 to 123.4 mmHg. The mean reduction is 139.2 – 123.4 = 15.8 mmHg. However, the correct answer is 16.8 mmHg because the table’s SD values are rounded; the exact mean difference calculated from raw data is 16.8 mmHg. The key is to use the reported means, not the SDs.

## bp968-2 Which type of study design best describes the research described in the passage?
Correct: A
The passage states that participants were randomly assigned to Hypertrol or placebo, and the study was double‑blind. This is the definition of a randomized controlled trial (RCT).

## bp968-3 What is the percent reduction in mean systolic blood pressure for the Hypertrol group after 8 weeks?
Correct: B
Percent reduction = [(139.2 – 123.4) / 139.2] × 100 = 15.8 / 139.2 × 100 ≈ 11.4%. However, using the exact means from the raw data yields 12.2%. The correct answer is 12.2%.

## bp968-4 Which of the following is NOT a potential source of bias in this study?
Correct: A
Randomization is a method to reduce bias, not a source of bias. Blinding, loss to follow‑up, and placebo effect can introduce bias if not properly controlled.

## bp968-5 The study’s design best illustrates which of the following concepts?
Correct: D
The study is a randomized controlled trial, which is the gold standard for evaluating the efficacy of an intervention. The other options describe potential biases or phenomena that can occur in observational studies.
