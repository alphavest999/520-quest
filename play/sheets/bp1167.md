# BP1167

Researchers studied the effects of a novel dietary supplement on cognitive performance in adults aged 25–45. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants who were randomly assigned to either the supplement group (n = 60) or the placebo group (n = 60). Over a 12‑week intervention period, participants completed a battery of neuropsychological tests at baseline and at the end of the study. The primary outcome was the change in the composite score of the Digit Symbol Substitution Test (DSST). Secondary outcomes included reaction time on a Stroop task and self‑reported memory complaints. The researchers hypothesized that the supplement would lead to a statistically significant improvement in DSST scores compared to placebo. Table 1 summarizes the key demographic and baseline cognitive characteristics of the two groups, as well as the mean change scores for the primary outcome.

## bp1167-01 Which of the following statements best describes the primary outcome results?
Correct: A
The table shows a mean DSST change of 8.5 for the supplement group and 4.1 for placebo. The difference is 4.4 points, indicating a greater improvement in the supplement group.

## bp1167-02 Which experimental design feature best controls for placebo effect in this study?
Correct: A
Random assignment ensures that any placebo effect is equally distributed between groups, thereby controlling for its influence on the outcome.

## bp1167-03 What is the approximate 95% confidence interval for the mean difference in DSST change between the supplement and placebo groups?
Correct: C
Mean difference = 8.5 – 4.1 = 4.4. Pooled SD ≈ √[(3.2²+3.5²)/2] ≈ 3.35. SE = 3.35/√60 ≈ 0.43. 95% CI = 4.4 ± 1.96×0.43 ≈ (3.5, 5.3). Option C (4.0–6.0) is the closest.

## bp1167-04 Which of the following statements is a potential trap for interpreting the study’s findings?
Correct: B
While double‑blindness controls for expectancy bias, it does not eliminate all biases such as selection bias or attrition bias. Option B is a common trap.
