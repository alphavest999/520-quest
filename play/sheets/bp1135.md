# BP1135

Researchers investigated the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive drug (Drug X) compared with placebo in adults with stage 1 hypertension. Eighty participants were randomized in a double‑blind fashion to receive either Drug X (n = 40) or placebo (n = 40). Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest. Participants were followed for 8 weeks, with SBP measured at week 4 and week 8. The study’s primary endpoint was the change in mean SBP from baseline to week 8. Secondary endpoints included the proportion of participants achieving SBP < 140 mmHg and the incidence of adverse events. The investigators reported that Drug X significantly lowered SBP compared with placebo, with a mean reduction of 12 mmHg versus 3 mmHg in the placebo group. Adverse events were mild and comparable between groups.
