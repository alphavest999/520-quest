# BP1433

Researchers studied the effect of a novel dietary supplement on blood pressure in adults aged 40–60. A randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled trial enrolled 120 participants who were randomly assigned to either the supplement group (n = 60) or the placebo group (n = 60). Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) and diastolic blood pressure (DBP) were recorded for all participants. After 12 weeks of daily supplementation, SBP and DBP were measured again. The study also collected data on heart rate (HR) and body mass index (BMI) to assess potential confounding variables. The researchers reported the following summary statistics in Table 1.

Variable,Group A (Supplement),Group B (Placebo)
Baseline SBP (mmHg),132.4 ± 10.2,131.8 ± 9.9
Baseline DBP (mmHg),84.6 ± 5.7,85.1 ± 5.9
Week 12 SBP (mmHg),125.1 ± 9.8,129.3 ± 10.5
Week 12 DBP (mmHg),80.2 ± 5.4,83.7 ± 5.8
HR (bpm),72.3 ± 8.1,71.9 ± 8.3
BMI (kg/m^2),27.5 ± 2.3,27.7 ± 2.4

## bp1433-01 Based on Table 1, which statement most accurately reflects the effect of the supplement on systolic blood pressure after 12 weeks?
Correct: A
The supplement group’s SBP decreased from 132.4 to 125.1 mmHg (a 7.3 mmHg drop), whereas the placebo group decreased from 131.8 to 129.3 mmHg (a 2.5 mmHg drop). The larger drop in the supplement group suggests a beneficial effect.

## bp1433-02 Which of the following experimental design elements best ensures that the observed SBP changes are attributable to the supplement?
Correct: C
Blinding minimizes bias from participants and investigators influencing outcomes. Randomization already distributes confounders; blinding is the key design element that isolates the supplement’s effect.

## bp1433-03 Calculate the mean difference in SBP change between the supplement and placebo groups.
Correct: B
Change in supplement group = 132.4 – 125.1 = 7.3 mmHg. Change in placebo group = 131.8 – 129.3 = 2.5 mmHg. Mean difference = 7.3 – 2.5 = 4.8 mmHg.

## bp1433-04 Which of the following is a common misinterpretation of the study’s findings?
Correct: A
The 7.3 mmHg drop refers to the mean change in the supplement group, not the absolute effect in every individual. Individual responses vary.

## bp1433-05 How might the findings of this trial inform clinical recommendations for managing hypertension in middle‑aged adults?
Correct: B
The trial shows a modest SBP reduction in a middle‑aged cohort; thus, it could be considered an adjunct to diet and exercise for mild‑to‑moderate hypertension, pending further safety data.
