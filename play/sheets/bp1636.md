# BP1636

Researchers studied the effects of three different nutritional supplements on cognitive performance in adults aged 30–50. Participants were randomly assigned to one of four groups: a control group receiving a placebo, and three treatment groups receiving Supplement A, Supplement B, or Supplement C. Each group consisted of 40 participants. Cognitive performance was assessed using a standardized memory test administered at baseline and after 12 weeks of supplementation. The mean scores (out of 100) and standard deviations (SD) for each group after 12 weeks were recorded. ## Q2 Which of the following best describes the design of the study?
Correct: A
Participants were randomly assigned to treatment or control groups, which is characteristic of a randomized controlled trial.

## Q3 If the overall mean score across all groups is 84.2, what is the weighted average of the mean scores?
Correct: A
All groups have equal sample sizes, so the weighted average equals the simple average of the means: (78.5+82.3+85.7+90.2)/4 = 84.2.

## Q4 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data presented?
