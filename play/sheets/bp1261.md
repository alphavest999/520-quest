# BP1261

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel antihypertensive drug on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. In a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial, 200 participants were assigned to one of four groups: placebo (n = 50), low dose (n = 50), medium dose (n = 50), or high dose (n = 50). Baseline SBP was recorded for all participants, and after 4 weeks of treatment, SBP was measured again. The investigators reported the mean baseline and week‑4 SBP values, as well as the mean change in SBP for each group. The results suggested a dose‑dependent reduction in SBP, with the high‑dose group exhibiting the greatest mean decrease. The study aimed to determine whether the drug’s efficacy increased with dose and to evaluate its safety profile over the short term.
