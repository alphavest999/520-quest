# BP1413

Researchers conducted a randomized controlled trial to evaluate the antihypertensive effect of a novel compound, Hypertol, in adults with mild to moderate hypertension. One hundred sixty participants were randomly assigned to four groups: a control group receiving no active drug, a placebo group, a low-dose Hypertol group (50 mg/day), and a high-dose Hypertol group (100 mg/day). Over a 12-week period, systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured weekly. Table 1 summarizes the mean SBP and standard deviation for each group at the study’s conclusion. The control group maintained a mean SBP of 140 mmHg (SD 10), the placebo group slightly improved to 138 mmHg (SD 9), the low-dose group reduced to 130 mmHg (SD 8), and the high-dose group achieved the greatest reduction, with a mean SBP of 120 mmHg (SD 7). These findings suggest a dose-dependent response of Hypertol, supporting its potential as an effective antihypertensive agent. The study’s design, including randomization and blinding, minimizes bias and strengthens the validity of the observed effects.

## bp1413_q1 Which group had the greatest reduction in systolic blood pressure compared to the control group?
Correct: D
The high-dose Hypertol group showed the largest mean reduction in systolic BP (120 mmHg) relative to the control group (140 mmHg).

## bp1413_q2 Which of the following best describes the study design used in this experiment?
Correct: C
Participants were randomly assigned to treatment groups and followed over time, which is characteristic of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1413_q3 What is the percent reduction in mean systolic blood pressure for the high-dose group compared to the control group?
Correct: B
Percent reduction = [(140 – 120)/140] × 100 = 14.3%.

## bp1413_q4 Which of the following is NOT a potential confounding variable in this study?
Correct: D
The color of the pill does not influence blood pressure and is not a confounder. Age, measurement timing, and diet can all affect BP readings.

## bp1413_q5 Which of the following best links the observed effect to the drug's mechanism of action?
Correct: A
Hypertol is a novel ACE inhibitor; its reduction of angiotensin II levels leads to vasodilation and lower BP.
