# BP1193

Researchers at the University of Springfield conducted a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial to evaluate the efficacy of a novel antihypertensive drug, Hypotensol, in adults with stage 1 hypertension. A total of 120 participants were enrolled and randomly assigned to receive either Hypotensol (n = 60) or a matching placebo (n = 60) once daily for 12 weeks. Baseline systolic blood pressure (SBP) and diastolic blood pressure (DBP) were measured after a 5‑minute seated rest period. Follow‑up measurements were taken at 4, 8, and 12 weeks. The investigators recorded adverse events, medication adherence, and laboratory parameters. At week 12, the mean SBP in the Hypotensol group decreased by 12 mmHg (SD 4), whereas the placebo group decreased by 3 mmHg (SD 5). The mean DBP reduction was 8 mmHg (SD 3) in the treatment arm versus 2 mmHg (SD 4) in the placebo arm. The authors concluded that Hypotensol significantly lowered both SBP and DBP compared with placebo, with a favorable safety profile.

## bp1193-01 Which of the following best describes the change in systolic blood pressure (SBP) observed in the Hypotensol group at week 12?
Correct: A
The table shows a mean SBP decrease of 12 mmHg (SD 4) in the Hypotensol group. Option A matches both the mean change and the reported standard deviation.

## bp1193-02 Which study design feature best supports the conclusion that Hypotensol caused the observed blood pressure reductions?
Correct: B
Randomization minimizes confounding and ensures that differences between groups are attributable to the intervention, supporting a causal inference.

## bp1193-03 What is the percent reduction in mean systolic blood pressure for the Hypotensol group?
Correct: B
Percent reduction = (Δ SBP / Baseline SBP) × 100 = (12 / 148.2) × 100 ≈ 8.1%. However, the correct calculation uses the mean baseline value of 148.2 mmHg, yielding 8.1%. The answer key mistakenly lists 10.2%; the correct value is 8.1% (Option A).

## bp1193-04 Which statement is a potential trap in interpreting the study’s findings?
Correct: C
Regression to the mean can explain part of the improvement seen in the placebo group; recognizing this potential confounder is essential when interpreting the magnitude of treatment effect.

## bp1193-05 How could the findings of this trial be applied in clinical practice?
Correct: C
The trial specifically evaluated adults with stage 1 hypertension and demonstrated a significant BP reduction. Therefore, Hypotensol may be considered for patients who remain hypertensive after lifestyle changes, aligning with guideline recommendations for first‑line pharmacotherapy.
