# BP1393

Researchers at the University of Midvale investigated the combined effect of two antibiotics, Drug A and Drug B, on the viability of *Staphylococcus aureus* cultures over a five‑day period. The study used a 2×2 factorial design, varying the concentration of each drug while keeping the other constant. Bacterial viability was measured daily by plating serial dilutions and counting colony‑forming units, expressed as a percentage of the initial inoculum. The researchers hypothesized that the combination would produce a synergistic reduction in viability compared to either drug alone. Data were collected under identical environmental conditions (37 °C, aerobic, 5 % CO₂) to minimize confounding variables. The study’s primary outcome was the percent reduction in viability on day 5 relative to day 0. Secondary outcomes included the time to achieve 90 % reduction and the emergence of resistant subpopulations. The authors reported that the combination of 8 mg/L Drug A and 4 mg/L Drug B resulted in a 99 % reduction by day 5, whereas each drug alone achieved only 70 % and 65 % reductions, respectively.

Day | Drug A (mg/L) | Drug B (mg/L) | Viability (%)
0 | 0 | 0 | 100
1 | 8 | 0 | 80
1 | 0 | 4 | 75
1 | 8 | 4 | 30
5 | 8 | 4 | 1

## 1 Based on the data presented, which of the following statements is most accurate regarding the effect of the drug combination on bacterial viability?
Correct: A
The table shows that on day 5, the combination of 8 mg/L Drug A and 4 mg/L Drug B yields a viability of 1 %, which is a 99 % reduction from the baseline of 100 %. This is the most accurate statement among the options.

## 2 Which experimental control is essential to determine whether the observed reduction in viability is due to the antibiotics rather than other factors?
Correct: B
An untreated control culture allows comparison of viability changes attributable solely to the antibiotics, controlling for environmental and procedural variables.

## 3 Calculate the percent reduction in viability from day 0 to day 5 for the combination treatment (8 mg/L Drug A + 4 mg/L Drug B).
Correct: A
Percent reduction = [(100 % – 1 %)/100 %] × 100 % = 99 %. The calculation directly uses the viability values from the table.

## 4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the data presented?
Correct: A
Synergy is inferred from a greater effect than either drug alone, but without testing a broader range of concentrations, one cannot conclusively rule out additive effects or other interactions.

## 5 The findings of this study best illustrate which pharmacological concept?
Correct: A
The study demonstrates that increasing concentrations of antibiotics (dose) lead to greater reductions in bacterial viability (response), exemplifying a dose–response relationship.
