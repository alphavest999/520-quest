# BP1280

Researchers studied the effect of a new drug X on blood pressure in hypertensive patients. They recruited 120 participants, randomized into three groups: placebo, low‑dose X (10 mg), high‑dose X (20 mg). Over 12 weeks, systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured weekly. Table 1 shows mean SBP at baseline and week 12 for each group. The placebo group had a baseline mean SBP of 150 mmHg, decreasing to 148 mmHg at week 12. The low‑dose group started at 149 mmHg and fell to 135 mmHg. The high‑dose group began at 151 mmHg and dropped to 120 mmHg. Researchers noted a dose‑dependent reduction in SBP, with the high‑dose group achieving the greatest absolute and relative decrease. Statistical analysis revealed that the reduction in the high‑dose group was statistically significant (p < 0.001) compared to placebo. The study design was double‑blind, placebo‑controlled, and randomized, ensuring minimal bias. The investigators also monitored adverse events, noting mild headaches in 5% of high‑dose participants. The findings suggest that drug X is effective in lowering systolic blood pressure in a dose‑responsive manner.

Placebo | 150 | 148

High‑dose | 151 | 120

## bp1280-1 What was the mean systolic blood pressure at week 12 in the high‑dose group?
Correct: A
The table shows the high‑dose group’s week 12 SBP as 120 mmHg, which is the lowest among the groups.

## bp1280-2 Which of the following best describes the study design used?
Correct: A
Participants were randomized into treatment groups and the study was double‑blind and placebo‑controlled, which are hallmarks of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1280-3 What was the percent reduction in SBP for the high‑dose group from baseline to week 12?
Correct: A
Percent reduction = (151 – 120) / 151 ≈ 0.205 ≈ 20 %. The other options overestimate the reduction.

## bp1280-4 Which group had the highest baseline systolic blood pressure?
Correct: C
Baseline SBP values are 150 mmHg (placebo), 149 mmHg (low‑dose), and 151 mmHg (high‑dose). The high‑dose group had the highest baseline.

## bp1280-5 The observed dose‑dependent reduction in SBP is most closely related to which pharmacological concept?
Correct: B
A dose‑response relationship describes how increasing doses of a drug produce greater pharmacologic effects, which is evident in the greater SBP reduction with higher doses of drug X.
