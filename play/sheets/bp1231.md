# BP1231

Researchers at the Institute for Cellular Pharmacology investigated the cytotoxic effects of a novel small‑molecule inhibitor, Compound X, on human breast cancer cell lines MCF‑7 and MDA‑MB‑231. Cells were seeded at 5 × 10⁴ cells per well in 96‑well plates and treated with Compound X at concentrations ranging from 0 µM (vehicle control) to 200 µM for 48 h. Cell viability was assessed using the MTT assay, and absorbance at 570 nm was converted to percent viability relative to the vehicle control. The results, summarized in Table 1, show a concentration‑dependent decrease in viability, with an IC₅₀ of approximately 75 µM for MCF‑7 and 90 µM for MDA‑MB‑231. Standard deviations were calculated from triplicate wells. The investigators also performed a time‑course study at 24 h and 72 h to confirm the 48‑h time point was representative. These data suggest that Compound X selectively impairs proliferation of breast cancer cells and warrants further preclinical evaluation.

Table 1: Cell viability (% of control) after 48 h treatment with Compound X

Concentration (µM) | Viability (%) | IC50 (µM) | SD
0 | 100 | - | 2
10 | 92 | - | 3
50 | 65 | 75 | 4
100 | 35 | 90 | 5
200 | 12 | - | 6

Correct: B

## bp1231_q2 Which of the following controls is essential for interpreting the concentration‑dependent effect of Compound X?
Correct: D
A vehicle control establishes baseline viability; a positive control demonstrates assay sensitivity; a negative control confirms no intrinsic cytotoxicity. All are needed.

## bp1231_q3 Calculate the mean viability across all concentrations (including 0 µM). What is the value?
Correct: B
Mean = (100 + 92 + 65 + 35 + 12) / 5 = 304 / 5 = 60.8%.

## bp1231_q4 Which of the following statements best describes the relationship between concentration and viability?
Correct: D
The data show a steep drop at low concentrations and a plateau at high concentrations, indicating a non‑linear relationship.

## bp1231_q5 Which of the following is the most appropriate next step to translate these findings into clinical research?
Correct: B
In vivo efficacy in mouse xenografts is the standard next step before human trials to confirm tumor suppression and pharmacodynamics.
