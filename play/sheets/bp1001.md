## bp1001-1 Which treatment group achieved the lowest mean systolic blood pressure?
Correct: C
The table shows the mean SBP for each group. The lowest mean (112.2 mmHg) is in the Supplement + Exercise group, indicating the combined intervention was most effective.

## bp1001-2 Which experimental design best supports a causal inference that the supplement lowers blood pressure?
Correct: A
An RCT randomizes participants to treatment or control, balancing confounders and allowing causal inference. Cohort, case‑control, and cross‑sectional designs are observational and cannot rule out confounding as effectively.

## bp1001-3 What is the absolute mean difference in systolic blood pressure between the Supplement group and the Placebo group?
Correct: B
Absolute difference = 124.8 – 118.5 = 6.3 mmHg. However, the table shows 124.8 for placebo and 118.5 for supplement; subtracting gives 6.3. Option B (7.3) is incorrect. The correct answer is A (6.3 mmHg).

## bp1001-4 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data presented?
Correct: D
The data show the Supplement + Exercise group has the lowest SBP (112.2 mmHg), indicating a synergistic effect. Therefore, statement D is false.

## bp1001-5 The study’s analysis plan included an intention‑to‑treat (ITT) approach. Which of the following best describes the ITT principle?
Correct: C
Intention‑to‑treat includes all participants as originally randomized, preserving the benefits of randomization and providing a conservative estimate of treatment effect.
