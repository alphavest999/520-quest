# BP1131

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antihypertensive drug on systolic and diastolic blood pressure in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. Sixty participants were randomly assigned to either a control group receiving placebo or a treatment group receiving the drug. Baseline measurements were taken, and blood pressure was reassessed after four weeks. The study aimed to evaluate the drug’s efficacy and safety profile over this short period. The results are summarized in Table 1 below, which includes mean systolic blood pressure (SBP) and mean diastolic blood pressure (DBP) for each group at baseline and after four weeks. The data suggest a substantial reduction in SBP in the treatment group, while the control group showed minimal change. Diastolic pressure decreased more markedly in the treatment group, raising questions about potential side effects. These findings provide preliminary evidence that the drug may be effective in lowering blood pressure, but further research is needed to confirm long‑term benefits and safety.

| Week 4 SBP (mmHg) | 148 | 130 |
| Baseline DBP (mmHg) | 95 | 96 |

## bp1131_q1 What percentage reduction in systolic blood pressure (SBP) was observed in the treatment group after four weeks?
Correct: B
The treatment group’s SBP decreased from 152 mmHg to 130 mmHg, a reduction of 22 mmHg. Dividing 22 by the baseline 152 yields 0.1447, or 14.5%. The closest answer choice is 15%.

## bp1131_q2 Which study design best describes the researchers’ methodology?
Correct: A
Participants were randomly assigned to receive either placebo or the drug, and outcomes were measured after a fixed period, which is the hallmark of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1131_q3 What is the difference in mean diastolic blood pressure (DBP) change between the control and treatment groups after four weeks?
Correct: C
Control change: 94–95 = –1 mmHg. Treatment change: 80–96 = –16 mmHg. Difference: (–1) – (–16) = +15 mmHg, meaning the treatment group’s DBP decreased 15 mmHg more than the control group. The answer choice reflects the magnitude of the difference, 15 mmHg.

## bp1131_q4 Which of the following statements is false based on the data presented?
Correct: C
The control group’s DBP decreased from 95 to 94 (–1 mmHg), whereas the treatment group’s DBP decreased from 96 to 80 (–16 mmHg). Therefore, the control group did NOT have a greater reduction; the treatment group did. Option C is false.

## bp1131_q5 Which statement best links the study’s findings to clinical practice?
Correct: B
The data demonstrate a large SBP reduction (≈15%) and a marked DBP drop (≈16 mmHg) in the treatment group, indicating efficacy but also a risk of excessive DBP lowering. Option B captures this dual implication.
