# BP1218

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antioxidant on oxidative stress markers in cultured human fibroblasts. The experiment involved five treatment groups: a vehicle control, two concentrations of the antioxidant (10 µM and 50 µM), and two positive controls (ascorbic acid 10 µM and N-acetylcysteine 10 µM). After 24 hours of exposure, intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS) levels were quantified by a fluorescent probe, and glutathione (GSH) concentrations were measured spectrophotometrically. The data, summarized in Table 1, show a dose-dependent decrease in ROS with the antioxidant, while GSH levels increased markedly at 50 µM. The positive controls exhibited similar trends, confirming assay validity. Statistical analysis revealed significant differences (p < 0.05) between the 50 µM antioxidant group and the vehicle control for both ROS and GSH, suggesting potent antioxidant activity. These findings support further investigation into the therapeutic potential of the compound for diseases associated with oxidative damage.

## bp1218-2 Which experimental design element is essential to attribute the observed effects specifically to the antioxidant?
Correct: B
A vehicle control allows comparison to baseline conditions, ensuring that changes are due to the antioxidant rather than the solvent or handling.

## bp1218-3 Calculate the fold change in ROS between the 50 µM antioxidant group and the vehicle control.
Correct: D
Fold change = ROS_50µM / ROS_vehicle = 600 / 1000 = 0.60. Option D represents the correct ratio (0.60).

## bp1218-4 Which statement is a common misconception about interpreting p-values in this context?
Correct: B
The p-value indicates the probability of observing data as extreme as those observed if the null hypothesis were true, not the probability that the null hypothesis itself is true.

## bp1218-5 Which concept is most directly linked to the observed increase in glutathione levels after antioxidant treatment?
Correct: B
Glutathione synthesis requires NADPH; antioxidants can enhance NADPH availability, boosting GSH production.
