# BP1053

Researchers studied the effect of varying concentrations of a novel antimicrobial peptide on bacterial growth rates in a controlled in vitro environment. They used a 96‑well microplate assay with four concentration levels (0, 5, 10, 20 µg/mL) and measured optical density at 600 nm after 24 hours. The experiment was performed in triplicate for each concentration. Additionally, they assessed the peptide’s cytotoxicity on a human keratinocyte cell line (HaCaT) using a lactate dehydrogenase release assay at the same concentrations. The data were recorded in Table 1. The researchers hypothesized that higher peptide concentrations would reduce bacterial growth while maintaining low cytotoxicity. Statistical analysis involved one‑way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post‑hoc test to compare mean OD values among groups. The results indicated a significant reduction in bacterial OD at 10 and 20 µg/mL (p < 0.01) and negligible cytotoxicity up to 20 µg/mL (LDH release < 5 % of control). These findings suggest the peptide is a promising candidate for topical antimicrobial therapy.

## bp1053_q1 Based on Table 1, which concentration of the antimicrobial peptide resulted in the lowest mean optical density (OD600) after 24 hours?
Correct: D
The lowest mean OD600 value (0.30) is observed at the highest peptide concentration (20 µg/mL), indicating the greatest reduction in bacterial growth.

## bp1053_q2 Which of the following best describes the experimental design used to assess the peptide’s cytotoxicity?
Correct: B
The study employed HaCaT cells and measured lactate dehydrogenase release to evaluate cytotoxicity, a standard in vitro method for assessing cell membrane integrity.

## bp1053_q3 If the mean OD600 at 0 µg/mL is 1.00 and at 20 µg/mL is 0.30, what is the percent reduction in bacterial growth at 20 µg/mL?
Correct: C
Percent reduction = [(1.00 – 0.30)/1.00] × 100 = 70 %. This calculation reflects the decrease in bacterial optical density relative to the untreated control.

## bp1053_q4 Which statement is a potential trap when interpreting the LDH release data?
Correct: B
LDH release measures necrotic cell death and does not detect apoptotic events. Relying solely on LDH could miss apoptosis, leading to an incomplete assessment of cytotoxicity.

## bp1053_q5 Which of the following best links the observed antimicrobial activity to a potential mechanism of action?
Correct: A
Antimicrobial peptides commonly exert their effect by inserting into and disrupting bacterial membranes, which aligns with the rapid reduction in OD600 observed at higher concentrations.
