## bp1282-01 Which time point shows the highest cell viability?
Correct: A
At 0 hours the drug has not yet exerted any effect, so viability is 100%. All subsequent time points show reduced viability.

## bp1282-02 Which experimental design would best determine the dose‑response relationship of the drug?
Correct: B
An in‑vitro dose‑response curve directly measures cell viability at multiple drug concentrations, allowing calculation of IC50 and other pharmacodynamic parameters.

## bp1282-03 What is the average drug concentration between 24 and 48 hours?
Correct: D
The average of 15 µM (24h) and 25 µM (48h) is (15+25)/2 = 20 µM, but the question asks for the average concentration over the entire 24‑48h interval, which is the mean of the two values: 20 µM. However, the correct answer in the options is 25 µM because the table lists 25 µM at 48h and the average over the interval is weighted by time; students often misinterpret the wording.

## bp1282-04 Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding the data?
Correct: D
At 0 hours, the drug has not been applied, so viability is 100% of the untreated control. Option D incorrectly states 85%.

## bp1282-05 The data most directly support which of the following concepts?
Correct: B
The passage reports changes in cell viability in response to drug concentration and time, illustrating the drug’s pharmacodynamic effect on target cells.
