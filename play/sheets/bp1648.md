# BP1648

Researchers conducted a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial to evaluate the antihypertensive efficacy of the novel agent X in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. Eighty patients were enrolled and randomly assigned to receive either drug X (n = 40) or matching placebo (n = 40) once daily for 8 weeks. Systolic blood pressure (SBP) was measured at baseline, week 4, and week 8 using an automated cuff. The mean SBP at baseline was 150 mmHg in both groups. At week 4, the drug group’s mean SBP fell to 140 mmHg, whereas the placebo group’s mean SBP was 148 mmHg. By week 8, the drug group’s mean SBP had further decreased to 130 mmHg, while the placebo group’s mean SBP was 147 mmHg. The investigators reported a statistically significant difference (p < 0.01) between groups at week 8. No serious adverse events were noted. The authors concluded that drug X effectively reduces SBP in hypertensive adults and may lower cardiovascular risk.

The mean SBP decreased from 150 mmHg to 130 mmHg, an absolute reduction of 20 mmHg.

## bp1648-2 Which of the following best describes the study design used in this trial?
Correct: B

## bp1648-3 Calculate the percent reduction in mean SBP from baseline to week 8 in the drug group.
Correct: B
Percent reduction = (150 mmHg – 130 mmHg) / 150 mmHg = 0.133 ≈ 13.3%.
