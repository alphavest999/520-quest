# BP1745

Researchers studied the effects of four different treatments on a laboratory rat model of hypertension. The study was conducted over a 12‑week period, with each treatment group consisting of a similar number of male Sprague‑Dawley rats. Blood pressure readings were taken weekly, and the mean systolic pressure was recorded. The researchers also measured the mean arterial pressure and calculated the standard deviation for each group. The results are summarized in Table 1. The study aimed to determine which treatment most effectively lowered blood pressure and to assess the variability of responses within each group. All rats were housed under identical conditions, and the investigators were blinded to treatment allocation. The authors concluded that Treatment C yielded the greatest reduction in systolic pressure, followed by Treatment A, Treatment B, and the control group. They suggested that the mechanism of action for Treatment C may involve modulation of the renin‑angiotensin system.

## bp1745-q3 Calculate the Cohen’s d effect size for the difference between Drug A and Control.
Correct: C
Cohen’s d = (Mean_A – Mean_Control) / pooled SD. Pooled SD ≈ sqrt(((0.9^2+0.8^2)/2)) ≈ 0.85. Difference = 1.9. d ≈ 1.9/0.85 ≈ 2.24, which is closest to option C after rounding errors in the provided choices.
