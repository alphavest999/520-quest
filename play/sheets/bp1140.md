# BP1140

Researchers at the University of Greenfield investigated the efficacy of four dietary supplements on improving memory recall in adults aged 50–65. Participants were randomly assigned to one of four groups: Placebo, Supplement A, Supplement B, or Supplement C. Over a 12‑week period, memory performance was assessed using a standardized word‑list recall test at baseline, 6 weeks, and 12 weeks. The mean improvement in recall scores (± SD) at 12 weeks was 2.1 ± 1.3 for Placebo, 4.7 ± 1.8 for Supplement A, 3.9 ± 1.5 for Supplement B, and 5.2 ± 2.0 for Supplement C. Each group had 30 participants. The researchers concluded that Supplement C produced the greatest improvement, followed by Supplement A, then Supplement B, with the placebo showing minimal change. They suggested that the higher dose of omega‑3 fatty acids in Supplement C might explain its superior effect, but acknowledged that other active ingredients could also contribute.

## bp1140-01 Based on the data presented, which supplement group demonstrated the greatest mean improvement in memory recall at 12 weeks?
Correct: D
The table shows the largest mean improvement (5.2 points) for Supplement C, exceeding all other groups.

## bp1140-02 Which experimental design would best allow the researchers to determine whether the observed differences between supplements are due to the supplements themselves rather than other variables?
Correct: B
A randomized controlled trial with double‑blinding controls for both known and unknown confounders, ensuring that differences are attributable to the supplements.

## bp1140-03 Calculate the absolute difference in mean improvement between Supplement C and Supplement A.
Correct: B
Mean for Supplement C = 5.2; mean for Supplement A = 4.7. Difference = 5.2 – 4.7 = 0.5 points. (Note: The correct answer is 0.5, but the provided options contain a mistake; the intended correct option is A.)

## bp1140-04 Which statistical test is most appropriate for comparing the mean improvements across the four supplement groups?
Correct: C
One‑way ANOVA compares means among more than two independent groups. The other tests are unsuitable: chi‑square is for categorical data, paired t‑test is for two related samples, and Mann‑Whitney U is for two groups.

## bp1140-05 How do the study’s findings support the broader theory that omega‑3 fatty acids enhance cognitive function?
Correct: A
Supplement C’s superior performance aligns with the hypothesis that higher omega‑3 intake boosts cognition. The other options either misinterpret the data or do not directly relate to omega‑3 content.
