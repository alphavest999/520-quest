Each participant completed a standardized reaction time task before and after a 4‑week training period. The mean post‑training reaction times (in milliseconds) and standard deviations are presented in Table 1. The study aimed to determine whether any of the training protocols produced a statistically significant improvement compared to the control condition and to assess the magnitude of the effect across protocols.
