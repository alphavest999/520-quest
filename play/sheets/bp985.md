# BP985

Researchers investigated the effect of caffeine on cognitive performance in a controlled laboratory setting. Sixty healthy adults aged 20–35 were randomly assigned to one of four groups: placebo, low‑dose caffeine (50 mg), moderate‑dose caffeine (100 mg), and high‑dose caffeine (200 mg). After ingestion, participants completed a 30‑minute computerized reaction‑time task. Table 1 summarizes the mean reaction times (in milliseconds) and standard deviations for each group. The researchers hypothesized that moderate caffeine would yield the fastest reaction times, while high doses might produce diminishing returns or adverse effects. Data were analyzed using ANOVA, and post‑hoc pairwise comparisons were planned to identify significant differences between groups. The study also collected self‑reported measures of alertness and mood to assess subjective effects. Researchers ensured blinding by using identical capsules and coded labels. The findings have implications for optimizing alertness in high‑stakes occupations such as air‑traffic control and surgery.

## bp985-1 Which of the following best describes the pattern of mean reaction times across the caffeine dosage groups?
Correct: B
The moderate‑dose group (100 mg) shows the fastest mean reaction time (310 ms), while the high‑dose group (200 mg) is slower (320 ms) than the moderate dose but faster than placebo, indicating a non‑linear, inverted‑U pattern.

## bp985-2 Which of the following best describes the experimental design used in this study?
Correct: B
Participants were randomly assigned to four groups and the study was conducted under controlled conditions with blinding, which are hallmarks of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp985-3 Calculate the mean difference in reaction time between the placebo group and the high‑dose caffeine group.
Correct: C
Mean difference = 350 ms (placebo) – 320 ms (high dose) = 30 ms.

## bp985-4 Which of the following is a potential confounding variable that the researchers should control for?
Correct: C
Both habitual caffeine use and circadian timing can influence reaction time and should be accounted for to isolate the effect of the experimental doses.

## bp985-5 Which of the following best explains the use of a placebo control in this study?
Correct: B
A placebo allows researchers and participants to remain unaware of the actual treatment, thereby minimizing expectancy and demand characteristics that could bias performance.
