# BP984

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel antihypertensive agent, Hypren, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with mild hypertension. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants (age 35–65). Participants were assigned to either Hypren (n=60) or placebo (n=60). Baseline SBP was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest, and follow‑up SBP was recorded after 12 weeks of therapy. Table 1 summarizes the mean SBP values, the mean change from baseline, and the p‑value for the difference between groups. The Hypren group demonstrated a mean SBP reduction of 12.4 mmHg (SD 5.6), whereas the placebo group had a reduction of 3.1 mmHg (SD 4.8). The between‑group difference was statistically significant (p<0.001). The study also collected data on adverse events, adherence rates, and quality‑of‑life scores, but these are not shown in Table 1. The authors conclude that Hypren provides a clinically meaningful reduction in SBP with a favorable safety profile.

## bp984-001 Which statement best describes the data presented in Table 1?
Correct: A
The table shows a mean SBP reduction of 12.4 mmHg for Hypren versus 3.1 mmHg for placebo, with a highly significant p‑value (<0.001), indicating a clinically meaningful difference.

## bp984-002 Which aspect of the study design best ensures that the observed SBP reduction is attributable to Hypren?
Correct: A
Randomization minimizes confounding and balances known and unknown variables between groups, attributing differences in SBP to the intervention.

## bp984-003 Calculate the average SBP reduction per participant in the Hypren group.
Correct: B
The table lists a mean change of –12.4 mmHg for the Hypren group; this value is the average reduction per participant.

## bp984-004 Which of the following is a common statistical trap when interpreting the study’s p‑value?
Correct: A
A small p‑value indicates statistical significance but does not automatically imply the effect is clinically important; the magnitude of the reduction must also be considered.

## bp984-005 How might the findings of this study influence clinical practice guidelines for mild hypertension?
Correct: B
The study demonstrates a significant SBP reduction with Hypren, supporting its consideration as an alternative for patients inadequately controlled by standard therapy.
