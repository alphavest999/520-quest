# BP1428

Researchers studied the effect of a novel dietary supplement, X, on insulin sensitivity in adults with prediabetes. In a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial, 120 participants were assigned to receive either X (n=60) or placebo (n=60) for 12 weeks. Baseline characteristics were similar between groups. The primary outcome was the change in HOMA‑IR from baseline to week 12. Secondary outcomes included fasting glucose, HbA1c, and body weight. Table 1 summarizes the mean changes for each group. The X group showed a mean decrease in HOMA‑IR of 1.2 ± 0.5 units, whereas the placebo group had a mean decrease of 0.3 ± 0.4 units. Fasting glucose decreased by 5 mg/dL in the X group versus 1 mg/dL in placebo. HbA1c decreased by 0.4% in the X group versus 0.1% in placebo. Body weight decreased by 3.5 lbs in the X group versus 1.0 lb in placebo. The authors concluded that X significantly improves insulin sensitivity and glycemic control in prediabetic adults.
