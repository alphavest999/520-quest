# BP1120

Researchers at the Cognitive Neuroscience Laboratory investigated how brief mindfulness training affects reaction times in a simple visual detection task. Forty healthy adults (age 20–35) were randomly assigned to either a mindfulness group or a control group. The mindfulness group received a 10‑minute guided meditation before each of the 20 trials, whereas the control group listened to a neutral audio clip. Reaction times (RTs) were recorded in milliseconds. Table 1 summarizes the mean RTs and standard deviations for each group. The results suggest that mindfulness training may reduce RTs, but the variability within groups also differs. These findings raise questions about the mechanisms underlying the observed effects and whether the benefits are consistent across individuals.
