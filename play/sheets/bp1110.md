Twenty-five participants were enrolled in a randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial. Baseline SBP was recorded for each patient, followed by a 4‑week treatment period during which SBP was measured again. The study aimed to evaluate the drug’s efficacy and safety profile. Table 1 presents the individual patient data, including baseline SBP, post‑treatment SBP, and the percent change. The investigators also monitored adverse events, but none were reported. The results suggest a modest but statistically significant reduction in SBP across the cohort, with a mean percent decrease of 8.4%. The authors conclude that CardioRelief may be a viable option for patients who cannot tolerate first‑line agents.
