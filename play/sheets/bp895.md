# BP895

Researchers at the University of Metropolis used UV–visible spectrophotometry to determine the pKa values of the side chains of four amino acids: aspartic acid, lysine, histidine, and glutamic acid. The experiment involved preparing 0.1 M solutions of each amino acid in 10 mM phosphate buffer and recording the absorbance at 280 nm while incrementally adjusting the pH from 1.0 to 12.0. The resulting titration curves were fitted to the Henderson–Hasselbalch equation to extract the pKa values. The data were then compared to literature values to assess the influence of ionic strength and temperature on the measurements. The study also examined how the protonation state of each side chain affects the overall charge of the amino acid at physiological pH.
