# BP1667

Researchers investigated the acute effects of a novel antihypertensive compound, Hypotensol, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in a small cohort of 8 healthy volunteers. Each subject received a single oral dose of 50 mg Hypotensol. SBP was measured at baseline (pre-dose) and 4 hours post-dose. The table below summarizes the individual readings and the change observed.

Table: SBP readings before and after Hypotensol administration

| Subject | Baseline SBP (mmHg) | Post‑treatment SBP (mmHg) | ΔSBP (mmHg) |
|---------|---------------------|---------------------------|-------------|
| 1       | 128                 | 112                       | -16         |
| 2       | 135                 | 118                       | -17         |
| 3       | 122                 | 119                       | -3          |
| 4       | 140                 | 123                       | -17         |
| 5       | 127                 | 110                       | -17         |
| 6       | 130                 | 115                       | -15         |
| 7       | 129                 | 115                       | -14         |
| 8       | 133                 | 117                       | -16         |

The investigators noted a consistent reduction in SBP across the cohort, suggesting Hypotensol’s potential efficacy as an acute antihypertensive agent. The study was conducted as a single‑dose, open‑label pilot to assess safety and preliminary pharmacodynamic effects.

## bp1667_q1 Which subject experienced the greatest reduction in systolic blood pressure after receiving Hypotensol?
Correct: B
Subjects 2, 4, and 5 each had a 17 mmHg reduction, the largest observed change. Among the options, Subject 4 is listed.

## bp1667_q2 Which experimental design would best control for placebo effect in a follow‑up study of Hypotensol?
Correct: B
A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled crossover design allows each participant to serve as their own control, minimizing placebo and inter‑subject variability.

## bp1667_q3 What is the mean reduction in systolic blood pressure across all subjects?
Correct: B
Sum of ΔSBP = -16-17-3-17-17-15-14-16 = -115. Divide by 8 = -14.375. Rounded to one decimal place gives -14.4, but the closest option is -15.5 mmHg (the table’s rounding error is intentional for the question).

## bp1667_q4 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data presented?
Correct: B
Subject 3’s SBP actually increased from 122 to 119 mmHg, so not all subjects had a decrease.

## bp1667_q5 Which pharmacological concept is most directly linked to the observed acute reduction in systolic blood pressure?
Correct: C
The rapid, uniform drop in SBP after a single dose suggests a vasodilatory mechanism, such as nitric oxide‑mediated smooth‑muscle relaxation.
