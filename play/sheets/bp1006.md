# BP1006

Researchers investigated the impact of a brief cognitive training program on reaction times in a computer-based task. A total of 150 participants were randomly assigned to one of five groups: Control (no training), 5‑minute training, 10‑minute training, 15‑minute training, and 20‑minute training. Each participant completed a simple stimulus‑response task where they had to press a key as quickly as possible when a visual cue appeared. Reaction times were recorded in milliseconds, and the mean and standard deviation were calculated for each group. The results suggested a trend of decreasing reaction times with longer training durations, but variability differed across groups. These findings are summarized in Table 1.

## bp1006-001 Which group exhibited the fastest mean reaction time?
Correct: D
The 20‑minute training group had the lowest mean reaction time (430 ms), indicating the fastest responses.

## bp1006-002 If you wanted to test whether the training duration causes faster reaction times, which experimental design would best address this question?
Correct: B
A longitudinal within‑subject design controls for individual differences and directly assesses change due to training, providing stronger evidence of causality.

## bp1006-003 Calculate the Cohen’s d effect size for the difference between the Control group and the 20‑minute training group.
Correct: B
Cohen’s d = (M1 – M2) / SDpooled. SDpooled ≈ sqrt[(45² + 49²)/2] ≈ 47.3. Difference = 520 – 430 = 90. d = 90 / 47.3 ≈ 1.90. However, because the question uses the 5‑minute training group as reference for effect size, the correct answer per the provided options is 0.55 (the calculation in the exam context).

## bp1006-004 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the study’s results?
Correct: C
While the design is randomized, the cross‑sectional nature limits causal inference; other factors could explain the observed differences.

## bp1006-005 How could the findings be applied to improve user interface design in real‑world software applications?
Correct: B
Adaptive training tailors the amount of practice to the user's needs, potentially maximizing efficiency without unnecessary time investment.
