## bp1100-01 What is the cell viability at a drug concentration of 20 µM?
Correct: B
The table shows that at 20 µM the viability is 70 % of the untreated control.

## bp1100-02 Which experimental control is most appropriate for this study?
Correct: A
The vehicle control accounts for any effects of the solvent on cell viability, ensuring observed changes are due to the drug itself.

## bp1100-03 What is the percent decrease in viability from the control to the 80 µM concentration?
Correct: C
The control is 100 %. At 80 µM viability is 20 %, so the decrease is 100 % – 20 % = 80 %.

## bp1100-04 Which statement about the data is FALSE?
Correct: B
While the standard deviation does increase slightly, the pattern is not strictly monotonic; the 20 µM concentration has a smaller SD (4) than 10 µM (3) and 40 µM (5). The statement implies a consistent increase, which is not supported by the data.

## bp1100-05 Which concept best explains the observed dose‑dependent decrease in cell viability?
Correct: B
A dose‑dependent cytotoxic effect is most consistent with apoptosis induction, a common mechanism for chemotherapeutic agents.
