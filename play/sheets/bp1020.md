# BP1020

Researchers investigated the effect of a novel small‑molecule inhibitor, Drug X, on the viability of cultured human cancer cells. Four concentrations of Drug X (0, 10, 50, and 100 µM) were applied to the cells for 48 hours. Cell viability was quantified using an MTT assay, and the results were expressed as a percentage of the untreated control. The data were collected from three independent experiments, each with ten replicates. The investigators noted a dose‑dependent decrease in viability, with the highest concentration producing the greatest cytotoxic effect. Standard deviations increased slightly with concentration, suggesting greater variability at higher drug doses. The study aimed to determine whether Drug X could be a potential chemotherapeutic agent and to identify a concentration that maximizes efficacy while minimizing toxicity.
