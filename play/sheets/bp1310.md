Each group consisted of 30 participants. The mean reaction time (RT) and standard deviation (SD) were recorded after a 4‑week training period. The results are summarized in Table 1. ## bp1310-01 Which group had the lowest mean reaction time?
Correct: B
The mean RT for Treatment A is 510 ms, which is lower than the means for Control (550 ms), Treatment B (530 ms), and Treatment C (520 ms).

## bp1310-02 What type of study design was used in this research?
Correct: A
Participants were randomly assigned to treatment groups, a hallmark of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1310-05 Which future research question would best extend the findings of this study?
