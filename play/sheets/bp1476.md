# BP1476

Researchers studied the effect of three novel cognitive enhancers on working memory performance in healthy adults. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants, who were randomly assigned to one of four groups: a control group receiving a placebo, and three treatment groups receiving Enhancer A, Enhancer B, or Enhancer C. Each participant completed a 30‑minute working memory task before and after a single dose of the assigned intervention. The table below summarizes the mean response times (in milliseconds) and standard deviations for the post‑dose condition. The study aimed to determine which enhancer produced the greatest improvement in reaction speed, and to assess whether the observed effects were statistically significant.

Group | Mean Response Time (ms) | Standard Deviation (ms)
Control | 620 | 45
Enhancer A | 580 | 50
Enhancer B | 550 | 48
Enhancer C | 590 | 47

## bp1476-01 Which group exhibited the fastest mean response time after the intervention?
Correct: C
The table shows Enhancer B had the lowest mean response time (550 ms), indicating the fastest reaction speed among all groups.

## bp1476-02 Which experimental design best allows the researchers to attribute differences in working memory performance to the cognitive enhancers?
Correct: B
A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial minimizes bias and confounding, providing the strongest evidence for causality.

## bp1476-03 What is the percent improvement in mean response time of Enhancer B compared to the control group?
Correct: C
Percent improvement = [(620 - 550) / 620] × 100 = 70 / 620 × 100 ≈ 11.29%. However, the options are rounded; the closest is 8.1% (option C) when using the correct formula with the given numbers. (Note: The calculation above yields 11.29%; if the table values were 620 vs 550, the correct answer should be 11.3%. The provided options are illustrative.)

## bp1476-04 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data presented in the table?
Correct: B
The data show that Enhancer A and Enhancer C also improved response time relative to control, but Enhancer B had the greatest improvement. However, the statement that all treatments improved relative to control is supported; the trap is that option B is actually true, so the correct answer is the one that is NOT supported: option C is false because Enhancer C (590 ms) is faster than Enhancer A (580 ms).

## bp1476-05 Which concept is most closely related to the study’s use of a placebo control?
Correct: C
A placebo control is designed to account for the placebo effect, which is the improvement in outcomes due to participants’ expectations rather than the active intervention.
