# BP1671

Researchers investigated the effect of four different formulations (A, B, C, and D) on the concentration of a bioactive compound in plasma. Each formulation was administered to a separate cohort of 30 healthy volunteers. The mean plasma concentration (in mg) and the standard deviation (SD) for each cohort were recorded. The data are summarized in Table 3‑4. The study aimed to identify which formulation produced the highest mean concentration while maintaining low variability. The researchers used a completely randomized design and ensured that all participants were matched for age and sex. The results provide a basis for selecting a formulation for further clinical development. The table below shows the key descriptive statistics for each treatment group.

Table 3‑4: Treatment Summary
| Treatment | Mean Response (mg) | SD | Sample Size |
|-----------|--------------------|----|-------------|
| A         | 12.5               | 1.2 | 30          |
| B         | 15.3               | 1.5 | 30          |
| C         | 13.8               | 1.1 | 30          |
| D         | 14.1               | 1.3 | 30          |

## bp1671_q1 Which treatment had the lowest mean plasma concentration?
Correct: A
The mean responses are 12.5 (A), 15.3 (B), 13.8 (C), and 14.1 (D). The lowest mean is 12.5, corresponding to Treatment A.

## bp1671_q2 Which experimental design best controls for inter‑subject variability in this study?
Correct: A
A randomized block design would group participants with similar characteristics (e.g., age, sex) into blocks before randomizing treatments, thereby controlling for inter‑subject variability. The study used a completely randomized design, but the question asks for the design that best controls variability.

## bp1671_q3 Calculate the pooled standard deviation for Treatments A and B.
Correct: B
Pooled SD = sqrt(((n1‑1)s1² + (n2‑1)s2²)/(n1+n2‑2)). With n1=n2=30, s1=1.2, s2=1.5:
((29)(1.44)+(29)(2.25))/58 = 107.01/58 = 1.845; sqrt(1.845) ≈ 1.36, closest to 1.35.

## bp1671_q4 Which of the following statements is FALSE?
Correct: D
Mean values: A=12.5, B=15.3, C=13.8, D=14.1. The highest mean is 15.3 (B). Statement D incorrectly claims C had the highest mean.

## bp1671_q5 Which statement best links the mean response to the standard deviation?
Correct: C
The mean response and standard deviation are descriptive statistics that characterize different aspects of the data: the mean indicates central tendency, while the SD indicates spread. They are not directly related by proportionality.
