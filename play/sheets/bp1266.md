# BP1266

Researchers conducted a randomized controlled trial to evaluate the effect of a novel antihypertensive drug on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. One hundred and fifty‑two participants were randomly assigned to one of four groups: placebo, low‑dose (5 mg), medium‑dose (10 mg), or high‑dose (20 mg). Baseline SBP was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest, and follow‑up readings were taken at 4 weeks. The mean SBP (±SD) for each group at baseline and after 4 weeks was: Placebo 150 ± 10 mmHg → 148 ± 9 mmHg; Low‑dose 149 ± 9 mmHg → 140 ± 8 mmHg; Medium‑dose 151 ± 11 mmHg → 130 ± 7 mmHg; High‑dose 152 ± 12 mmHg → 120 ± 6 mmHg. The study demonstrated a dose‑dependent reduction in SBP, with the high‑dose group achieving the greatest mean decrease.

## bp1266-01 Which group experienced the greatest average systolic blood pressure reduction?
Correct: D
The mean reduction is calculated by subtracting the 4‑week value from the baseline: 152 mmHg – 120 mmHg = 32 mmHg for the high‑dose group, which is the largest reduction among all groups.

## bp1266-02 What type of study design was employed in this investigation?
Correct: A
Participants were randomly assigned to treatment groups and followed over time, which is the hallmark of a randomized controlled trial.

## bp1266-03 Calculate the mean reduction in systolic blood pressure for the Medium‑dose group.
Correct: C
Mean reduction = Baseline – Week 4 = 151 mmHg – 130 mmHg = 21 mmHg.

## bp1266-04 Which group had the highest baseline systolic blood pressure?
Correct: D
Baseline values: Placebo 150, Low‑dose 149, Medium‑dose 151, High‑dose 152 mmHg. The high‑dose group has the greatest baseline pressure.

## bp1266-05 Which mnemonic best helps remember the key components of blood‑pressure management?
Correct: D
The mnemonic "SALT" is commonly used to remember the four pillars of blood‑pressure control: Systolic, Aortic, Lymphatic, and Thrombus management. Option D matches the correct wording.
