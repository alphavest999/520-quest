# BP1757

Researchers investigated the relationship between light intensity and the rate of photosynthesis in the freshwater alga *Chlorella vulgaris*. The algae were grown under controlled temperature (22 °C) and CO₂ concentration (400 ppm). Light intensity was varied using neutral density filters, and the rate of oxygen production was measured as an index of photosynthetic activity. The following data were obtained over a 30‑minute period:

| Light Intensity (µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹) | O₂ Production (µmol O₂ g⁻¹ dry wt h⁻¹) |
|----------------------------------------|-------------------------------------------|
| 50                                     | 12.4                                       |
| 150                                    | 32.7                                       |
| 300                                    | 58.9                                       |
| 600                                    | 81.2                                       |
| 1200                                   | 92.5                                       |

The researchers noted that the rate of photosynthesis increased with light intensity but began to plateau at higher intensities, suggesting a light‑saturation effect.

## 1 Which of the following best describes the trend observed in the data?
Correct: B
The data show a steep rise in O₂ production from 50 to 600 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹, followed by a smaller increase from 600 to 1200 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹, indicating a plateau characteristic of light saturation.

## 2 If the researchers wanted to investigate the effect of temperature on photosynthetic rate while keeping light intensity constant, which experimental design would be most appropriate?
Correct: B
To isolate temperature as the independent variable, light intensity must be held constant. Option B fixes light at 300 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹ and varies temperature, allowing assessment of temperature effects alone.

## 3 Using the data from the table, calculate the approximate slope (in µmol O₂ g⁻¹ dry wt h⁻¹ per µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹) of the linear portion of the response curve (from 50 to 300 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹).
Correct: A
Slope = (58.9 – 12.4) / (300 – 50) = 46.5 / 250 ≈ 0.186. The closest option is 0.15.

## 4 Which of the following statements would most likely lead a reader to incorrectly conclude that light intensity is the sole determinant of photosynthetic rate?
Correct: A
Option A focuses only on the positive correlation without mentioning the plateau, leading readers to think the relationship is linear and unlimited. The other options provide context that temp and CO₂ were controlled.

## 5 The observed plateau in O₂ production at high light intensities is most closely associated with which of the following concepts?
Correct: B
A plateau indicates that photosynthetic machinery is saturated with light; additional photons do not increase the rate, which is the definition of light saturation.
