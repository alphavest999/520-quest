# BP1746

Researchers studied the effects of a novel antioxidant compound on oxidative stress markers in cultured human fibroblasts. The experiment was conducted over a 48‑hour period, with cells exposed to varying concentrations (0, 10, 25, 50 µM) of the compound. At the end of the exposure, intracellular reactive oxygen species (ROS) levels were quantified using a fluorescence-based assay, and the expression of the antioxidant enzyme superoxide dismutase (SOD) was measured by qPCR. The study also included a positive control (ascorbic acid) and a vehicle control (DMSO). The results indicated a dose‑dependent decrease in ROS and a concomitant increase in SOD expression, suggesting the compound’s potential therapeutic benefit in reducing oxidative damage. The data were analyzed using one‑way ANOVA followed by Tukey’s post‑hoc test, with significance set at p < 0.05.

Variable | 0 µM | 10 µM | 25 µM | 50 µM
---|---|---|---|---
ROS fluorescence (RFU) | 1200 | 950 | 700 | 500
SOD expression (fold change) | 1.0 | 1.4 | 1.8 | 2.2

## bp1746_Q1 Based on the data presented, which concentration of the antioxidant compound resulted in the greatest reduction in ROS fluorescence compared to the vehicle control?
Correct: C
The ROS fluorescence values decrease progressively with higher concentrations; the lowest value (500 RFU) occurs at 50 µM, indicating the greatest reduction relative to the 0 µM control (1200 RFU).

## bp1746_Q2 Which of the following best describes the experimental design used to assess the antioxidant compound’s efficacy?
Correct: B
The study varied the compound concentration across multiple groups, included vehicle and positive controls, and measured outcomes in a controlled in‑vitro setting, which constitutes a dose‑response experimental design.

## bp1746_Q3 What is the percent decrease in ROS fluorescence at 50 µM compared to the 0 µM control?
Correct: D
Percent decrease = [(1200 - 500) / 1200] × 100 = 700/1200 × 100 ≈ 58.3%.

## bp1746_Q4 Which of the following statements is a common misinterpretation of the study’s findings?
Correct: C
The data show a dose‑dependent reduction in ROS; the vehicle control (0 µM) still exhibits high ROS levels, indicating that DMSO alone does not reduce ROS. Assuming no effect of DMSO is a trap.

## bp1746_Q5 How do the findings of this study relate to the broader concept of oxidative stress in aging?
Correct: B
The study shows that lowering ROS and increasing antioxidant enzyme expression can protect cells from oxidative damage, a key mechanism implicated in aging and age‑related diseases.
