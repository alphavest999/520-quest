## bp1012_q2 Which of the following best describes the experimental design used in this study?
Correct: B
The study randomized participants to either Hypotensol or placebo and followed them in parallel groups, with blinding implied by the placebo control. This matches a parallel‑group randomized controlled trial.

## bp1012_q4 Which of the following statements is a potential trap when interpreting the study’s findings?
Correct: B
A trap is to assume that a subset of 12 participants is representative of the entire 80‑person trial. The small sample limits external validity and may overstate precision.

## bp1012_q5 Which of the following links best explains why the researchers used a placebo control in this trial?
Correct: A
A placebo control is primarily used to blind participants and investigators, thereby minimizing expectancy and observer bias. While side‑effect monitoring and regulatory approval are also important, the main rationale for a placebo is to control for placebo effects.
