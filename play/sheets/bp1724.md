# BP1724

Researchers at the University of Midvale investigated how varying levels of sleep deprivation affect simple reaction time in healthy adults. A total of 120 participants (60 males, 60 females, ages 18–35) were randomly assigned to one of four sleep conditions: 8 hours (control), 6 hours, 4 hours, or 2 hours of sleep the night before testing. All participants completed a computerized simple reaction time task, in which they pressed a button as quickly as possible when a visual stimulus appeared. Reaction times were recorded in milliseconds. In addition to the mean reaction time, the study also reported the standard deviation for each group to reflect variability. The researchers hypothesized that reaction time would increase (i.e., become slower) as sleep duration decreased, and that the variability would also increase under greater sleep deprivation. The data collected provide a basis for testing this hypothesis and for exploring potential mechanisms underlying the cognitive effects of sleep loss. Participants were instructed to maintain regular caffeine intake and avoid alcohol 24 hours before testing. The reaction time task consisted of 200 trials, with stimuli presented at random intervals between 1.5 and 3.0 seconds. Accuracy was also recorded, with participants required to achieve at least 95% correct responses to be included in the final analysis.

## 1 Which sleep condition group had the highest mean reaction time?
Correct: D
The table shows the mean reaction time increases as sleep hours decrease. The 2‑hour group has the highest mean (380 ms).

## 2 Which variable is the independent variable in this study?
Correct: C
The researchers manipulated sleep duration to observe its effect on reaction time; thus sleep duration is the independent variable.

## 3 What is the difference in mean reaction time between the 8‑hour and 2‑hour sleep groups?
Correct: A
Mean for 8 hours = 250 ms; mean for 2 hours = 380 ms. Difference = 380 – 250 = 130 ms.

## 4 Which of the following statements is FALSE regarding the study’s findings?
Correct: C
The 6‑hour group’s mean reaction time (270 ms) is higher than the 8‑hour group (250 ms), not lower.

Correct: B
The study examines how reduced sleep affects simple reaction time, a key measure of cognitive performance.
