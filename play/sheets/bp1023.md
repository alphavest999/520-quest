# BP1023

Researchers studied the effect of varying concentrations of a novel antimicrobial peptide (AMP) on the growth of Escherichia coli in a controlled in vitro assay. The experiment used a 96‑well plate with eight concentrations (0, 1, 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 µg/mL) and three replicates per concentration. After 24 hours of incubation at 37 °C, optical density at 600 nm (OD600) was measured. The data, summarized in Table 1, show a dose‑dependent decrease in bacterial growth, with the lowest OD observed at the highest peptide concentration. Additionally, the researchers performed a time‑kill assay at 32 µg/mL, recording CFU counts at 0, 2, 4, and 6 hours. The results indicated a 3‑log reduction after 4 hours. The study also examined the peptide’s cytotoxicity against mammalian cell lines, reporting an IC50 of 48 µg/mL. These findings suggest that the peptide has potent antibacterial activity while maintaining a therapeutic window.

Table 1. Mean OD600 values for E. coli exposed to AMP.
| Concentration (µg/mL) | OD600 (mean ± SD) |
| 0 | 1.02 ± 0.04 |
| 1 | 0.88 ± 0.05 |
| 2 | 0.73 ± 0.06 |
| 4 | 0.58 ± 0.05 |
| 8 | 0.42 ± 0.04 |
| 16 | 0.28 ± 0.03 |
| 32 | 0.15 ± 0.02 |
| 64 | 0.08 ± 0.01 |

## bp1023_q1 Which AMP concentration results in the greatest reduction in OD600 relative to the control (0 µg/mL)?
Correct: D
The OD600 at 64 µg/mL (0.08) is the lowest among all concentrations, indicating the greatest reduction compared to the control (1.02).

## bp1023_q2 Which experimental design element best supports a dose‑response relationship in this study?
Correct: A
A 96‑well plate with multiple concentrations and replicates provides the necessary data points to establish a dose‑response curve.

## bp1023_q3 What is the percent reduction in OD600 at 32 µg/mL compared to the control?
Correct: A
Percent reduction = [(1.02 – 0.15)/1.02] × 100 ≈ 85.3 %.

## bp1023_q4 Which statement is NOT supported by the data presented?
Correct: C
The IC50 (48 µg/mL) is higher than the MIC (~32 µg/mL), so the peptide is less cytotoxic than its antibacterial potency; the statement incorrectly suggests the opposite.

## bp1023_q5 Which conclusion best links the peptide’s antibacterial potency to its therapeutic potential?
Correct: B
Rapid bactericidal activity at a concentration close to the IC50 implies a therapeutic window where the peptide kills bacteria without significant mammalian toxicity.
