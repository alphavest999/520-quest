# BP1462

Researchers investigated the effect of light intensity on the rate of photosynthesis in spinach leaves. A controlled experiment was set up in a growth chamber where the only variable altered was the photon flux density (µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹). Spinach leaf discs were exposed to five different light intensities: 100, 200, 300, 400, and 500 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹. For each intensity, the rate of oxygen evolution was measured over a 10‑minute period and expressed as µmol O₂ m⁻² s⁻¹. The results, summarized in Table 1, show a rapid increase in photosynthetic rate at low light, followed by a plateau at higher intensities, suggesting a saturation point. The researchers concluded that optimal light for maximal photosynthetic efficiency lies between 300 and 400 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹ for this species. This data can inform greenhouse lighting strategies and contribute to understanding the light‑response curve of C₃ plants.

Light Intensity (µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹) | O₂ evolution rate (µmol O₂ m⁻² s⁻¹)
100 | 12
200 | 24
300 | 35
400 | 42
500 | 45

## 1 Which light intensity produced the highest rate of oxygen evolution in the experiment?
Correct: C
The table shows the O₂ evolution rate peaks at 42 µmol O₂ m⁻² s⁻¹ when the light intensity is 400 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹, which is higher than the rates at 100, 200, 300, and 500 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹.

## 2 Which variable should be controlled to ensure that changes in oxygen evolution are attributable solely to light intensity?
Correct: D
To attribute changes in photosynthetic rate to light intensity alone, the experiment must keep temperature, CO₂ concentration, and leaf disc size constant, as each can influence photosynthesis.

## 3 What is the percent increase in oxygen evolution rate when light intensity increases from 200 to 400 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹?
Correct: C
Rate at 200 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹ = 24 µmol O₂ m⁻² s⁻¹; at 400 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹ = 42 µmol O₂ m⁻² s⁻¹. Percent increase = [(42‑24)/24]×100 = 75/24×100 ≈ 87.5%.

## 4 Which of the following is NOT a potential source of error in this experiment?
Correct: D
Using the same leaf species is intentional to reduce biological variability. The other options represent true sources of experimental error that could bias the results.

## 5 How could the findings of this study be applied to optimize lighting in a commercial greenhouse?
Correct: B
The study shows that photosynthetic rates plateau between 300 and 400 µmol photons m⁻² s⁻¹; thus, greenhouse lighting should be set within this range during periods when plants are actively growing to balance energy use and yield.
