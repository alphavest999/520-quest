# BP1082

Researchers conducted a randomized controlled trial to evaluate the impact of a novel 8‑week aerobic exercise program on cardiorespiratory fitness in sedentary adults aged 30–50. A total of 120 participants were randomly assigned to either the intervention group (n = 60) or the control group (n = 60). VO₂ max was measured at baseline and after 8 weeks using a graded treadmill test. The intervention group performed supervised moderate‑intensity cycling sessions three times per week, while the control group maintained their usual sedentary lifestyle. The study aimed to determine whether structured aerobic exercise could significantly improve VO₂ max compared to no intervention.

## 1 Which group showed the greatest absolute increase in VO₂ max?
Correct: A
The intervention group’s VO₂ max increased from 32.4 to 38.6 (Δ = 6.2), whereas the control group’s change was only 0.2. Thus, the intervention group had the greatest absolute increase.

## 2 Which of the following best describes the control condition used in this study?
Correct: B
The control group was instructed to maintain their usual sedentary lifestyle, with no structured exercise intervention.

## 3 What was the percent change in VO₂ max for the experimental group?
Correct: B
Percent change = [(38.6 – 32.4) / 32.4] × 100 = 6.2 / 32.4 × 100 ≈ 19.1%.

## 4 Which of the following statements is NOT supported by the data?
Correct: C
Baseline VO₂ max values differ slightly (32.4 vs. 32.1), so the statement that they were identical is incorrect.

## 5 The findings of this study are most relevant to which of the following clinical guidelines?
Correct: A
The study directly addresses aerobic exercise’s effect on cardiorespiratory fitness, aligning with the AHA guideline that recommends structured physical activity for adults.
