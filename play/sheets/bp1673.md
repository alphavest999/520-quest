# BP1673

Researchers studied the effect of a novel antihypertensive compound, Hypertensol, on systolic blood pressure (SBP) in adults with stage‑1 hypertension. A randomized, double‑blind, placebo‑controlled trial enrolled 120 participants who were assigned to either a Hypertensol group (n = 60) or a placebo group (n = 60). Baseline SBP was measured after a 5‑minute seated rest. Participants received either 10 mg Hypertensol or placebo once daily for 12 weeks. SBP was recorded at 4‑week intervals. The study aimed to evaluate the mean change in SBP from baseline to week 12 and to assess the safety profile of Hypertensol.
