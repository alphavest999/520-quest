How to use 520 Quest

Five minutes here and you will know everything the app does. The short version: scroll, fix your misses, beat the bosses, and let the Map tell you what to study next.

1. Install it on your phone

Tap the yellow APP button at the top of the app, or:

  • iPhone: open 520quest.com/play in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.

It opens full screen from your home screen and works offline.

2. Plans and signing in

Free includes 100 questions, 5 Mastery Sheets and 1 mnemonic. Monthly ($14.99), 6 Months ($44.99) and Yearly ($59.99) each unlock everything. Plans open soon on the Plans page.

  1. Subscribe with your email.
  2. Open 520 Quest, tap the Me tab, then Sign in with email. Enter the 6-digit code we email you.

Your plan works on up to 3 devices. On iPhone, the home-screen app counts as its own device, so sign in inside it.

3. Scroll: the main feed

Swipe up through questions like a social feed. Tap an answer to see right away why it's right or wrong.

  • Use the chips at the top (All, C/P, CARS, B/B, P/S) to pick a section.
  • Hint removes two wrong answers. You earn hint tokens from chests.
  • Mixed in are CARS passages, quick high-yield facts and Trap Alert cards that show common wrong-answer tricks.
  • The ring at the top tracks today's goal. Pick 15, 25, 40 or 60 questions in the Me tab or on the welcome screen.
  • Under every wrong-answer explanation, Related: Mastery Sheet opens the review sheet for that topic.

4. Your daily session

  • Welcome back: the first time you open the app each day, you see your level and today's goal. Tap Continue to pick up on the exact question you left, or adjust your goal or start a boss battle.
  • Results: when you hit your goal you get a results screen with accuracy, level progress, Revenge Queue and pace. Tap Review mistakes to redo today's misses.
  • Done for the Day: takes you to the Me tab with today's summary. Open the app again the same day and it goes straight to Me. Want more? Do 10 more for a +50 XP bonus.

5. Revenge queue

Every question you miss comes back for a rematch, tagged Revenge:

  1. Miss it: it returns the next day.
  2. Get it right: it returns in 3 days, then 7 days.
  3. Three right in a row: it's mastered and leaves the queue. Miss it again and it starts over.

Your misses are where your points are. This spacing is what makes the fix stick for test day.

6. Error DNA

After a miss, tag why it happened:

  • Content: you didn't know it.
  • Trap: you knew it but got tricked.
  • Time: you ran out of time (slow answers get tagged for you).

The Me tab adds these up and tells you your biggest leak and what to do about it.

7. Boss battles

Timed tests at real exam pace: 10 questions, 95 seconds each. Deal 70 damage before you lose 4 hearts. Fast right answers hit harder.

Four bosses: The Krebs Kraken (bio/biochem), Captain Torque (chem/physics), The Bias Hydra (psych/soc) and Overlord Omni (everything). Free players get one battle a day; every plan is unlimited. Misses go into your Revenge queue.

8. Mastery levels

Five levels: Rookie (0 to 40% accuracy), Contender (41 to 60%), Challenger (61 to 75%), Elite (76 to 85%) and Grand Master (86 to 100%).

  • Every 200 questions your accuracy is checked, with harder questions counting a little more.
  • Reach the next band and a Gatekeeper boss unlocks. Beat it to level up, with a fanfare you can mute in the Me tab. Levels never go down.

9. Course: Mastery Sheets and Mnemonics Vault

  • Tap the gold Course tab. Mastery Sheets are one-page reviews for each content category: high-yield summary, formulas and pathways, common traps and 3 practice questions. 5 are free; every plan unlocks all 33.
  • The Mnemonics Vault has 150 funny mnemonics in four categories. Search, upvote 😂, share one as an image, or tap through to its sheet and practice questions. 1 is free; every plan unlocks all 150.

10. Skill map

All 31 official MCAT content categories plus CARS and research skills, colored by how you're doing. ★ marks high-yield topics. Tap any category to drill only that topic in Scroll. Start with red rows that have a star.

11. Me tab

  • Your level, streak, badges, Revenge queue count, Error DNA summary, and analytics: accuracy by section and time per question over two weeks.
  • With a plan: section score estimate, full-length score tracker with a 520 goal line, and one-tap export of your misses to Anki or Quizlet.
  • Backup or move device: progress is saved on your device only. Use this to copy it to a new phone or keep a backup.

12. XP, streaks and chests

  • Right answers earn XP. Answer streaks build a combo (up to 3x XP), with random crits and jackpots.
  • Every 8 right answers opens a chest: XP, a hint token, or a streak freeze that saves your streak if you miss a day.
  • XP also earns fun titles from Pre-Med Rookie to 528 Legend, separate from your mastery level.

13. A simple daily plan

  1. Clear anything due in your Revenge queue (it shows up first in Scroll).
  2. Hit your daily goal in Scroll, tagging every miss.
  3. Run one boss battle to practice exam pace.
  4. Once a week, check the Map and Me tab and drill your weakest starred topics.
