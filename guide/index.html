How to use 520 Quest

Five minutes here and you will know everything the app does. The short version: scroll, fix your misses, beat the bosses, and let the Map tell you what to study next.

1. Install it on your phone

Tap the yellow APP button at the top of the app, or:

  • iPhone: open 520quest.com/play in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
  • Android: open it in Chrome, tap ⋮, then Install app.

It opens full screen from your home screen and works offline.

2. Unlock Pro

  1. Open 520 Quest and tap the Me tab (or the pink PRO button).
  2. Tap Get Pro, paste the code from your receipt email into the box, and tap Unlock.

You only do this once per device. Using a second phone, a computer, or a private browser window? Enter the code there too. On iPhone, the home-screen app counts as its own device, so enter the code inside it.

3. Scroll: the main feed

Swipe up through questions like a social feed. Tap an answer to see right away why it's right or wrong.

  • Use the chips at the top (All, C/P, CARS, B/B, P/S) to pick a section.
  • Hint removes two wrong answers. You earn hint tokens from chests.
  • Mixed in are CARS passages, quick high-yield facts and Trap Alert cards that show common wrong-answer tricks.
  • The ring at the top tracks today's goal (25 questions to start). Free players get 40 questions a day; Pro is unlimited.

4. Revenge queue

Every question you miss comes back for a rematch, tagged Revenge:

  1. Miss it: it returns the next day.
  2. Get it right: it returns in 3 days, then 7 days.
  3. Three right in a row: it's mastered and leaves the queue. Miss it again and it starts over.

Your misses are where your points are. This spacing is what makes the fix stick for test day.

5. Error DNA

After a miss, tag why it happened:

  • Content: you didn't know it.
  • Trap: you knew it but got tricked.
  • Time: you ran out of time (slow answers get tagged for you).

The Me tab adds these up and tells you your biggest leak and what to do about it.

6. Boss battles

Timed tests at real exam pace: 10 questions, 95 seconds each. Deal 70 damage before you lose 4 hearts. Fast right answers hit harder.

Four bosses: The Krebs Kraken (bio/biochem), Captain Torque (chem/physics), The Bias Hydra (psych/soc) and Overlord Omni (everything). Free players get one battle a day; Pro is unlimited. Misses go into your Revenge queue.

7. Skill map

All 31 official MCAT content categories plus CARS and research skills, colored by how you're doing. ★ marks high-yield topics. Tap any category to drill only that topic in Scroll. Start with red rows that have a star.

8. Me tab

  • Your level, streak, badges, Revenge queue count and Error DNA summary.
  • Pro: section score estimate, full-length score tracker with a 520 goal line, and one-tap export of your misses to Anki or Quizlet.
  • Backup or move device: progress is saved on your device only. Use this to copy it to a new phone or keep a backup.

9. XP, streaks and chests

  • Right answers earn XP. Answer streaks build a combo (up to 3x XP), with random crits and jackpots.
  • Every 8 right answers opens a chest: XP, a hint token, or a streak freeze that saves your streak if you miss a day.
  • Level up through ranks from Pre-Med Rookie to 528 Legend.

10. A simple daily plan

  1. Clear anything due in your Revenge queue (it shows up first in Scroll).
  2. Hit your daily goal in Scroll, tagging every miss.
  3. Run one boss battle to practice exam pace.
  4. Once a week, check the Map and Me tab and drill your weakest starred topics.
