Questions

Before you start

Is this official AAMC material?

No. Every question in 520 Quest is original. Use official AAMC practice exams for your full-lengths; 520 Quest is for daily practice between them.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and play. Your progress is saved on your device, and a backup code in the Me tab moves it to a new phone or laptop.

Will it guarantee a 520?

No app can promise a score. 520 Quest is built to make daily practice consistent and to show you exactly where your points are going.

Do you sell my data?

No. We help you get into medical school without selling your data. 520 Quest has no ads and no trackers, and your progress stays on your device.

How do I install it?

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app. After that it works offline.

How does Pro work?

You buy once through Gumroad and get an unlock code by email. Paste it in the Me tab and Pro turns on. No subscription, nothing renews.
