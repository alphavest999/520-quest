Inside the app

Everything a 520 scorer does,
in one app.

Qbank

Every answer choice, explained.

Tap how sure you are, answer, and see why each of the four choices is right or wrong. Then tag your miss so the app knows what to fix.

  • Error DNA. Tag each miss as content, misread passage, trap or time, and see which one costs you the most points.
  • Revenge Queue. Missed questions come back on a 1, 3 and 7 day schedule until you beat them.
  • Linked to the source. Every explanation points to the Mastery Sheet that teaches it.
Your plan

A plan that knows your weak spots.

Tell 520 Quest when you test and how many hours you have. The timed diagnostic and every answer after it keep your plan pointed at the topics that will move your score.

  • Skill map of all 31 AAMC content categories plus CARS and research skills, with your accuracy in each.
  • Adaptive practice serves more questions from your weakest areas automatically.
  • Analytics by section, pace per question and reasoning skill, in plain English.
The Course

Review that sticks, and makes you laugh.

The Course tab holds the content review that usually costs extra.

  • 33 Mastery Sheets: one for every content category plus CARS and research skills, with summaries, formulas, pathway diagrams, traps and practice questions.
  • Memorization checklist of the facts you must know cold. Tick them off as you master them.
  • Mnemonics Vault: 150 mnemonics, each with a clean version and a funny one you won't forget.
Exams and bosses

Practice under real exam pressure.

When it's time to test yourself, the clock is real.

  • 10 full-length and 10 mini exams with countdown, pause, flag, end-of-section review and a scaled score estimate.
  • Boss battles: 10 timed questions at about 95 seconds each. Beat the Gatekeeper to climb from Rookie to Grand Master.
  • Daily goal you choose. Hit it, review your mistakes and you're done for the day. No burnout by week three.

Questions

Before you start

Is this official AAMC material?

No. Every question is original and written to match the MCAT's style as closely as possible. Our content was checked against the AAMC's published content outline. MCAT is a registered trademark of the AAMC, which does not endorse this product.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and play; your progress saves on your device. Sign in with your email in the Me tab when you want your progress on your other devices.

How does the diagnostic work?

On the welcome screen, tap Create customized Action plan, set your test date, and take a 30-question timed diagnostic across the sections. Your results set the weak areas in Your plan, and your plan keeps updating as you answer more questions.

Will it guarantee a 520?

No app can promise a score. 520 Quest is built to make daily practice consistent and to show you exactly where your points are going.

Do you sell my data?

No. We help you get into medical school without selling your data. 520 Quest has no ads and no trackers.

How do I install it?

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app. After that it works offline.

How do plans work?

Choose Monthly ($14.99), 6 Months ($44.99) or Yearly ($59.99). Every plan unlocks everything. Sign in with the same email in the app's Me tab and your plan works on up to 3 devices. Plans renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime. They open soon.
