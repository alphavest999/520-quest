Questions

Before you start

Is this official AAMC material?

No. Every question is original and designed to simulate MCAT questions as closely as possible. MCAT is a registered trademark of the AAMC, which does not officially endorse this product.

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and play; your progress is saved on your device. Sign in with your email in the Me tab when you want your progress on your other devices, or to play 1v1 duels.

Will it guarantee a 520?

No app can promise a score. 520 Quest is built to make daily practice consistent and to show you exactly where your points are going.

Do you sell my data?

No. We help you get into medical school without selling your data. 520 Quest has no ads and no trackers, and your progress stays on your device.

How do I install it?

On iPhone, open the app in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. On Android, open it in Chrome and tap Install app. After that it works offline.

How do plans work?

Choose Monthly ($14.99), 6 Months ($44.99) or Yearly ($59.99). Every plan unlocks everything. Sign in with the same email in the app's Me tab and your plan works on up to 3 devices. Plans renew automatically and can be cancelled anytime. They open soon.
